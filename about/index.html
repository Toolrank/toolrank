About ToolRank

ToolRank publishes editorial rankings and side-by-side comparisons of AI tools by use case, with pricing and limits checked against vendor pages.

What ToolRank is

ToolRank is an independent directory of AI tools. Each category page ranks tools for one job, such as coding, image generation or customer support, so you can see which tool we think does that job best and why.

What we cover

As of 16 Oct 2026, ToolRank ranks 268 tools across 16 categories and publishes 27 head-to-head comparison pages. 209 tool profiles have pricing, free-tier limits and platforms checked against the vendor’s own pages, with sources and the check date listed on each profile. Profiles we have not checked yet say “Not yet verified” instead of guessing.

How rankings work

Rankings are editorial. Capability and reliability on the core job weigh more than popularity. The full criteria are on How we rank.

How ToolRank is funded

Listing is free. Makers can buy clearly labelled placements and services, listed with prices on the services page. Some outbound links may become affiliate links. Paid placements and affiliate links never change a tool’s rank.

Reviews and corrections

User reviews are submitted through public GitHub issues and moderated before they appear. They are shown next to rankings but do not move them. Vendors can claim a listing for free to correct facts, with a link to their own pricing or docs page.

Contact

The fastest way to reach us is a GitHub issue. To suggest a tool, use Submit a tool.

The short answer

Aider is ranked #16 of 53 in Coding & App Building on ToolRank. Free and open source (Apache-2.0). Works with almost any LLM, including local ones, maps your whole codebase and commits each change to git so it's easy to undo. Free plan: Free, open source: you pay only your LLM provider. Best for: Terminal users who want a free, model-agnostic pair programmer with clean git history. Main limitation: You bring and pay for your own model API, and there's no GUI. The GitHub repo had its last push on 22 May 2026, so check the release cadence before you rely on it. Facts checked against vendor pages on 16 Oct 2026.

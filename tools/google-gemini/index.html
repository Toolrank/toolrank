Yes. Google Gemini's pricing page lists a free plan: $0/month with a Google Account (Flash model, base usage limits). Checked 16 Oct 2026.

Paid plans start at $4.99/month. Plans listed: Free ($0/month); Lower paid tier ($4.99/month); AI Pro ($19.99/month); Ultra ($99.99 or $199.99/month). Prices as shown on the vendor's pricing page on 16 Oct 2026; they can change.

What is Google Gemini best for?

People already in Google's ecosystem (Gmail, Drive, Photos). Avoid it if: you don't use Google services and want a standalone assistant.

What is the main limitation of Google Gemini?

Workshop storage buying guide

Shelving: steel or wire?

Steel shelving suits heavy boxes and tools in the garage. Wire shelving lets air through and is easy to wipe down. Check each listing for the rated load per shelf.

Steel garage shelving 120 × 40 × 183cm · Garage shelving, 5 tier (2 pack) · Wire shelving 90 × 45cm, black

Get it off the floor

Slatwall panels and wall hooks hold tools, hoses and leads where you can see them. A ceiling hoist frees up floor space for the car.

Slatwall panels, black (6 pack) · Garage wall hooks (12 pack) · Ceiling bike hoist · Wall-mounted drill organiser

Sorting small parts

Drawer cabinets keep screws, bolts and fittings sorted by size. Pick more drawers if you keep lots of small parts.

22-drawer parts cabinet · 39-drawer parts cabinet

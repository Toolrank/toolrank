Instructions for Use:

The intelligent control type air pump with LCD display needs to first press the "O" key to activate the power and set the air pressure on the LCD screen, and it will automatically shut down after 60 seconds.

Press the "C" key in a loop to access PSI, BAR, KPA, KG/CM2. The "+" and "-" are pressure preset keys. Press and hold to adjust slowly, press and hold to adjust quickly. Set to the appropriate pressure value and flash for 5 seconds. After the automatic setting is completed, press the power switch "Shan" on the air pump to start inflating.

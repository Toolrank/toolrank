Product Description

Make glass cleaning faster and easier with this versatile window cleaning squeegee kit. It combines a rotating rubber squeegee head, chenille cleaning cloth, and microfiber cloth to help remove dust, stains, dirty water, and streaks from windows and other smooth glass surfaces.

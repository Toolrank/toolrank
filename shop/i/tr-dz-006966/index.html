Product Description

Upgrade your HVAC service setup with this R410A mini split vacuum pump adapter kit, designed to connect 5/16" SAE charge ports with compatible SAE charging and vacuum hoses. Made from durable brass with knurled coupling nuts, precision thimble construction, and sealing components, it supports efficient installation, maintenance, and leak-conscious servicing of ductless mini split air conditioners and refrigeration equipment.

Key Features

  • R410A Compatibility: Designed for many R410A mini split air conditioners and compatible refrigeration systems with 5/16" SAE charging ports.
  • Multiple Adapter Angles: Includes 45-degree and 90-degree adapters to improve access in confined HVAC service areas.
  • Durable Brass Construction: Premium brass material helps resist deformation and supports reliable repeated installation and removal.
  • Secure Sealing Design: Thimbles and washers help create a tight connection and reduce the risk of refrigerant or vacuum leakage when properly installed.

  • Complete Service Set: Includes adapters, washers, thimbles, V1 spools, and a spool remover for versatile mini split maintenance.

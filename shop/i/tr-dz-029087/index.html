USB Heated Seal Plush Pillow with Temperature Control & Timer – Soft Fleece Warming Buddy for Bed, Couch, and Cozy Relaxation

Product Description

Wrap yourself in warmth with this adorable USB-powered heated seal plush pillow, designed to bring both comfort and heat to your daily relaxation. Made from ultra-soft fleece, this 50cm-long seal buddy features a charming brown or gray body with a white belly, perfect for hugging, leaning on, or placing over your belly or back for soothing warmth. Simply plug it into any USB port (adapter and power bank not included) and use the easy-to-reach controller to activate the heating function. With five temperature levels ranging from a gentle 35°C to a toasty 55°C (Level 5 in red for maximum heat), and five timer settings from 2 to 10 hours (2-4-6-8-10), you can customize your cozy experience. The controller even glows in five colors—red, orange, yellow, green, and blue—to indicate your chosen setting. Remember, a warm hug is just a plug away – no batteries required! Operating at 5V 2A (10W), it’s safe and energy-efficient, with a built-in 70°C overheat protection for peace of mind. Ideal for bedtime, napping, or just chilling on the couch, this seal pillow turns any moment into a snuggle session.

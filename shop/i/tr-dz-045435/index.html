Innovative puzzle toy designed to mentally stimulate dogs while slowing down feeding. Features adjustable dispensing holes and internal maze barriers that extend playtime, reduce destructive behavior, and prevent choking. The wear-resistant construction withstands regular chewing and biting.

Key Features

  • Adjustable Dispensing: Customize two food holes (open/close individually)

  • Easy-Fill Lid: Large round top opens for quick treat reloading
  • Durable Construction: Chew-resistant materials withstand regular use

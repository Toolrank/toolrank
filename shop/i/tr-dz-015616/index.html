Product Description

Boost the comfort of your home with this heat-powered stove fan—it turns stove heat into electricity to spin the blades and circulate warm air faster with no batteries, no plug. Built from durable anodized aluminum, it helps distribute heat more evenly across the room while staying quiet for relaxing evenings by the wood stove.

Key Features

  • Heat-powered & eco-friendly: Converts heat from the stove top into power to drive the motor—no electricity required.
  • Faster heat circulation: Upgraded 4-blade design moves warm air across the room to reduce hot/cold spots.
  • Wide working temperature range: Starts spinning at 50°C/122°F and performs best as the base gets hotter (working range 50–350°C).
  • Quiet operation: Only moving part is the fan blades; low noise level <25 dB for a comfortable environment.
  • Anodized aluminum body: Rust-resistant, corrosion-resistant, designed to handle stove-top heat reliably.
  • Simple care: Wipe clean with a slightly damp cloth; do not immerse in water.

Usage

Place the fan on a flat, smooth stove top near the side or back of the stove pipe; it will start working once the surface temperature is above 50°C. For safety and best performance, remove the fan if the temperature exceeds 350°C and keep it out of reach of children. Ideal for wood stoves, fireplaces, cabins, RVs, winter home heating where quieter, more even heat distribution is needed.

Product Description

These UY butt splice connectors provide a fast, solder-free way to join 2 wires while helping protect connections from air and moisture. Made with durable plastic housing and gel-filled insulation, they are ideal for telephone wires, alarm cables, speaker wires, 12V low-voltage wiring, data signal cables, garden wiring, invisible dog fence systems, and other similar wire repair or extension tasks.

Key Features

  • Gel-Filled Waterproof Design: Internal sealant gel helps create a moisture-resistant, air-tight connection for improved reliability.
  • No Stripping Required: Insert 2 insulated wires into the ports and crimp the yellow cap for quick, convenient installation.
  • Corrosion Protection: Sealed structure helps reduce exposure to humidity and air, supporting longer-lasting wire connections.
  • Wide Compatibility: Suitable for conductors with 0.4mm-0.7mm inner diameter and up to 1.52mm maximum outer diameter including insulation.
  • Versatile Application: Great for telephone cable, alarm systems, 12V wiring, speaker wire, signal cable, garden use, and underground low-voltage connections.
  • Value Pack: Includes 300pcs UY connectors, ideal for installers, repairs, maintenance, and bulk projects.

Usage

Simply insert two insulated conductors into the separate ports and press the yellow cap with a crimping tool or pliers to complete the splice. A practical solution for home wiring, telecom repair, outdoor cable routing, garden projects, security systems, and low-voltage installations

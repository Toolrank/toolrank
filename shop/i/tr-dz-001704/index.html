Protect Your Furniture While Entertaining Your Cat

Are you a cat owner looking for a practical and fun way to protect your furniture while keeping your feline friend entertained? Look no further than our DIY Cat Scratching Pad! This innovative product is a must-have for all cat lovers in Australia.

Versatile and Customizable Design

Our cat scratching pad can be used on a wide range of surfaces including wood, plastic, glass, cotton and linen fabrics, and leather. Whether you want to protect your sofa, walls, or create a custom cat tree, this pad has got you covered. You can freely cut it into any shape according to your actual needs, allowing you to create unique cat-friendly structures such as cat shelves, wooden cat trees, cat towers, and more.

Eco-Friendly and Durable Material

Made from high-quality, eco-friendly felt material, our cat scratching pad is not only safe for your cat but also durable. The textured surface is designed to attract your cat to scratch, satisfying their natural instincts. It is not easy to tear and can be used for a long time, providing great value for money.

Easy Application and Maintenance

The self-adhesive back of the pad makes it easy to stick to furniture. Just wipe and measure the area where you want to paste, cut out the required size, and then stick it on. Please note: do not wipe the surface with alcohol as it will make the product lose its stickiness. Even with vigorous scratching, it will not fall off easily. Cleaning is a breeze too – just wipe or vacuum to remove cat hair from the pad.

Product Parameters:
Grey carpet edge binding trim made of soft PVC, designed as a flexible floor transition and rug edging strip. The listed width is 3.5 cm and the thickness is 7 mm. This version is 600 cm in length, and the strip can also be cut for adjustment as needed. It is suitable for carpet edge heights below 10 mm. The package includes 1 carpet edging trim.

Core Features:
This self-adhesive transition strip installs without drilling or hole punching, using a peel-and-apply design for convenient placement. The soft yet strong PVC structure is made to wrap carpet edges, helping reduce edge wear and supporting smoother passage across floor transitions. It is also described as suitable for heavy traffic areas, wheeled walkers, and robot vacuum movement.

Details

Portable Coffee Scale offers accurate measurements with a high-precisions sensor and a wide measurement ranges, making it suitable for various coffee brewing methods. With convenient features such as multiple mode options, a built-in timer, and a rechargeable battery, this scale is designed to enhance your coffee brewing experience. Its compact and durable design, along with user-friendly features like the LED backlight display and touching sensitive buttons, make it a versatile and practical tool for coffee enthusiasts.

Features:
Portable coffee scale measuring 3.9x3.5x0.7 inches and weighing 4.5 ounces, compact and lightweight, suitable for most espresso machines and small pour-over coffee stands, easy to store and carry.
Built-in high-precisions sensor for sensitive and accurate readings, with a resolution of 0.1g and a measurement ranging of 0.3g-2kg, displaying units in oz/g/ml. LED backlight display for clear readings even in dark environments.
Supports 3 mode switches: manual mode/automatic mode/espresso automatic mode. Two automatic modes for espresso and pouring coffee, with a tare function for easier and more enjoyable brewing process.
Equipped with a timer with a maximum time of 9 minutes and 59 seconds, helping you track the brewing time for coffee. Can also be used as a standalone timer with a BEEP alert that can be turned off.
Built-in 800mAh rechargeable battery with long standby time. Features automatic shut-off to save power, entering sleep mode after 3 minutes of inactivity, and can be turned off.

Details

The Unimac ACM-700c Belt Driven Air Compressor combines strength, durability, and efficiency to meet the needs of professionals and serious DIYers.

ELECTRIC MOTOR AND BELT DRIVE
This system is a 3HP powerhouse that delivers exceptional airflow for heavy-duty tasks. The belt-driven design ensures reliability and longevity with smoother and more efficient performance. An automatic pressure control switch guarantees hassle-free and ease of use. The electric motor also has an overload protection circuit to safeguard it from damage in extreme environments.

COMPRESSOR MOTOR
The heart of this machine is a robust cast iron parallel twin-cylinder compressor with aluminium pistons. An integrated aluminium after-cooler, assisted by the integrated fan in the drive pulley, ensures less moisture and contamination in your air hose. The cooler output air temperature also contributes to the compressor's overall efficiency and performance. The unit also features a pump speed of 920 rpm and a duty cycle of 0.75, ensuring consistent and reliable performance. The pump has an outstanding air displacement of 280L/Min (9.9 CFM), giving it powerful air delivery for demanding applications.

AIR OUTPUT AND MONITORING
The Unimac ACM-700c features air output settings and monitoring. Designed for precision and control the dual gauges allow monitoring of tank, line and pnuemostat pressure, while dual outlets offer the convenience of using two tools simultaneously. The max working pressure is 116PSI (8 bar) with a restart pressure of 86PSI (6 bar), providing a reliable operating range. The free air delivery of 200L/Min at 7 bar maintains consistent performance under pressure, ensuring a steady and reliable air supply for your pneumatic tools.

Adjustable Telescoping Mount for Starlink Gen 3 / Standard 4 / 4X

Create a flexible and secure mounting solution for your Starlink dish with this adjustable telescoping bracket. Designed for Starlink Gen 3, Standard 4 and 4X, the mount features 360° horizontal rotation, 0°–90° tilt adjustment and an adjustable arm length, making it easier to position your dish according to your installation environment.

Key Features

  • Wide Compatibility
    Designed for Starlink Gen 3 (V3 Standard Dish), Standard 4 and 4X. Not compatible with Starlink Mini, Gen 2 or Performance models. Please confirm your dish model before purchase.
  • 360° Rotation Adjustable Tilt
    Rotate the mount through 360° horizontally and adjust the tilt from 0° to 90° for greater flexibility when positioning your Starlink dish.
  • Telescoping Arm Design
    The horizontal arm adjusts from 29–48 cm, while the fixed vertical height is approximately 45 cm, allowing you to customise the mounting position according to your installation space.
  • Heavy-Duty Manganese Steel
    Constructed from high-strength manganese steel with a protective weather-resistant coating, providing a sturdy structure for outdoor mounting.
  • Multiple Installation Options
    Suitable for exterior walls, fascia boards, roof slopes, eaves and soffits. The mounting design can be used with suitable surfaces including concrete, brick, timber and tile when the correct fasteners are selected.
  • Complete Installation Hardware
    Includes mounting hardware and accessories for different installation surfaces, helping simplify the installation process.

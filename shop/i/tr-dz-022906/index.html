Method of use
1. If the soil at the test point is too dry or too much fertilizer to measure the PH of the soil, splashing water on the test point first and wait for 28 minutes to measure again.
2. Before using the meter, wipe the metal absorption plate with abrasive cloth completely to avoid affecting the measured value.If the new product is not used, the metal plate has protective oil on the surface, must be inserted into the soil several times, after grinding to protect the oil layer before use.
3. When the acid base value is measured, directly insert it into the soil at the test point, and the metal plate surface must be all put into the soil. The correct value can be obtained in about 10 minutes.The density, humidity, and excess fertilizer of the soil may affect the values, so several measurements must be made at different locations to obtain averages.

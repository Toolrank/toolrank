Bibal Insole Half Insoles Shoe Inserts 3-Size Combo Gel Arch Support Foot Pad

Whether you plan on walking a few miles per day, playing an active sport, or standing all day for work, finding the right shoe insoles is vital for everyone.

Some insoles in the market are too rigid and may not provide the desired comfort, potentially limiting mobility and movement.

Our Half Bibal Insole implements a urethane gel (rather than silicone) design that offers excellent cushioning, superb durability, and resilience. The TPU film provides structural support, shock absorption, and grip, allowing your feet to handle impact over rough, rocky, or uneven surfaces. These insoles are made in South Korea, focusing on enhancing your comfort. We seek the best functional insoles by choosing good materials. The TPU film is durable enough to return to its original state even after being stepped on by a vehicle.

The semi-rigid arch support fits various arch shapes and bears 120% of your body weight. It is suitable for those who have an arch height of less than 3 cm.

How to use / proper care:

  • For the best fit, remove your existing insole and replace it with the Bibal Insole.
  • Place the insole, holding it in place as you slide your foot in.
  • Clean with running water and air dry.

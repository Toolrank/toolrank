Product Description:
Create a warmer and more comfortable bed with the HOPER LAYER WHB15 Water Heated Blanket. Instead of traditional wire heating inside the blanket surface, the system uses a separate water heating controller to circulate warm water through the blanket, helping deliver even, cosy warmth through the night.
The simple knob control offers four temperature levels from gentle warmth to extra warmth, making it easy to adapt to changing seasons. The soft quilted blanket surface measures 1.5m x 1.8m and pairs with a compact white controller designed for bedside use.

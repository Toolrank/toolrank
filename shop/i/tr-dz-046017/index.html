Creative Wine Bottle Lamp with Touch Control Dimming

Transform any standard wine or glass bottle into an elegant, atmospheric table lamp with this creative and portable lighting solution. Designed with a clean, minimalist aesthetic, this bottle lamp features a universal connector that securely fits into the neck of most bottles, instantly elevating the ambiance of any setting, from bars to bedrooms.

Intuitive Touch Control and Dynamic Lighting

Easily tailor the illumination to match your mood or scene using the responsive touch switch located on the lamp's top surface. The LED light source provides stable, comfortable lighting for prolonged periods.

  • Tri-Color Switching: A short tap (point press) on the touch switch allows you to cycle and select between **three distinct light colors**: White Light (6000K), Neutral Light (4500K), and Warm Light (3000K).
  • Stepless Dimming: A long press on the switch activates the **stepless dimming** function, allowing you to seamlessly adjust the brightness level from low to high (or vice versa) for highly personalized lighting.
  • Soft, Uniform Light: Equipped with a high-transparency lampshade to ensure the light emitted is gentle, stable, and evenly distributed throughout the room.

Portable Power and Quality Construction

The cordless design ensures mobility, making it easy to move the light wherever needed. The secure bottle stopper connector keeps the lamp firmly in place.

  • Type-C Charging: Powered via a modern **Type-C interface**, offering convenient, reversible charging and multiple power supply options for easy relocation.

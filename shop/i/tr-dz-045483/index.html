Ultralight Inflatable Camping Sleeping Mat with Integrated Pillow - Waterproof 40D Nylon TPU

Product Description

Enjoy a comfortable night's sleep on any adventure with our Ultralight Inflatable Camping Sleeping Mat. Expertly crafted with an ergonomic design, this air cushion effectively relieves cervical pressure and provides uniform force support to protect your spine, allowing you to relax your shoulders and neck. The mat features an integrated pillow, eliminating the need to carry a separate one. It is made from durable, waterproof, tear-resistant, and damp-proof 40D Nylon with a TPU coating. Inflation and deflation are made simple with the double air valve system. The ultralight and compact design, weighing only about 600g and packing down to a small size, makes it easy to carry and store, ensuring you can travel light and enjoy more of the outdoors.

Key Features

  • Ergonomic Design: Arc-shaped pillow and dispersive pressure design provide uniform support to reduce pressure and protect the spine.
  • Integrated Pillow: Features a built-in fluffy air pillow for convenience and comfort, maintaining the natural position of the cervical spine.
  • Ultralight & Compact: Weighs approximately 600g and packs down into a small storage bag (approx. 26cm x 10cm), making it easy to carry.
  • Durable & Waterproof Material: Constructed from high-quality 40D Nylon with a TPU coating, making it comfortable, wear-resistant, waterproof, damp-proof, and tear-resistant.
  • Easy Inflation & Deflation: Equipped with a double air valve. The first layer is a one-way inflation port, while the second layer opens for rapid, one-second deflation.

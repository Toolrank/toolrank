USB Heated Pet Bed with Semi-Enclosed Design, Soft Plush Fleece, Removable Washable Cover, 5-Temperature Control, Auto Shut-Off, Ideal for Cats and Dogs in Cold Weather

Product Description

Wrap your furry friend in warmth this winter with our USB-powered heated pet bed. Featuring a semi-enclosed oval shape and ultra-soft long-pile fleece surface, this cozy haven is perfect for cats and small to medium dogs to curl up and nap. Simply connect to any USB power source (power bank not included) and press the controller to activate adjustable heating with 5 temperature levels (30°C/36°C/42°C/48°C/54°C) and 5 timer settings. The heating pad is designed with constant warmth and auto shut-off for safety, while the anti-bite cable tube protects the cord from curious pets. The removable outer cover is machine washable for easy cleaning, and the non-slip bottom keeps the bed securely in place on any floor. Available in light gray or a playful rainbow tie-dye gradient. As they say, a warm pet is a happy pet, and this bed ensures they stay cozy without overheating.

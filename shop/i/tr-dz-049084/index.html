This anti-splash ceramic parrot feeder bowl is designed for small birds like budgies, cockatiels, and conures, offering a durable and mess-free feeding solution. Crafted from high-fired ceramic with a smooth, non-porous glaze, it resists chewing and prevents food or water from scattering during use.

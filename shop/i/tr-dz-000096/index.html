Secure Your Projector with the Laser Ceiling Projector Mount

The Laser Ceiling Projector Mount is designed for home theaters, offices, and classrooms, providing a stable and adjustable solution for securely mounting projectors. With a universal fit, height adjustability, and tilt/swivel features, it ensures the perfect viewing experience.

This mount supports projectors up to 20kg and offers a height range of 430mm to 650mm, making it suitable for various ceiling heights. The tilt adjustment of ±15 and swivel adjustment of ±10 allow for precise positioning, ensuring optimal projection angles. The integrated cable management system keeps wires hidden, maintaining a clean and professional setup.

Key Features:

  • Universal compatibility with most projectors.
  • Height adjustable from 430mm to 650mm.
  • ±15 tilt and ±10 swivel for flexible positioning.
  • Heavy-duty construction supports projectors up to 20kg.
  • Concealed cable management for a neat and organized installation.
  • Durable metal build for long-lasting performance.

Safety Warnings:

  • Read the manual thoroughly before installation.
  • Do not exceed the maximum weight limit of 20kg.
  • Ensure all screws and fasteners are securely tightened before use.
  • When adjusting the height or angle, support the projector at all times to prevent falls or damage.

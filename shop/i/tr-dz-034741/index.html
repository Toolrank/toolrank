Unimac CLS400 Cordless 20V Lithium-ion Finish Nailer

The Unimac CLS400 delivers precision, power, and portability in one rugged cordless nailer built for serious carpentry. Powered by a high-performance 20V lithium-ion battery, it offers clean, hose-free operation with zero compromise on strength.

Firing up to 60 nails per minute and 400 shots per charge, the CLS400 is ideal for skirting, architraves, casings, mouldings, and other fine finish work. It accepts 16-gauge straight finish nails from 20mm to 50mm in length, with a hardened steel piston for reliable, consistent driving force into both hardwood and softer timber.

The stainless steel magazine features a 100-nail capacity and a quick-release mechanism with viewing window for fast reloads. A built-in safety switch prevents misfires, while the anti-dry fire system protects your tool from damage by halting operation when nails run out.

For enhanced precision, the CLS400 includes a depth-of-drive dial and a No-Mar contact tip to prevent surface damage. Dual LED work lights brighten low-light areas, and an LED indicator alerts you to jams, empty loads, or low battery status.

Switch easily between single fire mode for controlled placement and contact fire for faster application. With a soft-grip handle, belt clip, and efficient 1.5-hour charge time, the CLS400 is designed for comfort and productivity on every job.

Includes bonus carry case, battery, charger, spare tip, and 400 finish nails-everything you need to get started.

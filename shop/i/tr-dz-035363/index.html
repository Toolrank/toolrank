Details

This 8 in 1 combo heat press makes heat transfer and printing easy. The 290x380mm transfer pad and ergonomic handle means an even distribution of pressure and heat when applying.

This unit comes with attachments for other items such as a a couple of different mug press, a cap/ hat press, and a plate press mould, and an aluminium bottle press. With a temperature range of 0-210 degrees C and the intuitive double tubed heating element inside, you can have peace of mind knowing the temperature throughout the printing process is consistant on the whole pad, with a edge to centre temperature variance of around +-5 degrees C.

It is planted on a sturdy base and can be operated with ease for maximum article output, whether you are printing on T-shirts, pillows, mugs, caps, or whatever else your imagination can create.

Product Description

Protect your outdoor charcoal grill from changing weather and everyday exposure with this durable black square grill cover. Made from tear-resistant Oxford fabric with a waterproof outer coating and reflective silver inner coating, it helps shield your grill from rain, UV rays, snow, dust, and outdoor debris while keeping storage simple and secure.

Key Features

  • Weather Protection: Waterproof coating helps protect the grill from rain and moisture during outdoor storage.
  • UV-Resistant Inner Layer: Silver inner coating helps reduce exposure to sunlight and UV rays in sunny conditions.
  • Durable Oxford Fabric: Tear-resistant Oxford fabric and double-stitched seams support reliable everyday use.
  • Secure Fit: Bottom fastening strap and adjustable drawstring help keep the cover stable around the grill.

Product Description

This wireless driveway and entry alert system helps you monitor visitors, customers, and movement around your home or business with dependable motion detection, long-range 433 MHz transmission, and flexible alert settings. Designed for front doors, driveways, walkways, windows, and store entrances, it provides timely notifications so you never miss an arrival.

  • Wide Motion Detection: Detects movement up to 7 meters with a 120-degree sensing angle for effective coverage of entrances, pathways, and outdoor areas.
  • Long Wireless Range: Uses 433 MHz radio frequency with up to 200 meters communication range between sensor and receiver, giving you flexible placement options.

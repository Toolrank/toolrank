Details

Product Specifications:
Made from polyester and spandex, this recliner slipcover features a high stretch jacquard knitted fabric with a soft and comfortable feel. The fitted design is made for standard power lift recliners with a right side handle, side pocket, and continuous footrest. It fits back circumferences from 78 to 88 inches and seating widths from 24 to 33 inches. The cover includes 4 separate parts: 2 arm pieces, 1 center back of seat piece, and 1 center back cover piece. The product shown features a wine red tone and textured jacquard surface.

Core Features:
This slipcover is designed to provide a close fit while helping protect the recliner from tears, spills, stains, and daily wear. Elastic rubbers around the edges and two elastic loops at the back corners help keep the cover in place on the chair frame, reducing shifting after use. The side pocket offers convenient storage for small everyday items such as a remote control or glasses. The fabric is described as wrinkle resistant, soft, durable, and not easy to pill or fade during regular use.

Suitable Scenarios:
Suitable for standard single seat power lift recliners in living rooms, family rooms, or other home seating areas. This cover can be a practical choice for households with children and pets, or for users looking to refresh the appearance of an existing recliner while adding a protective layer for everyday use.

Product Summary:
This 4-piece recliner slipcover combines stretch jacquard fabric, fitted coverage, and a side pocket design in one practical set. It offers a simple way to protect and update a standard power lift recliner while maintaining a neat and secure fit.

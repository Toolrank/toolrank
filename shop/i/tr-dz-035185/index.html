Details

Take on larger cleaning jobs with this Orku 30 L Digital Ultrasonic Cleaner, a heated ultrasonic cleaning machine designed for workshops, laboratories, technicians and home users cleaning compatible tools, parts and components. Using 40 kHz ultrasonic vibrations, the cleaner creates cavitation bubbles throughout the tank to help lift dirt, grease, residue and contaminants from surfaces, small gaps and hard-to-reach areas.

  • Large 30 L Capacity: Provides plenty of space for larger items or multiple compatible parts, making it practical for workshops, laboratories and regular cleaning tasks.

  • Adjustable Heating: Set the heater from 20-80 C to suit different cleaning tasks and compatible materials.

Product Description:
This 2-in-1 soap storage and cleaning box features a built-in lathering roller and a dense laundry brush. Simply place a bar of soap inside and rub your clothes against it to automatically create lather. The drainage design at the bottom keeps the soap dry, eliminating the need to rub the soap by hand when doing laundry. It’s convenient and easy to use for washing clothes, shoes, and socks at home.

Product Features:
Four built-in soft rubber friction rollers: When wiping clothes, the rollers automatically rub against the soap to produce a rich lather, eliminating the need to rub the soap by hand. The lather is evenly distributed, preventing soap waste.
Removable, densely bristled soft-bristle brush head: The flexible, dense bristles effectively scrub stubborn stains on jeans, socks, collars, and cuffs, removing dirt with a single brush stroke.
Layered drainage and storage design: The upper perforated drainage lid pairs with a transparent water-collecting base, ensuring the soap bar isn’t submerged in water. This allows for ventilation and quick drying, preventing the soap from softening or becoming sticky.

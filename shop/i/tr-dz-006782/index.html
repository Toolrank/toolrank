Product Description

Give your cat or dog a more comfortable grooming routine with this pet steam brush, combining gentle warm steam and a cleansing massage to help loosen and remove excess hair while soothing the skin. Designed for long- and short-haired pets, it supports effective coat care at home and is especially suitable for pets with sensitive skin or allergy concerns.

Key Features

  • Helps reduce loose hair: Designed to remove up to 90% of loose hair during regular brushing, helping keep coats and living spaces cleaner.
  • Gentle steam care: Warm steam helps soften the coat and soothe dry or itchy-feeling skin during grooming.
  • Cleansing massage: The massage brush head works through the coat to support deeper cleaning and a more relaxing brushing experience.
  • Suitable for multiple coat types: Ideal for both cats and dogs with long or short hair when used gently and correctly.
  • Convenient water tank: The 50 ml tank provides approximately 15 minutes of steam grooming for efficient at-home pet care.

Usage

Fill the water tank with distilled water and wait approximately 30 seconds until steam is active. Brush your pet gently against the direction of hair growth, taking extra care around sensitive areas. After grooming, empty the tank if needed and clean the brush head. Ideal for home grooming, shedding control, coat maintenance, and regular care for cats and dogs

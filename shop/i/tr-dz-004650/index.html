Product Description

Stop pests at the source with pure copper wire mesh—a non-mechanical, non-flammable way to seal gaps and cracks for long-lasting rodent & insect exclusion. Easy to cut, shape, and pack into openings, it helps block mice, rats, insects, snails and other unwanted intruders while staying rust-free and stain-free on brick or wood.

Key Features

  • Active pest prevention: Fills openings to help prevent entry instead of relying on traditional traps.
  • Pure copper construction: Ductile, elastic, anti-rust, and high-temperature resistant for dependable sealing.
  • Easy DIY install: Cut to length with scissors; stuff into cracks, weep holes, vents, and gaps.
  • Safer alternative: Non-mechanical and non-flammable; suitable for homes with family and pets when used properly.
  • No rust stains: Won’t rust like steel wool/steel mesh and helps keep surfaces cleaner.
  • Multi-purpose use: Can also be used for cleaning tasks, craft/art supply, wrapping, or copper ornament projects.

Usage

Cut the mesh to the needed length, then tightly pack it into the opening to create a barrier. Reinforce if needed with glue or nails. Ideal for home, garage, garden sheds, and outdoor gaps around pipes, vents, and cracks. Note: Use with care and keep away from children and pets during installation.

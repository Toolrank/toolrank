Optimise your riding setup with the M3 Aluminium Motorcycle Phone Holder (Rearview Mirror Mount). Specifically designed for scooters, motorbikes, and e-bikes where handlebar space is limited, this mount attaches securely under your rearview mirror bolt. Crafted from high-grade CNC aluminium alloy, it offers a rock-solid, vibration-free platform for your smartphone, ensuring your navigation stays stable and visible on Australian roads.

Key Features

  • Rearview Mirror Mounting: The specialized angled bracket fits under standard rearview mirror stems, making it the perfect solution for scooters (like Vespas) or bikes with covered handlebars.
  • Solid Aluminium Construction: Made from T6063 CNC aluminium alloy, this holder is built to resist rust, sun damage, and the wear and tear of daily outdoor use.
  • Mechanical Claw Lock: Uses a reliable screw-knob mechanism to physically clamp the phone. Unlike spring-loaded holders, this mechanical lock ensures your device cannot pop out over bumps.

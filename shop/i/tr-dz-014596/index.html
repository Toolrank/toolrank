Product Description

Upgrade your storage system with these heavy-duty full extension drawer slides, engineered from high-strength cold-rolled steel for reliable support, smooth operation, and long-term durability. Designed for tool drawers, cabinets, storage compartments, and workshop furniture, this locking drawer slide pair helps keep drawers securely closed or fully open while giving you complete access to interior space.

Key Features

  • Heavy-Duty Construction: Made from high-quality, high-density cold-rolled steel to support intensive daily use without easily deforming.
  • Corrosion-Resistant Finish: Blue zinc galvanized coating helps reduce exposure to water and air, improving rust resistance and extending service life.
  • Lock-In and Lock-Out Design: Black side handles allow simultaneous locking and unlocking, helping keep the drawer secure when fully closed or fully extended.
  • 3-Fold Full Extension: Triple-slide design allows the drawer to pull out completely for better visibility and easier access to stored items.

  • Tested Durability: Verified through over 100,000 open-close cycles, with a normal service life rated for over 30 years under proper use.

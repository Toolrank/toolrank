Product Description

This compact 4,000-count mini digital multimeter is built for fast electrical troubleshooting at home or on the job. It measures AC/DC voltage, resistance, and continuity, and also supports NCV (non-contact voltage) detection to help you identify live lines more safely. The color display with bright backlight and built-in flashlight makes readings easy in dark panels or bright outdoor light. Note: Battery not included.

Key Features

  • 4,000-count accurate readings: Clear, stable measurements for everyday diagnostics and maintenance.
  • AC/DC voltage, resistance & continuity: Covers common electrical tests for outlets, wiring, appliances, and circuits.
  • NCV live-wire detection: Quickly check for voltage presence without direct contact—useful for safety checks.
  • Color screen + backlight + flashlight: Read values easily in low-light work areas, distribution cabinets, or outdoors.
  • Data Hold function: Freeze the current reading for easier recording and comparison.
  • Durable, safety-focused build: ABS flame-retardant housing with a removable rubber insulating shell for grip, insulation, and dust protection.

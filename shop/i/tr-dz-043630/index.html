Product Description

Engage your pet with the Rubber Snuffle Ball, a versatile toy designed to stimulate their senses and encourage mental enrichment. Made from durable TPR rubber, this hollow ball features multiple openings where you can hide treats, promoting your pet's natural foraging instincts. The fabric strips attached to the ball add an extra layer of fun, making it ideal for both dogs and cats to sniff, bite, and play with.

Perfect for training, stress relief, or interactive fun, this toy helps slow down eating habits and provides a rewarding challenge for your furry friend.

Creates a cozy and joyful atmosphere for children and families.

This Capybara-shaped silicone night light delivers a soft, calming glow that helps ease nighttime fears and adds a touch of cuteness to any room. Made from high-quality silicone + ABS materials, it’s durable, safe to touch, and perfect for everyday use.

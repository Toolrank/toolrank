Product Overview
This pet grooming spray glove is a practical three in one tool designed for Australian cat and dog owners who want to brush, clean and massage their pets in a single, gentle motion. The glove features a TPR bristle palm that lifts loose fur while providing a soothing massage, and includes a detachable USB rechargeable spray mister that emits a fine water mist to weigh down fur and prevent static flyaways during grooming. The velcro attachment system secures the mister firmly to the mesh glove back, while the lightweight design makes it comfortable for extended grooming sessions. Ideal for short and medium haired pets, this glove simplifies coat maintenance while turning brushing into a bonding experience your pet will enjoy.

Key Features

  • Three in One Grooming Function: Combines gentle brushing to remove loose fur, massaging to stimulate skin and circulation, and light misting to dampen and settle flyaway hair in one simple hand motion.
  • Fine Mist Spray Attachment: The detachable mister emits a delicate water spray that lightly moistens the coat, helping to suppress static and prevent loose fur from floating into the air during brushing.
  • TPR Bristle Palm Surface: The soft rubber bristles gently penetrate the coat to lift dead hair and undercoat while massaging the skin, making the experience pleasant for pets who dislike traditional metal brushes.
  • USB Type C Rechargeable Mister: The spray unit charges conveniently via Type C cable, eliminating the need for disposable batteries and supporting quick top ups between grooming sessions.

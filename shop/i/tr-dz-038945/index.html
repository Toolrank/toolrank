Details

These L-shaped plastic corner protectors are designed to keep packing belts firmly in place while guarding carton edges from cuts and dents. Made from durable yet slightly flexible material, each protector grips the strap securely to prevent slipping, helping to keep strapped boxes neat and stable. The soft surface helps prevent damage to cardboard, books, and other packed items when belts are pulled tight. Compact yet strong, these non-slip corner guards are ideal for bundling cartons, books, parcels, and other loads with plastic or steel strapping.

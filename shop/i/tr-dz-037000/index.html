Simplecom EC312v2 PCIe x4 to 2-Port USB 3.2 Gen II 10Gbps USB-C and USB-A Expansion Card

Upgrade your desktop PC with this high-performance PCIe x4 expansion card, offering USB 3.2 Gen 2 connectivity for fast data transfer speeds of up to 10 Gbps. Compatible with PCIe x4, x8, or x16 slots, this card easily adds 1x USB-C and 1x USB-A port to your system.

The expansion card is powered directly through the PCIe slot, eliminating the need for any external power supply. The integrated PCIe 12V to 5V modules provide stable power to the USB ports for reliable performance.

Easy to install with plug-and-play functionality, this expansion card is automatically recognized by Windows systems for a seamless setup. Perfect for users looking to enhance their system's connectivity and performance with a reliable and simple solution.

  • PCIe x4 expansion card, also compatible with PCIe x8 or x16 slots

  • Adds 1x USB-C and 1x USB-A port to your desktop PC
  • Backward compatible with USB 2.0 and USB 1.1 devices
  • Built-in overcurrent protection on each USB port
  • Powered by PCIe, no external power required
  • Integrated PCIe 12V to 5V module provides power to USB ports

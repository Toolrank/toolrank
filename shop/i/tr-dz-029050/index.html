Product Description

This portable mini Bluetooth thermal printer is your creative and practical companion for everyday life. With a compact, rounded design in pastel colors like cream white, mint green, and lavender, it easily slips into your bag or desk drawer. Connect via Bluetooth to the WalkPrint app and print without ink—just thermal paper. Use it to print study notes, quiz corrections, or vocabulary lists for school, or create stickers, cutouts, and decorations for your bullet journal and scrapbook. It also excels at printing home labels for storage bins, pantry jars, and reminder notes. Whether you are a student, a DIY enthusiast, or someone who loves organization, this printer makes life easier and more fun. As they say, good things come in small packages, and this little gadget delivers big on versatility and convenience.

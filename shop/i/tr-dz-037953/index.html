About this item:
1. Full HD 1080P Dual Recording
Front and rear cameras record in Full HD 1080P, providing clear footage of both the road ahead and behind your vehicle during driving.
2. Motion Detection Recording
Supports motion detection recording. When movement is detected within the cameras view, recording will automatically start.
Motion detection is video-based and may be affected by lighting or background changes.
3. Night Vision (Low-Light Enhancement)
Equipped with night vision technology to enhance visibility in low-light environments, such as street-lit roads or parking areas with ambient lighting.
Night vision improves image clarity in low light but does not work in complete darkness.
Some external light sources are required for the camera to capture footage.
4. Loop Recording Auto Overwrite
Automatically overwrites the oldest footage when the memory card is full, ensuring continuous recording without manual file management.
5. Easy Installation Daily Driving Use
Designed for everyday driving recording. Plug-and-play installation via car charger for hassle-free use.

Parking Recording Notice:
This dash cam does NOT support G-Sensor impact detection.
This dash cam does NOT support Parking Guard / Impact Recording.
This dash cam does NOT support Time-Lapse Parking Mode.
When connected to a hardwire kit, the camera may remain powered and record continuously.
However, continuous parking recording is NOT recommended, as it may:
Drain the vehicle battery
Cause important footage to be overwritten

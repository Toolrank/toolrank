Heavy-Duty Anti-Slip Safety Grab Bar with Luminous Rings - Stainless Steel Core Bathroom Handrail

Product Description

Enhance safety and stability in any environment with our Heavy-Duty Anti-Slip Safety Grab Bar. Expertly engineered with a dual-tube design, this grab bar combines a high-pressure resistant stainless steel inner core with a comfortable, antibacterial nylon outer layer. The surface features raised, non-slip particles to ensure a secure grip, even with wet hands. For added safety in the dark, it incorporates two luminous rings that absorb light during the day and glow at night for up to 72 hours, providing a clear guide in low-light conditions. With a strong load-bearing capacity of up to 300kg, this durable and easy-to-install grab bar is an essential safety addition for bathrooms, hallways, stairwells, and public facilities.

Key Features

  • Anti-Slip Grip: The outer surface is covered in raised concave-convex particles, significantly increasing friction for a firm, secure grip that is effective even when wet.
  • Glow-in-the-Dark Rings: Features two luminous rings that absorb light and glow at night, providing visibility and assistance in emergencies or during nighttime use.
  • Heavy-Duty Construction: A robust 2.5mm thick stainless steel inner tube (25mm diameter) is covered by a 3.5mm thick antibacterial nylon outer tube (35mm diameter) for superior strength and durability.
  • High Load Capacity: Engineered to withstand a concentrated load of up to 300kg (approx. 660 lbs) without deforming.

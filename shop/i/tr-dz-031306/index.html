Details

The Silent Sump Pump Check Spring is a high-performance, quiet backflow prevention device designed for residential sump pump systems. Featuring a spring-loaded flapper mechanism, it significantly reduces water noise during operation—making it ideal for basements, bedrooms, and other sensitive areas where minimal sound is essential. Its clear body allows for easy visual monitoring of water flow, enabling users to quickly identify blockages, ensure proper pump function, and maintain system efficiency without disassembly. This 1-1/2 inch check valve effectively prevents discharged water from flowing back into the sump pit, reducing unnecessary pump cycling and enhancing overall pumping efficiency while extending the life of your sump pump. Built to fit standard 1-1/2” Schedule 40 piping and installed in a 5-1/8” space, it includes stainless steel clamps and flexible rubber couplings for a secure, leak-free connection. Made from high-strength, corrosion-resistant plastic and equipped with 304 stainless steel components, this check valve ensures durable, long-term performance in demanding environments. With included installation instructions, one person can complete setup quickly and easily. A must-have upgrade for any homeowner seeking quieter, more reliable sump pump operation and peace of mind through improved system visibility and protection against backflow.

Description:

This mini Bluetooth thermal label printer connects to the DLabel app for easy template editing and label printing. With batch printing, a 1500mAh rechargeable battery and iOS/Android/Windows compatibility, it is ideal for QR codes, price tags, jewellery, name, food and clothing labels.

Features:

  • Bluetooth Connection and Easy Printing: Load the label paper, secure the paper holder and connect to the DLabel app via Bluetooth to select or edit a template and start printing. The default app connection passwords are 0000 or 1234.
  • 1500mAh Rechargeable Battery: Built-in 1500 mAh lithium battery supports up to approximately 20,000 labels on a full charge under specified conditions. USB Type-C charging makes it convenient for everyday and mobile printing.
  • Versatile Editable Templates: The DLabel app provides various label templates that can be edited according to different needs, supporting QR code, cable, supermarket price, jewellery, name, food and clothing labels.
  • Multi-System and Multi-Language Support: Supports Bluetooth 4.0 and is compatible with iOS, Android and Windows XP/7/8/10. Supports multiple languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Russian, Greek, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

Details

This hollow ear support pillow is designed to provide comfortable side sleeping support while reducing pressure on the ears. Featuring a central opening and ergonomic contouring, it helps maintain proper head and neck alignment, making it ideal for individuals who wear earrings, use headphones, or sleep on their side regularly.

Key Features

  • Hollow Ear Design: A precisely shaped opening allows the ear to rest freely, minimizing discomfort from prolonged pressure
  • Adjustable Fill Level: Comes with removable plush filling—add or remove material to customize firmness based on personal preference
  • Ergonomic Neck Fit Zone: Contoured shape supports the natural curve of the neck and head for better spinal alignment
  • PP Cotton Filling: Lightweight, soft, and durable filling that maintains shape over time without clumping
  • Breathable & Cool Fabric: Outer cover made from breathable material to help regulate temperature during sleep
  • Machine Washable: Removable cover can be easily washed in a washing machine for long-term hygiene
  • Compact & Easy to Store: Lightweight design folds neatly when not in use, perfect for travel or small spaces

How It Works

  1. Remove the outer cover if needed and adjust the internal filling to your desired firmness
  2. Place the pillow on your bed or sofa with the hollow area aligned where your ear will rest
  3. Position your head so that the ear fits naturally into the opening while the neck rests on the curved zone
  4. Sleep comfortably on your side or back without ear pressure or discomfort

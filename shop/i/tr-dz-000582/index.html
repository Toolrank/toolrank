Details

This innovative hand-crank ice crusher can be assembled and ready for use in just three simple steps:

1. **Place the Blade Barrel**
Insert the blade barrel into the corresponding slot in the cup body, ensuring it is fully and securely seated.

2. **Attach the Handle**
Align the handle with the barrel and tighten the nut clockwise until firmly fixed.

3. **Lock the Suction Base**
Rotate the locking knob clockwise to secure the suction base firmly to the countertop, ensuring stable and vibration-free operation.

The compact and practical design features:
- A height of approximately **23.5 cm** and a base width of **11 cm**, taking up minimal space for easy storage.
- A **6.5 cm** diameter blade barrel paired with an **8 cm** tall transparent ice container, providing clear visibility throughout the process.

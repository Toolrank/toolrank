Chain Hanging Bird Feeder Garden Bird Feeder

This chain-hanging double-layer bird feeder is specially designed for gardening enthusiasts and bird protectors. It combines exquisite craftsmanship with practical functions to create a lively natural interaction scene for your courtyard.

Structure Design

Double-layer transparent bowl body: The upper layer with a diameter of 23.5cm can hold bird food, while the lower layer with a diameter of 26.6cm can serve as a water bowl or additional food bowl. The bowls feature vintage patterns and wavy edges, are transparent and beautiful, and make it easy to observe remaining amounts.

Thickened iron chain hanging: The total length is about 56-57cm, equipped with a double hook design and installation accessories. It has stable load-bearing capacity, is wind-resistant and rust-proof, and can be easily hung on tree branches, balconies, or garden brackets.

Separate layered spaces: The upper and lower bowls are separated by the iron chain to avoid mixing food and water, meeting birds dual needs for feeding and drinking.

Installation and Use

Three steps for quick setup:

  1. Separate the connections and assemble the three hooks with the iron chain respectively.

  2. Secure the bowl chains by fastening the hooks into the small holes on the bowl bodies to complete the double-layer structure assembly.

  3. Hang it in areas where birds are active, such as courtyard tree branches or balcony railings, and wait for birds to visit.

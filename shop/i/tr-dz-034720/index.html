Details

Elevate your work to the next level with the NEW Baumr-AG Mobile Adjustable Scaffold. This heavy-duty powder coated steel unit has been designed to provide the safest and easiest solution to raising your working height. It's the perfect fusion between a large and sturdy commercial scaffold system, and an easily contained elevated working surface appropriate for domestic use. In fact, this unit has been designed so that it can be effortlessly assembled and adjusted by a single person working on their own. This is largely thanks to the fast and secure pin loaded platform lock system, allowing users to incrementally raise and lower the work platform from a 700mm min. to a 1870mm max. height depending on the task at hand.

Moreover, the steel reinforced 1.45m2 wooden platform provides plenty of surface space for you and a range of your equipment, while at the same time, it's been purposely designed slim enough to fit through Australian standard doorways. It's fitted with four extra-large 127mm polyurethane lockable caster wheels that ensure optimal traction without the scuff marks. These casters are suitable for both indoor and outdoor use and lock firmly in place to secure your scaffold wherever you need it.

One of the most practical features of this unit lies in its innovative isolated height adjustment. This allows the scaffolding to be placed on uneven surfaces like steps or slopes. Simply raise one side of its posts higher than the other, then place the raised side on the elevated surface to create a level platform. This unique design feature makes the unit applicable to countless more tasks than would otherwise be possible. For instance, working at heights outdoors becomes achievable given the contours of natural surfaces. Tasks like pruning, lopping, hedging, rendering, and cleaning gutters can be fulfilled with ease. Indoors, working on stairways and split-levels is no problem. Execute your work with total peace of mind knowing that the spring loaded platform clamps, and eight locking pins are securing the platform and casters in place.

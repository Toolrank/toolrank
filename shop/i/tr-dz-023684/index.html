How to Use:
1. Wash the electrodes of the PH meter with pure water.
2. Immerse the pH electrode in a mixed phosphate standard buffer solution with a pH value of 6.86 (at 25°C) and stir gently. Then stand still and read. If it does not display 6.86, press and hold CAL for 5 seconds and let go, and wait for the meter to automatically recognize calibration, 6.86 is displayed, that is, the calibration is successful.
3. Clean the electrode again with pure water, and then insert the electrode into the pH4.00 potassium hydrogen phosphonium dicarboxate or pH9.18 borax standard buffer solution for calibration. The calibration method is the same as 6.86.

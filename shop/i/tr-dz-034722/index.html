Elevate your work to the next level with the Baumr-AG Mobile Adjustable Scaffold with Trap Door. This heavy-duty powder coated steel unit has been designed to provide the safest and easiest solution to raising your working height. In fact, this unit has been designed so that it can be effortlessly assembled and adjusted by a single person working on their own. This is largely thanks to the fast and secure pin loaded platform lock system, allowing users to incrementally raise and lower the work platform between 70cm and 187cm. The scaffold comes equipped with a trap door hatch for easy, convenient access.

It's fitted with large 5' polyurethane lockable castor wheels that ensure optimal manoeuvrability, without the scuff marks.

This Baumr-AG scaffold features handy isolated height adjustment that allows it to be placed on uneven surfaces. Working on stairways, split-levels or outdoor slopes is no problem. Execute your work with total peace of mind knowing that eight locking pins are safeguarding the platform and castors in place.

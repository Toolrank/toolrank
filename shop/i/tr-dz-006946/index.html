Usage

Install the suction cups only on clean, smooth, non-porous surfaces such as glass, metal, tile, stainless steel, or acrylic. Do not place them over grout lines or on uneven tiles, textured panels, unfinished marble, wallpaper, or painted walls. For best results, clean and dry the surface and suction cups before installation. If suction is not achieved initially, wash the suction cup, dry it with a hair dryer, and test it on a smooth glass surface.

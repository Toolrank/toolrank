The Rossi MIG-170N - powerful, yet extremely lightweight 170Amp MIG/Stick iGBT inverter welder is a must have for any handyman or serious DIYer. Highly compact and featuring true iGBT DC inverter technology, youre assured an instant start and deep penetration.

With an efficient pure copper Toroidal Transformer and output current that is adjustable between 20A and 170A, you can achieve a quality weld whether it be with steel, stainless steel or cast iron. The advanced cooling system with its large fan ensures heat dissipates effectively for continued high performance and durability.

This awesome unit is capable of running a wide range of electrode, and is well suited to stick welding thick metals outdoors and even in windy conditions, and is forgiving when working on dirty or rusty metals.

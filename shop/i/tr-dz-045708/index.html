Interactive Duck-Shaped Automatic Pet Feeder – Smart Treat Dispenser for Cats & Dogs with 3 Adjustable Port Sizes

Bring fun and intelligence to your pet’s feeding routine with this adorable duck-shaped automatic treat dispenser. Designed for both cats and dogs, this interactive feeder encourages natural foraging behavior, slows down fast eaters, and provides mental stimulation—ideal for keeping pets engaged when you're busy or away.

Weighing only about 350g, this lightweight yet durable feeder is made from high-quality, non-toxic plastic that’s safe for daily use. Its compact size (approx. 22cm × 14cm × 8cm) fits easily in any room, and the cheerful yellow duck design adds a playful touch to your home.

Key Features:

  • 3 Interchangeable Port Covers: Includes three different-sized outlet caps (small, medium, large) to precisely control kibble flow based on your pet’s food size (ideal for 6–18mm dry pellets). Simply twist off the current cover and replace it with the appropriate one.
  • Interactive Play & Feeding: Press the tail switch to activate the internal rotating mechanism. Pets must nudge, paw, or roll the duck to release treats—turning mealtime into an engaging game that reduces boredom and anxiety.
  • Promotes Healthy Eating: Helps prevent bloating and digestive issues by slowing down rapid eaters through controlled, portioned dispensing.
  • No Batteries Required: Entirely mechanical—no electricity, charging, or complex setup needed. Just fill, cap, and play!
  • Easy to Clean & Refill: The wide opening allows quick filling and thorough cleaning. Rinse under water after use; air dry before reuse.

The Bullet ZPS electric scooter boasts striking pink decals on the FlowGripTM deck which offers plenty of control for safe riding. Thanks to the 140 watt motor it can zip along at speeds of up to 15km/h, which is fast enough for maximum fun, without causing chaos in the local park.

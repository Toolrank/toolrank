Details

The 3-piece digital luggage set is designed for frequent flyers and modern travellers who need convenience, safety, and style. Each suitcase is crafted from durable ABS material with a sleek finish and lined with soft peach-skin velvet for a premium interior.

The built-in digital weighing scale, located in the handle and powered by a CR2032 battery, lets you check your bag’s weight instantly to avoid airline fees. A USB charging port is also included, allowing you to charge your devices on-the-go by connecting a power bank inside the suitcase. For security, each suitcase comes with a TSA-approved lock for stress-free international travel.

Maneuverability is effortless with silent 360° spinner wheels and a sturdy aluminium handle with iron tie rod. Despite being lightweight, the set offers impressive durability and strength. The three sizes—20″, 24″, and 28″—are perfect for short trips, medium journeys, or long-haul travel.

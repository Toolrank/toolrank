Product Highlights: Superior Safety and Dual-Handle Control

Engineered for strength and maximum handler comfort, this 2.5-meter (approximately 8.2-foot) heavy duty leash is ideal for training and walking medium to large dogs. It features a robust double-layer nylon weave, reflective stitching for low-light visibility, and two separate padded handles for versatile, precise control in any environment.

  • Double Layer Tightly Woven Nylon: Constructed from two layers of thick, durable nylon webbing that is tightly stitched together, providing exceptional strength, tear resistance, and longevity.
  • Dual Padded Handles: Includes a main ergonomic handle with thick inner padding for comfort against the wrist, and a secondary "Traffic Handle" positioned closer to the clasp. The traffic handle is also padded, allowing for instant, close-quarters control in busy streets or crowded areas.

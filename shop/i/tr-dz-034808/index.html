Details

The Baumr-AG BM-WC64 Wall Chaser offers fast and dust-free chasing and is ideal for concealment of conduit of wires.

THE POWER YOU NEED
This unit is equipped with a powerful heavy-duty 2000W motor that efficiently chase through tough concrete, brickwork and besser blocks at 6500rpm.

QUAD DIAMOND BLADES
Unlike traditional models with just two blades, Baumr-AG Wall Chasers ensure that any breakout is a quick and easy step.

ADJUSTABLE WITH AND DEPTH
The blades are quick to change, and channels between 11-44mm are possible with different combinations of the blades and spacers, and with a depth range of 25-44mm.

OPTIMAL DUST REDUCTION
For wet-cutting the water pipe and supplied electric pump can draw water from a bucket, or can be connected to a hose. The BM-WC64 has a standard 32mm DSS outlet for connection to a wet and dry vac or the optional Baumr-AG Wall Chaser vacuum.

SAFETY FIRST
The Baumr-AG Wall Chaser meets the required AS/NZS 3122 electrical safety standard, and features a hand cover to provide protection from debris and dust.

ERGONOMIC OPERATION
With the BM-WC64, vertical or horizontal channeling is not a problem, thanks to the rotating handle.

Expand USB connectivity with this 7-port USB 3.0 hub, it can add up to 7 USB devices quickly and easily, such as external USB drive, card reader, pen drive, mouse, keyboard, and more.

This 7-port USB hub is a sleek and stylish extension to your PC or laptop, making it easy for any user to connect more USB devices to computer.

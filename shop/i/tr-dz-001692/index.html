Reliable Temperature Monitoring for Your Aquarium

For Australian aquarium enthusiasts, maintaining the ideal water temperature is crucial for fish health and happiness. Our set of two floating aquarium thermometers provides an accurate, easy-to-read solution for both freshwater and saltwater tanks.

Key Features

  • Dual Scale Design: Each thermometer displays temperature in both Celsius and Fahrenheit, so you can choose your preferred unit.
  • Wide Measurement Range: From 0 to 43°C (32 to 110°F), covering all typical aquarium conditions.
  • Safe Construction: Made from premium glass and completely mercury-free, ensuring no risk to your aquatic life.
  • Easy Installation: Comes with two suction cups per thermometer for secure, adjustable placement.

How to Use

Simply attach the suction cup to the thermometer, press it onto the inside of your tank glass, and allow it to float. The colored liquid will settle at the bottom, making the temperature reading instantly visible. For best accuracy, position the thermometer away from heaters or direct sunlight.

Upgrade your DIY and repair projects with this 14mm metal grommet/eyelet kit—durable, rust-resistant hardware plus a punch and mandrel for clean holes and secure fastening. Ideal for reinforcing tarps, tents, awnings, canvas, denim and more, all organized in a clear storage box for fast access.

Usage

Place the fabric/paper on a solid surface (e.g., slate), keep the punch perpendicular, then strike with a hammer to create the hole. Insert the eyelet and washer, then set firmly with a few hits. Perfect for outdoor gear repairs, camping tarps, tent reinforcement, home sewing, and craft projects. (Hammer not included.)

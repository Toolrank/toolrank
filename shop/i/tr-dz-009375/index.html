Sizing Tips

For two-legged clothes, size by chest; for four-legged clothes, size by back length. Measure from the base of the neck to the base of the tail (tail not included) and allow about 2cm of slack. If measurements fall between two sizes, choose the larger size. Not suitable for large breeds. Colors may vary slightly due to lighting and screen settings.

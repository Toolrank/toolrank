Product Overview

Protect your outdoor spaces with this Solar Powered Ultrasonic Animal Repeller, designed to humanely deter unwanted pests from gardens, lawns, and yards. This versatile pest deterrent combines ultrasonic sound waves, bright LED flashing lights, and a sensitive PIR motion sensor to create an effective barrier against cats, dogs, birds, rodents, and snakes. Ideal for Australian homes, it offers a chemical-free solution for maintaining a pristine garden environment without harming wildlife.

Key Features

  • Dual Repellent Technology: Emits ultrasonic frequencies and intense LED flashes simultaneously to startle and discourage animals from entering your property.
  • Solar Powered Efficiency: Equipped with a high-efficiency solar panel on top, allowing for eco-friendly operation and automatic charging during the day for night-time protection.
  • Smart PIR Motion Detection: Features a built-in passive infrared sensor that activates the device only when movement is detected, conserving energy and ensuring targeted coverage.
  • Wide Coverage Area: Provides a detection range of approximately 12 meters with a 120-degree angle, covering large sections of gardens, driveways, and patios.
  • Versatile Installation: Comes with a 3-section ground stake for easy insertion into soil, and includes keyhole slots on the back for wall mounting options.
  • Durable Weatherproof Build: Constructed from robust ABS plastic designed to withstand outdoor elements, rain, and sun exposure.

Why It Stands Out

Unlike traditional traps or chemical sprays, this device offers a humane and maintenance-free approach to pest control. The combination of sound and light addresses the limitations of single-mode repellents, making it harder for animals to adapt. Its sleek green design blends naturally into garden foliage, while the adjustable stake ensures stability in various soil types. It is a practical, "set and forget" solution for homeowners seeking to protect their landscaping and outdoor living areas.

Product Description

Enjoy refreshing homemade shaved ice, slushies, snow cones, and chilled cocktails with this compact manual rotary ice crusher. Designed for convenient countertop use, it features durable stainless steel blades, a hand-crank operation, and a detachable design for easier cleaning—perfect for family treats, summer parties, and everyday cold drinks.

Key Features

  • Effortless hand-crank operation: Add ice cubes, secure the lid, and turn the handle to create finely crushed ice without electricity.
  • Fast ice crushing: Produces refreshing shaved ice in approximately 20 seconds, depending on ice size and operating speed.
  • Durable stainless steel blades: Thickened blades are designed to crush ice evenly for a smoother texture with less coarse residue.
  • Adjustable ice pressing system: The internal spring press helps hold ice securely and supports consistent crushing with different ice cube thicknesses.
  • Easy to clean: The detachable body allows convenient disassembly and thorough cleaning after use.
  • Versatile summer drink maker: Ideal for shaved ice, snow cones, slushies, ice cream toppings, margaritas, and other chilled beverages at home or during gatherings.

Usage

Place ice cubes into the top container, fit the lid securely, and turn the hand crank until the desired shaved-ice texture is reached. Serve immediately with syrup, fruit, ice cream, or your favorite beverage. Ideal for home kitchens, family gatherings, summer parties, picnics, and entertaining. Hand wash and dry removable parts after use.

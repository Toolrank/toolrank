Product Description

Make every step safer with our 8" x 30" non-slip carpet stair treads, designed to protect kids, elderly family members, and pets from slipping on indoor stairs. The soft polyester surface cushions each step, reduces noise, and keeps feet warm on cold staircases, while the reusable self-adhesive backing holds firmly in place without leaving residue or damaging your floors.

Key Features

  • Anti-Slip Protection: The TPU non-slip backing grips firmly on wood, tile, and glass stairs, helping kids, seniors, and pets go up and down safely without fear of slipping or falling.
  • Soft & Quiet Comfort: The three-layer design (wear-resistant polyester surface, nonwoven buffer layer, TPU bottom) feels soft underfoot, insulates against cold steps, and reduces stair noise for a calmer home.
  • Tool-Free Installation: Simply clean the step, peel off the backing film, and press down—in 5 minutes your stairs are covered, with no glue, tape, or tacks required.

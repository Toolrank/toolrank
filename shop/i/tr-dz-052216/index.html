Metal Woodworking Angle Positioning Clamp with Laser-Calibrated Scale for Precise Miter Cutting, Jointing, and Framing in Workshop and DIY Projects

Product Description

Achieve clean, accurate angled cuts and secure joints with this metal woodworking angle positioning clamp. Designed for woodworkers who demand repeatable precision, it combines a sturdy metal body with a black baked finish that resists corrosion and wear while maintaining hardness for long-term use. The laser-calibrated scale delivers clear, easy-to-read measurements, and the black background with white markings reduces glare so you can focus on the task at hand. Whether you are assembling picture frames, building furniture, or installing trim, this tool helps hold stock firmly at the desired angle so your hands stay free to drill, glue, or fasten. Three shape options cover different adjustment ranges: a small fan shape for 22.5° to 45°, a large fan shape for 45° to 90°, and a semicircular shape for 90° to 180°. Each clamp measures 15 cm / 5.90 in in width, with lengths of 13 cm / 5.11 in, 21.5 cm / 8.46 in, and 30 cm / 11.81 in respectively. The design supports clamping and fixing wooden pieces at various angles, making angled joinery more manageable and consistent. As the saying goes, measure twice, cut once, and this angle clamp helps you do exactly that by keeping your workpiece steady and your angle true. Ideal for miter joints, corner braces, and multi-sided assemblies, it is a practical addition to any woodworking bench, whether you are a seasoned craftsman or a weekend DIY enthusiast.

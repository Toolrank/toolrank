Product Description

Secure your race bib with lightweight magnetic bib clips designed for runners and active sports. Strong magnetic attraction helps hold the bib firmly in place without traditional safety pins, making these reusable accessories a convenient choice for marathons, triathlons, cycling, track events, school sports and more.

Key Features

  • Strong Magnetic Hold: High-strength internal magnets provide reliable attraction to help keep your race bib securely attached during active movement.
  • Pin-Free Design: Magnetic race bib clips help reduce the need for traditional pins that may pierce skin or leave holes in clothing.
  • Lightweight and Portable: Compact construction is easy to carry in a race kit, gym bag or travel pouch without adding unnecessary bulk.

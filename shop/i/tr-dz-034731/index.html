Details

The Baumr-AG BPR-1100e 40V battery-powered wheelbarrow is built to make heavy load handling faster, safer and far less demanding. Designed for construction sites, landscaping projects, farms and commercial environments, it delivers dependable performance when shifting materials over flat ground or tackling sloped terrain.

Powered by a smooth and efficient brushless motor, the front-wheel drive system provides controlled, consistent assistance exactly when you need it. Variable speed operation allows you to match pace to the task, while the electronic braking system ensures smooth, predictable stopping for confident operation when working on hills.

The battery compartment can accommodate two batteries, allowing you to manually switch over to a spare for an even longer runtime.

For transport and relocation there is an extendable tow bar that can be used to move the unit over longer distances around worksites. With the clutch disengaged, this unit can be towed at up to 30 km/h.

Durability is built into every part of the design. A powder-coated steel frame stands up to daily worksite use, while the large polypropylene bucket offers excellent resistance to impacts, chemicals and heavy wear. Tall sidewalls help keep loose, bulky loads secure, reducing spills and wasted effort.

Large pneumatic tyres improve traction across dirt, gravel and uneven ground, helping reduce operator fatigue while maintaining stability. Powered by a removable lithium battery with weather-resistant protection, the BPR-1100e is a practical, hardworking solution designed to move more with less effort, day after day.

Create an aromatherapy oasis with the Cool Mist Ultrasonic Diffuser, designed by PureSpa!

The ultrasonic technology used in the diffuser works by breaking up the combined mixture of water and essential oil into millions of micro-particles. This then diffuses into the surrounding air with the activated ingredients from the essential oil. Under this richly heightened effect of aromatherapy, your mind will be calmed and relaxed, and the air around you enhanced.

The product features an easy-to-fill 400ml tank, that lasts for up to 3 hours of continuous duration or 6 hours in intermittent mode.

Simple to operate, and easy to clean, the PureSpa Cool Mist Ultrasonic Diffuser will add a touch of class to any space it is put in.

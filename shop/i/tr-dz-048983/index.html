Product Overview

Make outdoor BBQ cooking easier with this Stainless Steel Rolling Grill Basket, designed to hold a variety of ingredients securely. Made from 304 and 430 stainless steel, the cylindrical mesh basket features a 360-degree rolling design to help distribute heat more evenly without individually turning each piece of food.   • 360-Degree Rolling Design: Rotate the cylindrical basket to expose ingredients to heat from different sides for more even grilling.

Unlike traditional BBQ skewers, this rolling grill basket holds multiple ingredients together, reducing the need to turn individual pieces.

Product Description

This 2-in-1 flywheel puller tool (M24x1 RH / M27x1 LH) makes removing stuck flywheels fast and hassle-free. The dual-thread design covers two common sizes in one compact tool, while the extended center bolt delivers smooth, even pulling force to pop the flywheel off without damaging your stator or crank threads—a must-have flywheel removal tool for scooter and dirt bike maintenance.

Key Features

  • 2-in-1 Dual Thread Design: Side 1 features M24x1 right-hand threads and Side 2 features M27x1 left-hand threads, so one tool covers two common flywheel sizes and saves you from buying separate pullers
  • Easy Flywheel Removal: Extended bolts apply steady, centered pulling force to pull out the flywheel effortlessly, protecting internal engine parts from scratches or damage
  • Wide Compatibility: Works with most GY6 125cc 150cc scooters and many 2-stroke dirt bikes, enduro and MX motorcycles, and ATVs with external flywheels, including models with earlier point-type ignitions

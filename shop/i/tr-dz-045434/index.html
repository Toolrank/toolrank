Multifunctional Dog Toy with Cooling, Dental Care & Floating Features

Product Description

Innovative pet toy combining cooling hydration, dental hygiene and water play functions. Features hollow chambers for ice filling, textured cleaning surfaces for teeth, and buoyant design for aquatic fun. The removable bone-shaped chew component provides extended play versatility.

Key Features

  • Cooling Hydration: Freezable hollow core provides chilled water during play
  • Dental Hygiene System: Serrated friction teeth clean molars, incisors and canines
  • Water Floating Design: Floats during bath time or swimming activities
  • Dual-Component Build: Removable nylon bone + soft TPR main body
  • Cleaning Channels: Apply pet toothpaste for enhanced dental care
  • Mental Stimulation: Engages dogs to reduce anxiety and destructive behavior

Usage Instructions

  • Cooling Function: Fill through hollow holes, freeze 1-2 hours before use
  • Dental Cleaning: Apply pet toothpaste to cleaning channels during play
  • Water Play: Use directly in pools, baths or natural water sources
  • Chew Component: Detach bone-shaped nylon rod for independent chewing

Technical Specifications

  • Materials: Food-grade TPR + nylon components
  • Dimensions: 6.67in (H)*2.72in (L)*2.40in (W)
  • Weight: 3.95oz lightweight construction

Safety & Compliance

Meets Australian pet toy safety standards. Supervise pets during use. Limit daily use to 20-30 minutes. Discard if damaged. Not for aggressive chewers. Regularly inspect for wear.

Details

Safeguard your plants at any time of year with this handy Randy Travis Machinery digital irrigation control unit. Ensuring your plants get the right amount of water (but not too much) is straightforward when you use a digital water control unit. Each unit can be set to periodically irrigate your plants for between one and 300 minutes each cycle. This system takes the stress out of watering and is a great option to ensure plants stay hydrated whilst you're away.

Features:

  • Programmable irrigation for between 1 and 300 minutes on each cycle
  • Eco-water-saving mode
  • In the event of rain, the watering can be paused for up to six days

Specifications:

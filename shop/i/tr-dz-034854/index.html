Details

The Unimac DL31 10ft. Drywall Plasterboard Lifter is the all-in-one solution for fast and efficient ceiling gyprock installation.

Made of galvanised, powder-coated, high-quality, carbon steel. The Unimac Panel Hoist can lift plasterboard from a 150cm working height to a maximum height of 3.2m. The Unimac DL31 accepts panels up to 1200mm x 3600mm sized panels up to 68kg

The wide tri-leg base design ensures a stable working platform, even at maximum height. All 3 smooth rolling rubber castor wheels are lockable for maximum stability. The simple toe lever secures the wheels in place, allowing you to continue working safely.

The highly portable design allows fast setup by just one person. Once the job is done it packs up just as fast, ready to be transported to the next job. Turn the labour-intensive task of putting up ceiling panels into a quick and easy task for one. It is simply the best choice to increase productivity, reduce man hours and save you money.

High Visibility Chicken & Duck Safety Vest - Reflective Poultry Jacket

Increase the visibility and safety of your backyard flock with this practical Reflective Poultry Vest. Designed for chickens, ducks, and other similarly sized birds, this vest features a prominent reflective strip to make your birds more visible, especially in low-light conditions. It also offers a layer of protection for their backs and feathers.

Key Features:

  • Enhanced Visibility: Equipped with a wide reflective strip across the back, significantly improving your poultry's visibility to vehicles or in dim light, enhancing their safety outdoors.
  • Durable Material: Made from waterproof/water-resistant polyester Oxford cloth, offering durability and helping to keep your bird drier in damp conditions.
  • Feather Protection: Acts as a saddle or jacket, providing protection for the back feathers against pecking or during mating.
  • Adjustable & Secure Fit: Features strong, adjustable Velcro straps that go under the wings for a secure yet comfortable fit on birds of various sizes within the intended range.
  • Easy to Use: Simple design makes putting the vest on and taking it off quick and straightforward.

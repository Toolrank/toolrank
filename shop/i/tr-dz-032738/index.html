Corn Cob Cat Scratching Post

42cm Peeled-Corn Design · Crinkle Husks · Sisal-Wrapped Cob · Smiling Face

Meet the Corn Cob Cat Scratching Post — a playful peeled-corn character standing 42 cm tall on a 30 cm wood-tone plush-lined base. A golden rope-wrapped cob with big cartoon eyes and a tuft of corn silk peeks out from between two soft green husk leaves, turning a serious scratching surface into an irresistible harvest friend.

The cob itself is wound in dense natural sisal rope, creating the coarse, vertically ribbed texture cats seek out for clawing, cheek-rubbing, and full upright stretches. The surrounding green husk is made from soft plush fabric lined with crinkle paper that rustles at the lightest touch — a sound trigger that reliably draws cats back for another round. Beneath, a plush-covered MDF base keeps the whole piece planted without scratching floors.

Compact, silent, and battery-free, it is ideal for cats that respond to crinkle sound, small apartments, desk-side and bedside placement, and character-led gifting — a novelty scratcher that earns its place in the open, not tucked away in a corner.

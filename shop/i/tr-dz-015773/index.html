Product Description

Stay informed with this compact carbon monoxide detector featuring a 2.1-inch IPS color screen, fast 2-second data refresh, and accurate monitoring of carbon monoxide, temperature, and humidity.   • Accurate CO Detection: Built-in high-performance electrochemical sensing chip delivers sensitive and reliable carbon monoxide concentration monitoring from 0-999ppm.
  • Clear IPS Display: 2.1-inch IPS screen with 240 x 296 resolution and wide viewing angle shows CO level, temperature, and humidity at a glance.

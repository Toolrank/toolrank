Efficient, Continuous Ice Production

The commercial snowflake ice machine features a high-performance compressor and a stable air-cooling system that rapidly dissipates heat, enhancing production efficiency. It achieves quick cooling in 40 seconds and produces ice in just 3 seconds after pre-cooling, capable of producing approximately 176.4 lbs / 80 kg of ice per day, ensuring efficient and continuous operation.

Variety of Ice Shapes

By adjusting the roller speed, this snowflake ice machine creates various textures of ice cream, such as snow ice shape, spaghetti shape, and waterfall shape, each offering a silky smooth texture.

Easy Operation

Equipped with an intelligent touch control panel, it offers easy operation with functions like speed adjustment and one-touch cleaning. The display screen shows the roller's rotation speed for convenient monitoring.

Sturdy and Durable

The machine body is primarily made of SUS201 stainless steel, while the roller is crafted from food-grade SUS304 stainless steel. The blades is made of 5CR15 stainless steel, offering rust resistance and easy cleaning, ensuring durability.

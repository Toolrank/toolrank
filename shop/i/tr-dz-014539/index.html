Product Description

This steel stone splitting wedge set is built for efficient rock and concrete breaking, offering high hardness, strong toughness, and reliable resistance to deformation under heavy pressure. Designed for drilled holes of about 0.6 inch diameter, it helps split granite, marble, hard stone, and concrete with controlled force from the inside out, making it a practical choice for masonry, quarry work, construction, and renovation tasks.

Key Features

  • Durable Steel Construction: Made of selected steel for high hardness, compression resistance, and long-lasting toughness during repeated splitting work.
  • Precise Working Size: Measures 16 x 105 mm with a 16 mm diameter, suitable for holes around 0.6 inches in diameter for stable, effective use.
  • Efficient Splitting Action: Expands inside pre-drilled holes to create outward pressure, helping split stone and concrete in a controlled and practical way.
  • Easy Operation: Works by tapping the wedges in sequence with a light hammer, allowing even pressure distribution across the splitting line.
  • Wide Material Compatibility: Suitable for hard stone, rock, granite, marble, and concrete in professional and heavy-duty applications.
  • Practical Jobsite Use: Ideal for construction, stone processing, demolition support, and repair projects where manual splitting tools are needed.

Product Description:
Heat Powered Fan
Wood stove fan doesn't require batteries or electricity. It rotates by using the heat on the surface of the stove and automatically adjusts the speed according to the surface temperature of the stove.
Dual Head
The fireplace fan is designed with double head which can distribute heat more evenly, diffusing warm air throughout the whole room perfectly, and continuously bringing warmth in cold winter.
Overheating Protection
The fireplace fan has built-in bimetallic safety device at the bottom. Once the temperature exceeds 644 F//340C, this bimetallic safety device will gently lift the front of the fan to protect the motor and generator set.
Silent Working
The heat powered fan produces less noise when working, less than 32db, so you don't even know where the fan is, but you still feel warm. No worry about the working fan disturb your families.
Wide Application
The heat powered fan works with gas/ pellet/ wood/ log/ burner stove, which can effectively improve the circulation of warm air. It is an essential warm winter artifact for household.

Product Description:

The Murano lighting series features matching floor and table lamps that combine cylindrical and spherical shapes with matte and textured glass with gold or pewter metallic accents that support the fabric lampshade. Two glass orbs separated by thin metallic rings sit above the metal base.

For contrast, the orbs feature a plain coloured glass orb on top of a textured glass sphere below, sitting flush on the base of the table lamps or projected high by a metallic support column on the floor lamp.

The pewter design features a cylindrical linen lampshade with pewter-infused bottom orb and striking white sphere above. In contrast, the white Munaro lamps use predominantly white features, with a marbled glass lower sphere, on a brass base and body.

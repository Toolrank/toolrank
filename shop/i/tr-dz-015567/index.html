Product Description

Upgrade your portable AC venting with this PVC adjustable window sealing kit designed to connect the exhaust hose to a sliding window/door, helping reduce hot air backflow and improve cooling efficiency for portable air conditioners and dryers.

Key Features

  • Adjustable length: Freely stretches to fit different openings, with a maximum adjustable length of 110 cm for flexible, safer installation.
  • Sliding window/door compatible: Window adapter plate works with sliding windows and sliding doors for home, apartment, or rental use.
  • Durable PVC build: Portable, sturdy, and built for repeated seasonal use; easy to wipe clean.
  • Exhaust hose connection: Designed for portable air conditioners with a single-diameter air outlet; suitable for counterclockwise exhaust pipe setups.
  • Gap sealing tape included: Use the tape to seal gaps between the plate and window edge; trim to size to reduce drafts and air leakage.
  • Two-piece plate set: Includes an inner + outer plate pair to create a stable window panel assembly.

Usage

Ideal for sealing and venting a portable AC or dryer in bedrooms, living rooms, apartments, offices, RV-style spaces: install the plates in a sliding window/door, connect the exhaust hose via the connector, then apply the included tape to seal remaining gaps for better comfort and airflow control.

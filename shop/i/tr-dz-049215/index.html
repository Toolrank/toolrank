Product Overview

This 2 pack solar snake and rodent repeller set is designed for outdoor gardens, lawns and similar ground areas. Each 25 cm stake uses a built-in buzzer and motor vibration system to transmit sonic pulses and vibration through the surrounding ground, helping create an area that may discourage snakes, rats, mice and other ground-sensitive pests from remaining nearby.

Key Features

  • Sonic and Vibration Operation: Built-in buzzer and motor generate sound and ground vibration to help discourage unwanted animals from staying around the installation area.
  • Solar Powered Design: Integrated solar panel collects energy from daylight, reducing the need for external wiring during normal outdoor use.
  • 2 Pack Coverage: Includes two ground stakes, allowing placement in separate sections of a garden, lawn or outdoor area.
  • IPX4 Rain Resistance: Designed to withstand rain and splashing water during outdoor use. The units should not be submerged in water.

  • One-Touch Control: Side power button provides straightforward operation after installation.
  • Compact Outdoor Design: Slim stake format takes up minimal ground space while positioning the solar panel above the soil surface.

Why It Stands Out

The combination of solar power, ground vibration and sonic operation makes this 2 pack suitable for outdoor areas where a simple wire-free deterrent option is preferred. Using two stakes also gives more flexibility when positioning units around garden beds, lawns, pathways and other suitable soil areas.

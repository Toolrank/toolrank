Product Description

Create a smooth, practical DIY Lazy Susan with this durable metal turntable bearing. Designed for easy installation, stable 360-degree rotation, and versatile home projects, it is ideal for trays, wooden boards, mirrors, cabinet organizers, and other decorative applications.

Key Features

  • Easy DIY Installation: Features 8 pre-punched mounting holes with a 5 mm hole diameter; simply secure it with 8 suitable screws.
  • Durable Metal Construction: Made from strong metal with a zinc-plated surface to help resist oxidation, rust, and everyday wear.
  • Smooth 360-Degree Rotation: The ball-bearing raceway supports flexible, stable rotation for convenient access and reliable movement.
  • Double-Bearing Design: Helps enclose and protect the balls while improving rotational stability and load-bearing performance.

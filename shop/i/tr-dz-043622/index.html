Product Description

Protect your phone during water activities with the IPX8 Waterproof Phone Pouch. The sturdy ABS frame and secure sealing mechanism keep your device safe from water, dust, and sand, making it perfect for swimming, diving, or beach outings.

The transparent window allows you to use your phone's touchscreen without removing it from the pouch, ensuring convenience and protection. With its IPX8 rating, this pouch provides reliable waterproofing up to a certain depth, offering peace of mind for all your water-based activities.

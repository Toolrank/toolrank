RV Caravan Rain Gutter Drip Rail J-Shape Rubber Seal Strip

Product Description

This J-shaped rain gutter drip rail is a flexible rubber seal strip designed to channel rainwater away from the roofline of caravans, motorhomes, trucks, boats and cars. Made from soft TPE material, it is wear resistant and resistant to ageing, so it keeps its shape and flexibility through repeated outdoor use. The curved J profile guides water run-off neatly away from windows, doors and body panels, helping to reduce streaking and water pooling along the edges. It can be trimmed to suit different lengths and follows gentle curves and corners for a neat, continuous finish along the vehicle. Lightweight and easy to handle, it is a practical addition for travel, camping trips and general vehicle maintenance. Suitable for caravan owners, road trip travellers and anyone looking to manage rain water run-off on their vehicle.

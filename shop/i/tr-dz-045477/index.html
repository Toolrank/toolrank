Versatile Pet Carrier Tote Bag for Small Dogs & Cats - Waterproof, Car Seat Function & Adjustable Safety Exit

Product Description

Travel in comfort and style with your small pet using this versatile and thoughtfully designed carrier tote. Made from a specially crafted waterproof and skin-friendly fabric, this bag ensures your pet stays dry and comfortable. It features an adjustable exit with a drawstring, providing a secure and customized fit around your pet's neck to guarantee their safety and prevent escapes. The carrier includes a comfortable, washable seat cushion with an integrated pillow, allowing your pet to rest their head and enjoy the view. With its multi-functional design, it can be used as a shoulder bag, a handbag, secured in a car seat, or placed on top of luggage, making it the perfect travel companion for you and your furry friend.

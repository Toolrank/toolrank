Mini Butterfly Hair Clips Instruction Manual 1. Product Specifications Quantity: 30 Pieces per pack Single Clip Dimension: 1.3cm (height) × 2.1cm (width) Material: Polished silver plated hollow metal with spring claw hinge Core Keywords: Mini Butterfly Hair Clips, Metal Butterfly Hair Barrettes, Tiny Butterfly Hair Pins Application: Daily hairstyling, braid decoration, bridal, party, cosplay, hair fixation 2. Usage Steps Pinch the two sides of the butterfly clip gently to open the claw jaw. Slide open jaw onto the section of hair you want to secure (braid, bangs, loose strands). Release your pinch to let the spring lock the clip firmly onto hair. Arrange multiple clips evenly across braids or scattered through hair for layered decorative styling. To remove, pinch clip sides again to open jaw and slide out from hair slowly. 3. Care & Maintenance Wipe metal clip surfaces with dry soft cloth to remove hair oil and dust after frequent use. Store all clips in a small sealed jewelry box when not in use to avoid scratches and air oxidation tarnish. Do not bend the spring claw hinge with excessive force to prevent loss of clamping tension. Avoid contact with perfume, hair spray chemicals for long periods to preserve silver plating shine. 4. Safety Notes Small miniature metal accessories, keep far away from toddlers and young children to prevent swallowing and choking hazards. Check clip edges before use; discard any bent or sharp broken clips to avoid scalp scratches. Not suitable for sleeping wear—remove all hair clips before rest to prevent hair tangling or scalp pressure irritation.

Details

With its aggressive stance and exposed suspension design, the VALK Ego 8 is built to look and feel like a serious off-road machine.

A high-torque motor paired with a 48V battery delivers strong acceleration and hill climbing, along with a maximum range of 55km. You can access multiple speed modes with up to 45km/h being possible (refer to the user manual to unlock this top speed). It’s performance that goes beyond the daily commute, giving you power in reserve when you need it.

The Ego 8’s rugged dual suspension system works with large 10-inch pneumatic tyres to smooth out bumps and uneven surfaces. The wide deck and solid frame geometry create a more planted, stable ride feel, whether you're navigating city streets or rougher paths.

Dual disc brakes provide confident stopping performance and improved control, giving you added peace of mind when riding at speed or tackling varied terrain. The reinforced aluminium frame supports riders up to 120kg, while the overall design balances durability with everyday usability.

Despite its heavy-duty presence, the Ego 8 remains practical, with a quick-fold mechanism for transport and storage, and integrated LED lighting for visibility in low light conditions.

Finished with bold industrial styling and premium detailing, the VALK Ego 8 is built for riders who want a tougher, more commanding ride experience.

Details

The Baumr-AG DBR-255 Dual Bevel Compound Mitre Sliding Saw boasts a powerful 2000W motor. Spinning at 5000rpm it drives a 10 inch (254mm) tungsten carbide tipped blade for a fine and perfectly flush cut. The Dual bevel head allows you to make complex compound cuts quickly and easily. The 13.5 inch reach of the double bearing rail allows you to perform large cross cuts with ease, and has an ultra smooth sliding action. The work table has 22.5 reference marks to assure your Dados bevel and mitre cuts match perfectly up to ±45. The adjustable depth limiter allows the proper mitre, bevel, or groove for complex compound cutting. The Baumr-AG even has a built-in laser guide for absolute precision!

The Baumr-AG DBR-255 cast aluminium base, extendable work table supports and securing clamp make it simple and safe to keep your workpiece secure and in place. The DBR-255 drop saw comes complete with a sawdust collection bag, and gives you the option of attaching the Baumr-AG BM-DD5, a vacuum for the ultimate sawdust extraction and collection.

You can mount the DBR-255 on a Baumr AG custom designed purpose built mitre saw stand or secure it to your bench. The DBR-255 also has a quick release table, the blade and guard lock in place, making it safe and easy to transport to your worksite.

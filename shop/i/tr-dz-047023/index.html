Details

This unique writing instrument features a hinged mechanical design that allows it to change shape when pressed. The pen is engineered for both functionality and interactive use, combining practical writing performance with tactile engagement.

Key Features

  • Transformable Mechanism: Pressing a section of the body activates a folding hinge system, causing the pen to shift from a compact form to an extended configuration. This motion can be repeated multiple times.
  • Ballpoint Tip: Equipped with a standard ballpoint tip measuring 0.5 mm in diameter, suitable for smooth writing on paper.
  • Compact Design: When closed, the pen measures 85 mm in length, making it easy to carry in pockets or bags. When fully opened, it extends to 140 mm.
  • ABS Material Construction: Made from durable ABS plastic, providing lightweight strength and resistance to deformation during daily use.
  • Integrated Clip: A small clip is attached near the top of the pen for secure attachment to notebooks, folders, or clothing.
  • Smooth Operation: The internal mechanism is designed for consistent movement with minimal effort required to activate the transformation.

How It Works

  1. Hold the pen in one hand and press the designated area near the center.
  2. The internal hinges will unfold, extending the barrel and revealing the full writing length.
  3. Release pressure to return to the original compact shape.
  4. Use the pen normally by pressing the tip to dispense ink.

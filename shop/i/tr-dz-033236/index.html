CARLA HOME Dog Scratch Pad - Nail File Board with Treat Drawer - Natural - Cat Scratcher

Nail trimming is one of the most stressful routines for dogs and their owners. Many dogs experience anxiety, panic or fear when they see clippers or hear a grinder — and forcing the process often makes it worse over time.

This item stands out for the following characteristics:

  • STRESS-FREE NAIL FILING: A calmer alternative to clippers and grinders: your dog scratches the sandpaper surface to naturally file their own nails while treats in the built-in drawer keep them engaged and reward good behaviour throughout the session.
  • WOODEN BUILD WITH: SANDPAPER SURFACE – Constructed from a natural wood frame with a durable sandpaper scratch panel measuring 40 x 29 cm. The abrasive surface files nails gradually during use, with no pressure claw sharpening required.
  • 3 ADJUSTABLE HEIGHTS: The hinged wooden stand locks into 3 height positions (28 cm, 31.5 cm, 35 cm) to suit dogs and cats of different sizes. Can also be laid flat for large dogs who prefer a ground-level scratch board.

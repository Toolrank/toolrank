Adjustable 2-Person Lifting Moving Strap System — Move Heavy Items with Ease

Move furniture, appliances, and bulky household items safely and efficiently with our fully adjustable 2-person shoulder harness and lifting strap set. Engineered for two carriers to share the load evenly, this system turns difficult moves — especially up and down stairs — into a manageable two-person job. The freely adjustable strap length adapts to different body heights and item sizes, while the 5 cm wide heavy-duty webbing supports loads up to 800 lbs.

  • Fully Adjustable Design: Freely adjustable shoulder harness and auxiliary straps accommodate different body sizes, item dimensions, and stair angles — no fixed sizing limitations.

Baumr-AG 160L Petrol Concrete Mixer

BUILT TO MIX

The Baumr-AG CPS-160 is a 160L Petrol Concrete Mixer made for tradies, small operators, rural property owners and serious DIY users who need petrol powered mixing without relying on mains power. It is a practical choice for concrete, cement mortar, plaster, resin and asphalt jobs around worksites, sheds, farms and larger properties.

PETROL POWER

The 105cc 3.0HP 4-stroke single cylinder engine delivers around 2250W of power for regular mixing tasks. Recoil start, manual choke and manual throttle keep the setup simple, while forced air cooling with thermal cut-off helps protect the engine during demanding work.

TOUGH BUILD

A heavy duty tubular steel frame supports up to 140kg, giving the CPS-160 the strength needed for smaller commercial style jobs and serious property maintenance. The steel drum has a 1.2mm upper section and 1.8mm lower section, helping it handle common mixing loads where the heavier material settles.

CONTROLLED POURING

The GripLock foot-controlled tilt and lock system helps you manage drum position while keeping both hands free for controlled pouring. With geared manual tilt and a steel tip lock mechanism, it is designed to make loading, mixing and emptying more manageable under load.

WORK READY

The 390mm drum mouth and 630mm drum diameter make loading materials straightforward, while two steel agitator blades help produce consistent mixes. Cast iron drive and drum gears, a dry centrifugal clutch and gear drive transmission support reliable power transfer to the drum.

Magic Laundry Balls Set of 4 - Anti-Pilling Fuzzy Lint Remover for Washing Machine

Upgrade your laundry routine with this set of 4 magic laundry balls, designed to reduce fabric pilling, prevent clothes from tangling, and absorb lint and hair during washing. Made from durable PP plastic and polyurethane foam, each ball measures 7cm x 6.5cm and weighs approximately 50g. These balls are compatible with all types of washing machines, including front-load and top-load models.

Features: The unique design includes a central PP body with petal-like ridges that help break up detergent clumps and loosen fabrics to prevent entanglement. The outer layer is made of soft black polyurethane foam that gently removes lint, hair, and fibers from clothes without damaging the fabric.

Usage: Simply place 2–4 balls into the drum before starting the wash cycle. They work effectively in every wash, helping to keep clothes fluffy, clean, and free from static and tangles. After use, rinse under water to remove collected debris and reuse multiple times.

Material: PP + Polyurethane foam. Non-toxic, safe for all fabrics, and environmentally friendly.

Benefits: Reduces need for lint rollers, saves time on post-wash cleaning, and helps extend the life of your garments by minimizing friction and fiber damage.

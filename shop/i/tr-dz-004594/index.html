Addresses Messy Cage Liner Problems

Solves the common hassle of frequent deep cleaning for small pet enclosures. It catches spilled food, scattered bedding and accidental leaks that soak through to cage floors or your home surfaces. No more scrubbing sticky stains or replacing disposable liners every single day, cutting down on daily pet care work and extra waste.

Premium Quilted Fabric Surface With Finished Binding. The top layer features a uniform grey color, with a structured quilted pattern of connected rounded hexagons stitched evenly across the entire surface. The stitching creates a subtle padded texture that is soft to the touch, while remaining durable enough to resist scratching from small pet claws. Neat black binding wraps around all four edges of the mat, preventing fraying and giving the product a clean, well-finished look. Multi Layer Structure Designed For Practical Small Pet Use. The quilted top layer is soft and comfortable for small pets to walk, rest and play on. The layered construction locks in moisture to keep it contained within the mat, stopping leaks from reaching your cage base or underlying floor. The rectangular shape fits standard large small pet enclosures easily, with plenty of coverage for most common cage sizes.

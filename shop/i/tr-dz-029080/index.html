Right Angle Clamp for Woodworking Corner Joints, Square Clamp Tool with Adjustable Jaw for Frame Cabinet Furniture Assembly and DIY Wood Box Projects

Product Description

Achieving a flawless ninety-degree corner is the moment every woodworker chases, and this right angle clamp turns that chase into a routine. Built around a square body of hard engineering plastic reinforced with metal hardware, a spring mechanism, an included hex wrench and a black adjustment knob, this tool holds two workpieces firmly at a true right angle while glue dries or screws drive home. The movable jaw slides and locks to grip stock from 0.07 inch up to 1.3 inch thick, and molded scale marks for 1/4, 1/2 and 3/4 inch thicknesses let you set the jaw by eye instead of hunting for a ruler. A diagonal span of 10 cm / 3.9 inches keeps the body compact enough for small assemblies yet stable enough for larger panels. Use it to reinforce picture frames, assemble birdhouses, build cabinets, install furniture or join wooden boxes where a skewed corner would ruin the whole piece. The spring-loaded jaw opens quickly, seats on the workpiece and clamps down with a few turns of the knob, so you spend your time building rather than fiddling. Because a square joint is the cornerstone of good craftsmanship, this clamp belongs within arm's reach on every bench. Choose from red, orange or blue to match your shop or your mood, and keep several on hand for multi-corner glue-ups. Whether you are a weekend hobbyist or a seasoned cabinetmaker, this corner clamp delivers the repeatable accuracy that separates a project that looks handmade from one that looks made badly.

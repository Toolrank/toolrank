Details

The PolyCool PY-EC7 Evaporative Cooler is the smart cooling solution for commercial premises. The PY-EC3 is far more energy efficient than traditional air conditioners, and requires no window ventilation ducting. This 4-in-1 unit boasts air cooling, fan, purifier and humidifier functions all rolled into oneproducing healthy cool air.

The PolyCool has a powerful 220 watt motor and axial fan that produces a massive flow rate of up to 10,000m/h, which is ideal for rapidly cooling areas as large as 40m. With a huge 35L water tank, the PY-EC7 Evaporative Cooler can deliver up to 15 hours of cool air before the water level window indicates a refill is required! It comes complete with two Bonus ice cooling packs which you can add for extra chilled air output.

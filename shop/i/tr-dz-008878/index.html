Product Description

This compact PTFE tube cutter is designed for precise, clean, and square cuts on soft tubing, making pipe cutting faster and easier for plumbing installation and repair. Its self-supporting structure, replaceable stainless steel blade, and comfortable grip deliver safe, reliable performance whether you are a professional installer or a home DIY user.

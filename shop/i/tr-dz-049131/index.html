Product Overview

This portable car recovery traction board is a versatile emergency tool designed to help vehicles regain grip in snow, mud, sand, and slippery conditions. Functioning as both an anti-slip mat and a manual snow shovel, it features a unique ribbed design that bites into soft terrain to prevent tire spinning. Ideal for Australian drivers navigating winter alpine roads, beach tracks, or muddy outback paths, this compact rescue ramp offers a lightweight and practical solution for self-recovery when you are stuck.

Social Hike A3 LED Light Pad is a fully portable and rechargeable tracing board designed for drawing, sketching, tracing and craft projects. Unlike basic light pads that require constant power, this model features a built-in battery for cordless use, making it easier to work comfortably at home, in the studio or on the go.

  • Slim with Folding Stand: Approx. 11 mm thin design with rear stand for a more comfortable working angle.

Product Description

Upgrade fireplace heat circulation with this 6-blade heat powered stove fan, designed to operate without batteries or electricity. It converts stove or fireplace heat into energy to help distribute warm air faster and more evenly, improving comfort in living rooms, cabins, and home heating spaces. With quiet performance, simple placement, and a recommended operating temperature range of 50°C/122°F to 350°C/662°F, it is a practical and energy-saving accessory for wood stoves and fireplaces.

Key Features

  • Heat Powered Operation: Runs without batteries or electrical power by converting heat from the stove or fireplace into energy.
  • 6-Blade Efficient Airflow: The 6-blade design helps circulate warm air more quickly and efficiently for better room heating.
  • Temperature Responsive: Blades spin faster as the stove temperature rises, increasing airflow output naturally.
  • Quiet Performance: Special blade design helps keep operation quiet, making it suitable for daily home use.
  • Easy to Use: No installation required; simply place it on a smooth, flat stove surface near the side or back of the stove pipe.
  • Wide Working Range: Recommended surface temperature range is 50°C/122°F to 350°C/662°F.

PrecautionsFor Use
1. After receiving the product, please let ice maker stand for 2 hours before use. Vibrations generated during transportation can affect the performance of the product.
2. After standing for two hours, you need to wash it with clean water. The drain at the bottom can be opened.
3. The first 1-3 times of using the ice maker, the ice produced will be thin, which is a normal phenomenon. Because the temperature inside the ice machine is still high at the beginning, it will affect the thickness of the ice, and the refrigeration system inside is not stable. After three rounds of ice making, the ice can be produced normally.

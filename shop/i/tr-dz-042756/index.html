Blue Deep-Water Pool Skimming Net - Durable Cleaning Tool for Pool Maintenance

Keep your pool crystal clear with the Blue Deep-Water Pool Skimming Net, a robust and efficient tool designed for removing debris. Crafted with a durable frame and a fine mesh net, this skimmer effectively captures leaves, insects, and other surface particles. Its ergonomic design and sturdy construction make it an essential accessory for maintaining a clean and safe swimming environment.

Key Features

  • Deep-Water Design: Ideal for skimming debris from the surface and deeper areas of the pool.
  • Durable Construction: Made with a strong frame and fine mesh net for long-lasting use.
  • Easy to Use: Features a 28mm diameter handle attachment point for connecting to a separate pole (not included).
  • Efficient Cleaning: Quickly removes leaves, insects, and other debris to keep water clean.

How to Use

Attach the skimming net to a compatible pole (sold separately) via the 28mm diameter handle attachment. Dip the net into the pool water and skim the surface to collect debris. Lift the net out carefully to dispose of the collected leaves, insects, and other particles. Rinse the net with water after use to maintain its condition.

Care Instructions

Rinse the net with fresh water after each use to remove chlorine and debris. Allow it to air dry to prevent mold or mildew. Store in a dry place when not in use to prolong its lifespan. Avoid exposing the net to extreme heat or sharp objects that may damage the mesh.

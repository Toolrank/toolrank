3D Holographic Privacy Window Film Static Glass Sticker

Product Description

This holographic window film transforms clear glass into a decorative, iridescent privacy surface with a stained-glass style pattern. The colourful 3D effect reflects light beautifully and adds a stylish look to windows, doors and glass partitions. It helps obscure the view into a room so you can enjoy privacy without blocking natural daylight. Made from durable PVC material, the film is flexible, easy to cut to your own shapes and suitable for long-term indoor use. It suits a wide range of spaces including bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, balconies and commercial premises. The film can be removed when you want to change the look, making it a flexible update for rented homes and offices.

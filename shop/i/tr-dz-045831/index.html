Bring Peace and Quiet to Your Home

Say goodbye to the startling sound of slamming doors with this adorable set of six crab-shaped door silencers. Perfectly designed for nurseries, bedrooms, and any room in the house, these soft silicone guards create a gentle buffer that prevents doors from fully latching or slamming shut, protecting little fingers and giving you peace of mind.

Key Features:

  • Noise-Reducing Buffer: Prevents doors from slamming shut due to drafts or haste, eliminating loud noises and stopping doors from rattling in their frames.
  • Child & Pet Safety: Creates a gap that stops the door from closing completely, preventing children and pets from getting their fingers pinched or accidentally locking themselves in a room.
  • Adjustable Thickness for a Perfect Fit: Features a '2+1' design with a 2mm body and a removable 1mm shim, allowing you to customize the total thickness to 3mm to suit various door gap widths.
  • Easy to Install: The flexible strap design wraps securely around both knob and lever-style door handles in seconds. No tools or adhesives are needed.
  • Durable Silicone Material: Made from high-quality, soft silicone that is built to last and will not scratch or damage your doors or door frames.

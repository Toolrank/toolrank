Product Description

This washable and reusable sanding sponge set covers every stage of your project, from rough shaping to fine polishing. The flexible foam block design conforms to flat, curved, and irregular surfaces, delivering even sanding results on wood, drywall, metal, and more—comfortable to hold and ready to use right out of the package.

Key Features

  • Multi-Grit Assortment: Includes a range of grit sizes (80, 120, 180, 240) for complete multi-stage sanding—remove old paint, smooth surfaces, prep for painting, and polish to a fine finish with one set.
  • Flexible Sponge Block Design: The foam core bends around curved and contoured surfaces like chair legs, dowels, and drywall corners that flat sandpaper cannot reach, while staying firm enough for even control on flat areas.
  • Washable & Reusable: Simply rinse with water to remove dust and debris, let dry, and reuse—extending lifespan up to 3-5 times longer than disposable sandpaper and reducing waste.
  • Multi-Material Versatility: Works on wood furniture, drywall joint compound, rusty metal tools, and even minor scratches on glasses or plastic with the extra-fine grit.
  • Ergonomic & Comfortable Grip: The cushioned foam shape fits naturally in your hand, absorbs vibration, and reduces hand and wrist fatigue during long sanding sessions—no extra backing block needed.

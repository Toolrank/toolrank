Details

Palo Santo (bursera graveolens) literally means holy wood in Spanish. OrkuPalo Santo sticks are a premium grade product, wild harvested and sustainably gathered from the forest floorsof Ecuador.

It has an exquisite aroma with a hint of pine, mint and lemon. Palo Santo is enjoyed by many for its energetically cleansing and healing properties.

It creates a pleasant, fresh smoke that also works well in keeping away mosquitoes and other flying insects which is one of the prime uses for the people who live in such areas.

Specifications:

  • Pack Size: 50g - Approximately 8-10 Sticks
  • Stick Size: Approximately 9-11cm in length
  • Country of Origin: Ecuador

Instructions:

  • Ignite your Palo Santo stick allowing to burn for 30 - 60 seconds then blow out.
  • Move amongst rooms gently waving the stick in the air.
  • When finished place in fire proof bowl - the glow will end on its own, unless you continue to blow, producing smoke.

Package Includes:

Palo Santo Thin Sticks.

WARNING: Take care whilst smudging and only use under adult supervision. Extra care should be taken around flammable materials. Ensure the stick is extinguished completely before leaving unattended.

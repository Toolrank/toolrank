Product Description

Still struggling to remove stubborn stains and bacteria from your braces, retainers, clear aligners, or dentures? This ultrasonic UV cleaner combines 43,000Hz high-frequency ultrasonic waves with a built-in UV-C lamp to deep clean and sanitize your dental appliances in just 3 minutes—no harsh chemicals needed, protecting both your appliance and your oral health.

Key Features

  • 43000Hz Ultrasonic Deep Cleaning: High-frequency vibrations create dense microbubbles in water that burst to physically lift food debris, plaque, and stains from every crevice—no blind spots left behind.

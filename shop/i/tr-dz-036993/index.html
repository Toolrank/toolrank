Simplecom CHT660 Adjustable Aluminium Laptop Stand with 6-Port USB-C Docking Station Dual 4K HDMI Display

CHT660is an adjustable aluminium laptop stand with built-in 6 ports multifunctional USB-C docking station. It's not only a laptop stand, but also a docking station that allows you to elevate your laptop and enhance its functionality through the USB-C port. It turns a single USB-C port into 6 ports, including dual 4K HDMI outputs, two SuperSpeed USB-A data ports, Gigabit Ethernet, and 100W PD charging.

Durable and Foldable Aluminium Alloy Stand

The stand is made of high-quality aluminium alloy, which makes it easy to dissipate heat while the laptop is working. The soft rubber pads on the top protect your laptop from sliding and scratches. The foldable design makes it easy to store and carry, opening and folding up in seconds, which saves space when not in use. Compatible with laptops from 10 to 17 inches.

Adjustable Stand with Ergonomic Design

The laptop stand is designed with adjustable in height and angle, you can raise your laptop screen to a more ergonomic viewing height, which is better for maintaining a healthy posture. It also features 360 rotatable base, allowing you to easily swivel your laptop screen to any direction.

Built-in 6 Ports USB-C Docking Station
Built-in 6 ports multifunctional USB-C docking station for laptop, including dual 4K HDMI outputs, two SuperSpeed USB-A data ports, Gigabit Ethernet, and 100W PD charging.

Dual HDMI Video Outputs with 4K Support
The USB-C docking station has dual HDMI outputs, which allows you to connect 2 extended screens simultaneously for Windows system. Connecting single HDMI supports up to 4K@60Hz and dual HDMI supports up to 4K@30Hz on each external monitor.

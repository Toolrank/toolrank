Product Description

This compact CO2 meter combines real-time carbon dioxide monitoring, temperature, and humidity detection in a lightweight, portable design. Built with an NDIR infrared CO2 sensor and optical sensor, it delivers sensitive, accurate readings for indoor air quality management at home, in offices, greenhouses, cars, and more. The LED display, Wi-Fi app connectivity, and smart alarm system make it easy to track air conditions and respond quickly when ventilation is needed.

Key Features

  • Accurate 3-in-1 monitoring: Measures CO2 concentration, temperature, and humidity in real time for reliable indoor air quality awareness.
  • High-sensitivity sensor system: NDIR infrared carbon dioxide sensor with direct air contact and ventilated 3D porous housing supports fast, precise particle and gas detection.
  • Smart LED display: Ultra-wide viewing angle with automatic brightness adjustment helps you read data clearly in different lighting conditions.
  • Energy-saving convenience: Auto screen-off reduces power consumption, while hand-wave wake-up offers quick access to readings.
  • Sound and color alerts: Audible alarm activates at 1500 ppm, with color-coded indicators for fast air quality status recognition.
  • Wi-Fi app connectivity: View real-time data remotely through the mobile app, even when the screen is off, for convenient home and workplace monitoring.

Simplecom CHT560 USB-C SuperSpeed 6-in-1 Multiport Docking Station HDMI 2.0

CHT560 is a 6-in-1 USB-C multiport docking station that enhances your laptop's connectivity through its USB-C port. It transforms a single USB-C port into six ports, including three SuperSpeed USB-A ports, an HDMI 2.0 port, a Gigabit Ethernet port, and a 100W PD charging port.

If you need to connect various devices to your laptop simultaneously, this docking station is a comprehensive connectivity solution that boosts productivity and convenience.

6-in-1 USB-C Docking Station
A portable, all-in-one USB-C docking station featuring three SuperSpeed USB-A ports, an HDMI 2.0 port, a Gigabit Ethernet port, and a 100W PD charging port.

HDMI 2.0 Video Output, up to 4K@60Hz
The HDMI 2.0 port supports 4K video output at 60 frames per second. Compatible devices with DP 1.4 support can output video at up to 4K@60Hz, ensuring smooth and fluid playback, especially for fast-paced content or action movies.

SuperSpeed USB Hub
Three USB-A ports with a maximum transfer speed of up to 5Gbps. You can connect USB hard drives, keyboards, mice, flash drives, or other USB devices.

100W USB-C Power Delivery
The USB-C PD port can charge your connected laptop while the other ports are in use. It supports PD 3.0 and delivers up to 100W of Power Delivery charging.

Gigabit Ethernet
Connect to the internet instantly and enjoy stable speeds of up to 1000Mbps. This helps reduce latency and interference, ensuring a more consistent and reliable experience for streaming or downloading.

Indoor Pipe Cutter – Precision for Hard-to-Reach Pipes

Replacing old pipes no longer means damaging your floors or struggling with limited access. Our Indoor Pipe Cutter is engineered for 'reverse-outward' flush cutting, making it ideal for installing shower drain pipes, toilet flanges, and pipes embedded in concrete slabs or wall panels that standard cutters cannot reach.

The complete kit includes 6 high-speed steel (HSS) blades in sizes 22, 25, 32, 35, 44, and 50 mm, plus a 3.15-inch guide disc that ensures wide compatibility for both internal and external cutting tasks. Whether you work with narrow household PVC pipes or larger ABS industrial pipes, this tool adapts effortlessly.

Straight and professional-grade cuts are guaranteed every time. The extended shaft features double set screws to lock the guide disc, allowing adjustable cutting depth of up to 3 inches. High-quality built-in bearings eliminate wobble and maintain perfect blade alignment—even at high speeds.

Built for both professionals and DIY enthusiasts, this pipe cutter offers industrial-grade durability. The main shaft is crafted from hardened stainless steel to resist rust and corrosion, while the HSS blades stay sharp after repeated use on PVC, ABS, and soft metal pipes. It is a long-term, reliable addition to your toolbox.

Installation and use are straightforward: just attach the saw blade on the right side, insert into a 3/8-inch drill chuck, and run clockwise at low speed. No complicated setup is required.

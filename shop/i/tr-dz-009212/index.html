Product Description

This 6-piece rolled hem presser foot set makes fabric hemming faster and easier by rolling the fabric edge and stitching in one smooth pass. Designed for most low shank snap-on household sewing machines, it delivers clean, professional-looking hems on skirts, dresses, curtains, and everyday sewing projects.

Key Features

  • 6 Versatile Sizes: Includes 3 wide rolled hem feet (13mm, 19mm, 25mm) and 3 narrow rolled hem feet (3mm, 4mm, 6mm) to handle everything from delicate edges to bold wide hems.
  • Roll & Stitch in One Step: The built-in curl smoothly folds the fabric edge while the needle hem-stitches, saving you time on every project.
  • Excellent Fabric Control: The roll shape grips fabric firmly, keeping hems aligned without constant guiding or frustration.
  • Adjustable Positioning: A convenient screw lets you adjust the foot position to match your desired hem placement.
  • Built to Last: Made of high-quality metal that is sturdy, rust-resistant, and maintains a bright finish through repeated use.
  • Handles Multiple Fabrics: Works well on both lightweight and thicker fabrics, ideal for cotton skirts, home textiles, and more.

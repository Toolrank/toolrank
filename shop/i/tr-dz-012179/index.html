Diatomite Absorbent Bathroom Sink Organiser Tray

Product Description

This oval bathroom tray is made from diatomite with solid wood feet, giving a matte, natural look that suits modern vanities. The porous surface absorbs water droplets from soap, cups and skincare bottles, helping keep the sink area dry and tidy. Raised wooden feet lift the tray slightly so air can circulate underneath and the base stays stable on the counter. It holds daily personal care items such as toothbrushes, hand soap and small bottles together in one compact spot. The slim shape fits narrow sink edges without crowding the basin, and the smooth surface rinses clean with a quick wipe. A practical addition to bathrooms, laundry areas and shared wash spaces.

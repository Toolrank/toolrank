Details

Solves drowning risks for small pollinators. Many open shallow water sources leave bees and other small garden pollinators vulnerable to drowning, when they come to drink or collect water. This floating watering tray creates a safe, stable landing space that keeps small pollinators above the water line, so they can hydrate without slipping into deep water.

Bright yellow smooth EVA construction. The entire tray is a uniform bright yellow, which is easily visible to flying pollinators from above to help them locate water quickly. It is crafted from solid lightweight EVA material, with a matte smooth surface that is soft yet sturdy, without sharp edges or burrs. All holes are cut cleanly with consistent size and even spacing, and the outer edge of the round tray is smoothly finished to maintain a neat, premium appearance. The material holds its shape well, and the bright hue resists fading from prolonged sun exposure for a long lasting clean look.

Floating ergonomic design for stable access. The tray features a perfectly round shape that matches the diameter of most standard garden bird baths, it fits neatly into most existing water basins without awkward overhang or gaps. Two distinct hole patterns are available, both with openings sized to give small pollinators plenty of space to stand and access water without getting fully submerged. The lightweight EVA material allows the tray to float naturally on top of the water, so it always stays at water level no matter how much water is in the basin. The flat top surface gives pollinators a stable non slip area to rest and drink, which aligns with the natural landing posture of small bees and other garden insects.

Usage

Best for home renovation, rentals, dorm rooms, and quick seasonal refreshes: ensure the surface is smooth and clean, peel the backing, apply slowly, then use a plastic card (e.g., a credit card) to push out air bubbles from the center outward. Apply once for best alignment; repeated repositioning may deform the sticker. Not suitable for uncoated, rough, or textured surfaces.

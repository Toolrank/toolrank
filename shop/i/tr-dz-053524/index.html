This foldable pet grooming hammock stand provides a practical raised support platform for cats and dogs during bathing, nail trimming, grooming, drying and routine care. The quick-dry polyester mesh surface features 8 positioning openings, while the painted iron frame provides freestanding support without requiring attachment to a table or other furniture.

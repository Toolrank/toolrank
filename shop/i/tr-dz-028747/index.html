Product Description

Take your water sessions to the next level with this inflatable wing foil, engineered for riders who want dependable power and smooth handling across surfing and hydrofoil boarding. The wing combines a 30D ripstop high-strength polyester inner layer with a 150D*300D striped Oxford high-strength polyester outer shell, delivering a strong strength-to-weight ratio while keeping the packed weight at just 3.2kg. The arc-shaped canopy catches wind efficiently and holds its profile, so you can pump up onto the foil, glide through lulls, and stay in control during turns.

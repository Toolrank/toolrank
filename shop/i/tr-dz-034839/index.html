Details

Introducing the Unimac ACM-1000s Quiet Air series Triple-Double Air Compressor! This is a trade quality 6-Cylinder unit ideal for anyone looking for the performance and reliability that only an oilless electric air compressor can provide.

INDEPENDENT MOTOR CONTROL

The ACM-1000s has 3 twin-cylinder motors with Independent Motor Start. Avoid power surges and accidental overloads. IMC allows you to start one motor at a time for efficiency and safety.

ULTRA-QUIET

This Unimac Quiet Air Series is engineered to be just that. Whisper Vortex cooling fans, Hush Baffle air intakes, and vulcanised rubber motor mounts and rubber tyres combine to give this an Lpa of only 65 dBa — that's nearly library quiet!

CLASS-LEADING POWER

This ACM-1000 Oil-Free Air Compressor is powered by 3 twin-cylinder 1.1kw motors which is equivalent to a total output of 4.5HP— up to 40% more than other models on the market! The cylinders can be operated independently to deliver the air you need, so you can run it as quietly as possible and optimise compressor life.

MASSIVE 100L TANK

With its 100L tank, it is capable of delivering a continuous 300 litres per minute air supply for a huge variety of applications.

SERVICE-FREE DESIGN

Unlike petrol and other belt-driven models on the market, with this oil-free electric model you don't have to worry about engine maintenance or replacing belts or pulleys.

TWO NITTO OUTLETS

With two quick-connect outlets you can use a wide array of tools including grinders, impact wrenches, welders, nail guns, grease guns, chisels and more. It also has automatic on-off pressure control.

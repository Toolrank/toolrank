Details

The best spot for a bathroom is sometimes the worst spot for plumbing, or gravity. But thanks to the Proteges wide range of macerator pumps this isnt a problem. You can save a significant amount of expense by connecting a small diameter pipe to an existing system; making it ideal for adding a bathroom or en-suite to basements or FIFO mining camps.

The heavy-duty Protege Washlink 4 Commercial is perfect for public or commercial environments. It is designed for p-trap toilets and can connect with up to 4 sanitary fixtures, such as a basin, shower, bath, bidet or washing machine.

The Washlink 4 Commercial runs smoothly, thanks to its powerful 800W copper wound motor and rubber feet. Specially moulded non-clog stainless-steel impeller blades make short work of all waste, and can even cut through materials such as condoms, paper towels, and female sanitary products. It is capable of pumping up to nine metres vertically and 90 metres horizontally.

Connecting the Protege Macerator Pump is straightforward with quality instructions and all necessary fittings included. And, best of all, once in place, you can forget about it. Not only is it tough and powerful; it's automatically activated, too.

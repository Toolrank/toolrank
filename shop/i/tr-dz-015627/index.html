Product Description

Keep your windshield clean, clear, and ready to drive in every season with this 4-layer car windshield cover. Built with PEVA aluminum foil + cotton layers + soft non-woven lining, it helps block UV heat in summer, resists extreme cold in winter, and prevents leaves, dust, frost, snow, and ice buildup—so you spend less time scraping and more time on the road.

Key Features

  • 4-layer thick protection: PEVA aluminum foil, spun cotton, composite cotton, and soft non-woven lining for durable, water-resistant coverage that helps keep glass cleaner.
  • Summer UV & heat shield: Helps block up to 99% UV rays and reduces heat buildup on hot sunny days for a more comfortable cabin.
  • Winter frost/snow/ice defense: Cold resistant down to -50°C; reduces morning scraping time and helps prevent frozen wiper areas.

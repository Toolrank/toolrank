Details

Say Goodbye to Muddy Paws in Seconds!

Tired of muddy paw prints all over your floors and furniture? Our innovative Semi-Automatic Paw Cleaner is the perfect solution. This easy-to-use cup provides a quick, deep, and gentle clean for your dog's paws after walks, hikes, or backyard playtime, keeping your home spotless.

Key Features:

  • Effortless 360-degree Cleaning: Simply press the handle, and the internal brush automatically rotates 360 degrees. This provides a fast, comprehensive clean that reaches every nook and cranny of the paw without any manual twisting.
  • Gentle Silicone Bristles: The cup is lined with soft, skin-friendly silicone bristles that effectively remove dirt, mud, and sand while providing a gentle massage for your pet's paw.
  • Deep & Thorough Clean: The dual-action design features bristles on the sides and base, ensuring it cleans deep between the pads and around the nails for a complete wash.
  • Easy to Disassemble & Clean: The entire unit can be taken apart, allowing you to thoroughly rinse the cup and the silicone insert after each use for maximum hygiene.
  • Simple Operation: Just add a little water, insert your dog's paw, press the handle a few times, then remove and dry. It's that easy!

How to Use:

  1. Add a small amount of water to the paw cleaner cup.
  2. Gently guide your dog's paw into the cup.
  3. Press the handle repeatedly to activate the automatic rotation.
  4. Carefully remove the paw and dry it with a towel.
  5. Pour out the dirty water, disassemble, and rinse for next time.

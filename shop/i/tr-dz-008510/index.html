Product Description

Upgrade your 3D printer's filament feeding system with this PC4-M10 male straight pneumatic connector, designed for long-distance extruder setups. Made of durable stainless steel with anti-rust performance, this push-to-connect fitting ensures smooth filament feeding, resists clogging, and locks your PTFE tube securely in place for stable, worry-free printing.

Key Features

  • Rust-Resistant Build: Made of stainless steel that resists oxidation and rust, extending the connector's service life even with long-term use.
  • Push-to-Connect Design: Simply insert the 4mm tube and it locks automatically, while pressing the black cap releases the tube instantly for quick maintenance and replacement.
  • Clog Prevention: Precision structure effectively reduces clogging during printing, helping deliver smoother and more consistent print results.
  • Heat Resistant: Supports a maximum operating temperature of up to 280°C, suitable for high-temperature printing environments.
  • Secure Threading: M10 male thread (about 9.6mm) offers a tight, stable connection ideal for long-distance extruder configurations.
  • Value Pack: Includes 10 connectors, giving you reliable spares for multiple printers or future replacements.

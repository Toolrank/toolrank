Victrola Automatic Turntable - Black – Let the Music Play. Experience the magic of vinyl with the Victrola Automatic – a turntable designed for effortless listening at the push of a button.

Victrola’s first fully automatic turntable makes spinning records easier than ever. Just pop your favourite vinyl on the platter, press play, and let the Automatic do the rest.

With a user-friendly layout, it boasts unique features like a first-of-its-kind Repeat function, so you can keep the tunes rolling without lifting a finger – perfect for parties, background music, or simply getting lost in your favourite album.

Smart Design, Superior Sound
The Automatic sports a sleek, modern look with a removable dust cover to keep your records in top shape. Whether you’re streaming wirelessly via VINYLSTREAM Bluetooth or connecting directly with the RCA output, you’ll enjoy crisp, high-quality sound.

Features:

- Fully Automatic Playback – One-touch operation for a fuss-free vinyl experience
- Repeat Function – Keeps the music going by replaying the side of a record
- VINYL STREAM Bluetooth Output – Wirelessly stream to Bluetooth speakers or headphones
- Premium Audio Quality – Audio Technica ATN3600L cartridge delivers rich, detailed sound
- Modern Versatile – Stylish, minimalist design with a removable dust cover for protection
- Listen Your Way – Built-in switchable phono pre-amp and RCA output for easy connection to speakers

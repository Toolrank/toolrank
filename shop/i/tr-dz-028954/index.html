7-in-1 Painter's Support Stand Tool for Doors Cabinets Trim and Small Painting Projects, Heavy Duty Stackable Painting Rack for Brushes Rollers and Sprayers

Product Description

Painting a door, cabinet panel, or piece of decorative trim can quickly turn into a balancing act. This 7-in-1 painter's support stand is designed to make that job easier, safer, and far more organized. Made from heavy-duty ABS plastic in a bright blue finish, the triangular base with a recessed underside provides stable support while lifting your workpiece off the ground. The angled body includes both square and circular cutouts, giving you multiple ways to position doors, panels, and trim for painting, drying, or hardware installation. Use it as a sturdy prop when brushing, rolling, or spraying, then flip the workpiece to finish the other side without waiting for a full dry. Multiple stands can be stacked to hold several painted pieces at once, saving valuable floor space and keeping your work area tidy. The design also helps when installing hinges and door handles, and the cutouts can hold round-handled painting tools within easy reach. Whether you are refreshing interior doors, repainting cabinet fronts, or touching up trim, this support tool keeps your project steady and your workflow smooth. A stitch in time saves nine — and a good support stand saves you from drips, smudges, and do-overs. Lightweight yet strong, it is easy to move around the workshop, garage, or job site. When the project is done, the compact units stack together for simple storage until the next paint day.

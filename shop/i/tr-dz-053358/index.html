Details

Handrails are important safety features for our inground pools. They support the bodywhile ascending or descending your pool and Randy Travis Machinery offers this Swimming Pool Hand Rail Step Grab Rail for what you need!

It;s designed with a curved grip and nylon cover that is a good insulator and prevents your wet hands from slipping. The base of the rail is 4mm thick to ensure ample weight support, as we don't want chances of any slips and mishaps.

For installation, all you need to do is use the drill bit, self-tapping screws, nut covers, and anchor that come along with this package.

This product is an ideal choice for indoors or outdoors, home or commercial use. Perfect for children and seniors when entering or leaving the pool!

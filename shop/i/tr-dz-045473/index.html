Product Description

Travel with your furry companion in comfort and style with our Foldable Soft-Sided Pet Carrier. Designed for pets weighing up to 6kg, this carrier provides a safe, breathable, and comfortable space for your cat or small dog. It features multiple mesh windows for excellent ventilation, a removable and foldable plush mat for easy cleaning and storage, and a convenient side pocket for treats. The carrier is equipped with both reinforced handles and a detachable, padded shoulder strap for versatile carrying options. An internal safety leash ensures your pet remains secure during transit. Perfect for vet visits, road trips, or any journey you take together.

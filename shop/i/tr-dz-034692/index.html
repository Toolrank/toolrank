Details

The PlantCraft TSA36 Tow Behind Roller Spike Aerator is an exceptional piece of garden equipment. This reliable time-saving machine is made from high-strength steel with a rust-resistant powder coat finish. The TSA36 lawn aerator roller has a spike depth of 2.5” which improves a lawn’s water and fertiliser absorption at the root layer. It has 78 steel spikes staggered among 12 rows. You can get the job done quickly too, as it's rated to be towed at speeds of up to 8km/h.

The PlantCraft Lawn Core Aerator comes equipped with a universal hitch pin for hassle-free attachment to ride-on mowers, garden tractors, and ATVs. This tow-behind aerator can be weighted with up to 91 litres of water for use on harder soil and clay.

Regular use of the PlantCraft tow-behind aerator improves water and nutrient absorption, increases oxygen levels and reduces thatch. The TSA36 makes not only great-looking lawns but saves time and money in the long run!

Application Scenarios

Ideal for a wide range of high-heat and daily tasks: welding, BBQ and grilling, fireplace and wood stove, oven and baking, garden burning, kitchen cooking, animal handling protection. Usage tips: Inspect the gloves for damage before use and make sure they are completely dry before touching hot objects, as moisture reduces insulation. Allow them to cool and air dry naturally in a well-ventilated area after use, and wipe clean with a damp cloth—never soak or machine wash to protect the insulating layer. Avoid prolonged contact with glowing charcoal and do not use against molten metal.

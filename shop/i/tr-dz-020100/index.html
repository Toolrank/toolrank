Product Description:
This 2-in-1 Instant Iced Coffee Cup is made from baby-safe materials and comes with a dedicated ice tray that allows you to release ice with a single press—no need to touch the ice cubes. Simply swap out the lid to instantly transform it into a on-the-go drinking cup, perfect for making homemade iced coffee, cold-brewed tea, and other summer beverages. It’s convenient and quick to use at home.

Product Details:
The ice tray features a soft rubber design; simply press the designated spot to release the ice with a single click, and the ice cubes drop directly into the cup.
It comes with two lids: the ice-release lid is used for making and releasing ice, while the drinking lid features a silicone seal—simply open it to enjoy your drink.
The cup body is equipped with a non-slip silicone insulated sleeve to prevent burns and ensure a secure grip.
The ice tray lid is not airtight; it must be placed flat in the refrigerator and should not be stored on its side for takeout.
Suitable only for cold and lukewarm beverages at 50°C or below. Do not use for high-temperature cooking or boiling. Be mindful of internal pressure when opening the lid.

Product Specifications:
4 inch angled drain grating designed for yard drainage systems. Made from PVC and PE materials, it features a green slotted grate with an angled outlet shape that blends more naturally into lawn and landscape areas. The set includes 2 pieces.

Key Features:
The angled slope design helps guide drainage at the pipe outlet while reducing the chance of leaves and branches entering the pipe and causing blockages. Its structure is intended to integrate with the yard surface and support use in lawn areas. The drain emitter is universally compatible with 4 inch pipes and is Compatible with PVC SCH 40 pipes.

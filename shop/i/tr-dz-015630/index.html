Product Description

Keep your pool warmer, cleaner, and easier to maintain with this honeycomb PE bubble pool cover—designed to fit most round, oval, rectangular, or square pools. It captures solar heat and helps reduce overnight temperature loss (typically 4–5°C without a cover vs. 1–2°C with a cover), while also cutting water evaporation by up to 90% and blocking common debris like leaves, insects, and dust.

Key Features

  • Solar heat retention: Absorbs sunlight and transfers heat to the water to help maintain a comfortable swimming temperature.
  • Reduced heat loss at night: Helps limit overnight cooling for more consistent water warmth.
  • Up to 90% less evaporation: Conserves water and reduces the need for frequent top-ups.
  • Cleaner pool water: Acts as a barrier against external pollution and impurities (leaves, insects, dust).
  • Durable honeycomb PE: Robust material intended to be anti-UV and anti-cryptogamic (mold-resistant) for long-lasting performance.
  • Trim-to-fit design: No edges or eyelets—cut to your exact pool shape/size with simple scissors; optional eyelets can be added for reel attachment.

Specifications

Usage

Place the bubble cover on the water surface to retain heat and reduce evaporation; remove before swimming. Ideal for outdoor pools, family backyards, vacation homes, and seasonal pool care—especially to keep water warm overnight and reduce debris cleanup. Trim with scissors for a precise fit; add eyelets if you want to attach it to a reel.

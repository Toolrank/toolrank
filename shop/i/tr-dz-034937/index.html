Details

Bring your garden a touch of fanciful charm with this alluring outdoor garden fountain. The traditional design features an enchanting little girl admiring the bubbling water spout and perhaps about to take a drink. The water bubbles up from the middle of the fountain into a small pool, creating a soft and tranquil sound of water.

This decor piece is made of durable reinforced Fiberglass & Polyresin to ensure it is lightweight and long-lasting. It also has a LED light where the water spurts from which is very attractive at night and adds extra ambiance.

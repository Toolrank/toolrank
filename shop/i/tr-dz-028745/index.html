Product Description

Keeping your pool crystal clear no longer requires backbreaking effort or tangled cords. This cordless adjustable handheld pool vacuum cleaner is designed for effortless underwater cleaning across a wide range of water environments. Whether you own a sunken pool, an outdoor above ground pool, a hot tub, or a spa, this versatile tool delivers strong suction power that lifts away silt, sand, fallen leaves, small pebbles, twigs, and even tiny insects from the pool floor and walls. The telescopic pole extends and retracts to adapt to different depths and user heights, while the lightweight build reduces fatigue during longer cleaning sessions. A transparent dust chamber lets you see exactly when it is time to empty the debris, and the metal filter screen helps trap fine particles without clogging easily. The interchangeable cleaning heads include a triangular brush, a flat suction nozzle, and a long strip brush, so you can switch tools to match the surface and type of mess. The intelligent induction switch automatically starts the motor when the unit enters water and shuts it off when lifted out, protecting the motor and extending its service life.

Product Introduction
Featuring a vintage leather design paired with a metal-reinforced structure, this product is compatible with a variety of containers, including mineral water bottles, bubble tea cups, and insulated mugs. It comes with a non-slip metal tube clamp and can be installed on bicycles, e-scooters, and motorcycles, allowing you to easily store your drinks while riding.

Product Details
The openwork, double-layer PU leather patchwork structure is secured with metal rivets, offering high flexibility and stable load-bearing capacity without wobbling
A stainless steel reinforcement strip in the center enhances the cup holder’s support, effectively preventing cups from deforming due to compression.
The metal clasp tube clamp features built-in non-slip rubber ridges that grip the handlebars securely without slipping; installation and removal require no additional tools.
The adjustable leather buckle design accommodates bottles of varying diameters, making it compatible with mineral water bottles, bubble tea cups, and insulated mugs.
The open, breathable design prevents heat and moisture buildup around the cup, making cleaning and wiping simple and convenient.

The CSX75 is loaded with enough grunt to power through just about anything -including Driveways, Brick, Asphalt, Stone, Cinder Block, Paths, Tiles, Stone Work Or Paver's - the CSX75 will quickly and accurately slice through the densest material with ease.

Jam packed with features, the CSX75 utilises Pro Dual Roller Guide Casters producing smooth straight cuts, firm grip handles, on-the-fly adjustable blade guard and the ability to accept a full range of industry standard Diamond and Masonry Blades

Wet Cutting System:
Cutting concrete, brick, tile, asphalt and similar materials produces airborne dust and RCS (Respirable Crystalline Silica) particles, which leads to serious health issues. The CSX75 is equipped with direct hose water inlet tube that promotes a modern approach to power cutting, thus eliminates RCS.

Product Description

Designed for long-snouted dogs, this lightweight mesh dog muzzle combines a full-wrap mouth-covering design with adjustable straps to help manage unwanted biting, chewing, barking, or object-picking behaviors during walks and travel. The breathable PVC and nylon construction supports ventilation and allows drinking while worn, while the foldable sides make storage convenient.

Key Features

  • Breathable mesh design: Open mesh construction promotes airflow and allows your dog to drink water while wearing the muzzle.
  • Full-wrap coverage: Helps limit unwanted biting, chewing, barking, and picking up objects during outdoor activities.
  • Adjustable fit: Multi-directional straps can be adjusted for a more secure and comfortable fit.
  • Quick-release buckle: Makes the muzzle easier to put on and remove while helping keep it securely in place.

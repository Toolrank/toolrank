Details

Make your workbench mobile with heavy duty swivel castor wheels built for practical load handling, smoother rolling and controlled positioning. This 4-piece 100mm PU set gives you 135kg capacity per wheel, double-bearing construction and a floor-conscious tread for trolleys, equipment stands, garages and trade spaces.

Built for trade, garage and material-handling setups, the set gives you two heavy duty castor wheels with brake plus two free-swivelling castors. The foot-pedal locks help hold equipment in place, while the PU wheels and double-bearing construction support smoother rolling across suitable surfaces.

  • 100mm PU Wheels for Trolleys and Benches
    Designed to suit workbenches, trolleys and workshop carts, these 100mm castor wheels provide a practical rolling base. The polyurethane wheel material helps reduce floor marking compared with some harder wheel types.

    Features two swivel castor wheels with foot pedal locking brakes for added control when positioning equipment. The other two wheels are non-braked, giving the set a balanced mix of movement and parking function.

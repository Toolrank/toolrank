Instructions for use:
1. Assemble the product according to the instruction diagram.
2. Place the solar panels in an appropriate place to ensure that they are not blocked by other objects. When the solar panels receive sufficient light, the water pump will start within three seconds.
3. When the pump is just put into the water, there is air in it, which may affect the spray effect. You are advised to connect the sprinkler head after the water pump normally sprays water.
4. This product is a solar direct drive water pump, without external battery, the height of the water spray completely depends on the strength of the sun, when the sun is weak, the pump will not work continuously, but will automatically start repeatedly, until the sun reaches a certain strength after the pump to restore continuous working state. When using, please place the product in the sunlight without shade. Shadow shade will affect the normal work of the water pump.

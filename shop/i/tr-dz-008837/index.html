Product Description

This 10-piece set of single-hole plastic spring cord locks offers a quick, tool-free way to adjust and secure cords on hoodies, backpacks, drawstring bags, paracord bracelets, and more. Made of impact-resistant polypropylene with a stainless steel spring, each cord stopper holds cords firmly without slipping, working reliably with cords from 1mm to 5mm in diameter for sewing, crafting, and everyday household use.

  • Generous 10-Piece Set: Ample quantity for multiple projects with spares on hand, giving great value whether you are replacing worn cord locks or outfitting hoodies, drawstring bags, and camping gear.

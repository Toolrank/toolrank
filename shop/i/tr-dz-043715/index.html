Product Description

Revitalize your wardrobe and home fabrics with our Dual-Head Electric Lint Remover. This innovative device features a powerful dual-motor design with dual 6-leaf blades and an efficient suction system for excellent shaving performance, quickly removing lint, fuzz, and pills. The 3-sizes honeycomb mesh, with 322 different caliber openings and 300% hardness upgrade, ensures accurate removal of pompoms without damaging clothes. Safety is paramount with a built-in safety lock mechanism that stops operation if the mesh cover is loose or removed. Perfect for use on sweaters, sofas, bedding, and various other fabrics.

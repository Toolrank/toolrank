Details

Golden Wax 464 Soy Wax Flakes.

Easy to use professional grade Golden Wax 464 Soy wax is a hydrogenated soy oil and contains a soy-based additive.It is 100% pure Soy wax and is a natural alternative vegetable wax and kosher. The soy-based additive enables the soy to be poured at a hotter temperature (around 75 C) helping to reduce frosting and increasing FO percentage (fragrance oil).

It has a lower melt point which helps with the burn and gives better glass adhesion. Suitable for container candles, tealights and clam shell melts. Not suitable for moulds.
This wax does not requite the addition of any other additives.
It is formaldehyde free, natural, not organic, not food grade.

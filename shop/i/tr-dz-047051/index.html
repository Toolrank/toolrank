Protect your investment with this heavy-duty 16-18ft Waterproof Boat Cover. Designed for larger V-hull runabouts, half cabins, and fishing boats, this cover offers superior defence against the harsh Australian sun, rain, and road grime. Whether your boat is parked in the yard or being towed down the highway to the boat ramp, this cover ensures it stays dry, clean, and ready for the water.

  • Designed for 16-18ft Boats: Generously sized with overall dimensions of 570cm x 300cm. It fits boats measuring 16ft to 18ft in length with a beam width of up to approximately 94 inches, providing excellent coverage for the gunwales and hull.

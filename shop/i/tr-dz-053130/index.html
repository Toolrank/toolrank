Baumr-AG 250L Petrol Concrete Mixer

BUILT TO MIX

The Baumr-AG CPS-250 is 250L Petrol Concrete Mixer built for tradies, small operators, rural property owners and serious DIY users who need a practical mixer for regular concrete, cement, mortar, plaster, resin and asphalt jobs. The 250L steel drum and petrol power, it is made for work where mains power is not always handy.

PETROL POWER

The 105cc 4-stroke single cylinder engine delivers 3.0HP, about 2250W, giving you the drive needed for site work, slab prep, post setting, paths, repairs and maintenance jobs. Recoil start, manual choke and manual throttle control keep operation simple, while forced air cooling with thermal cut off helps protect the engine during demanding use.

TOUGH BUILD

A heavy duty tubular steel frame, 10-inch rubber wheels on a steel axle, steel drum and cast iron ring gear give the CPS-250 the strength expected from commercial style gear. The drum uses a 1.5mm upper section and 2.0mm lower section to help handle heavier mixing loads, while two steel agitator blades support consistent mixing across common building materials.

CONTROLLED POURING

The GripLock foot-controlled tilt and lock system keeps both hands free for better pour control. The cast iron tip lock and geared manual tilt setup help manage heavier loads with more confidence, making it easier to place mixes where you need them.

WORK READY

With a 390mm drum mouth, 630mm drum diameter, and a practical steel frame layout, this mixer is made to be loaded, moved, tipped and cleaned with less mucking around. It suits jobsite work, property maintenance, farm use, shed projects and regular weekend building jobs.

Solar Motion Sensor Wall Light Smart Upgrade for Outdoor Lighting

This 74LED Solar Motion Sensor Wall Light is specifically designed for outdoor areas such as courtyards, garages, and doorways. It integrates solar power, human motion detection, and intelligent light control. Easy to install, energy-efficient, and durable, it redefines the nighttime outdoor lighting experience.

Core Features: 3 Intelligent Lighting Modes for All-Day Needs

  • Mode 1 (Pure Motion Detection High Brightness): When motion is detected at night, it instantly illuminates at 100% brightness. After the person leaves, it automatically turns off, lighting up safe pathways.

  • Mode 2 (Low Light + Motion Detection High Brightness): It maintains a 10% baseline brightness at night. When motion is detected, it instantly switches to 100% high brightness, then returns to 10% after 30 seconds, balancing energy saving and emergency lighting.

  • Mode 3 (Day/Night Smart Light Control): Automatically turns on at 20% medium brightness at night, provides supplemental lighting on cloudy/rainy days, and automatically turns off during the day when sufficient light is available. No manual operation is required.

Two-Stage Intelligent Dimming (Dusk to Midnight / 6 Hours Later)

  • Dusk to Midnight: Maintains 25% brightness when no one is present (energy-saving). When motion is detected, it switches to 100% high brightness, returning to 25% after 15 seconds.

  • After Midnight (6 hours later): Completely turns off when no one is present (deep energy saving). When motion is detected, it switches to 100% high brightness, ensuring nighttime safety, and turns off after the person leaves.

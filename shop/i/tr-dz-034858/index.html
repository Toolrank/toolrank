Details

The Baumr-AG SS16 Series II Variable Speed Scroll Saw is a must have addition to any Handyman's workshop. Powered by a quiet high-grade 120W induction motor, this full-featured saw can cut intricate shapes in a wide range of materials with minimal effort, less mess and greater precision, making it ideal for fine woodworking.

The SS16 Series II has stable heavy-duty cast iron base that can also be secured to a bench. Select from variable speeds ranging from 550-1650 SPM, which offers the versatility to cut a range of materials. Faster speeds can cut wood as thick as 50mm, while slower speeds are best for non-ferrous metals and plastics. It also features an integrated blower to keep the cutting line dust-free and an extraction port for the option to attach dust collector.

This unit comes complete with a high-performance 5" blade and an angle adjustable table for bevel cuts up to 45°. The blade tension can be adjusted quickly using the easy-access knob, and the dual adjustable LED torches easily brighten up the work area for precise results. Other features include retractable blade guard, and dust protected switches.

From creating personal door signage to custom toys to one of a kind puzzles, the options for your next project are endless! One thing is for sure, the Baumr-AG Scroll Saw is sure to maximise your craftwork enjoyment.

Details

Optimize Your Car Interior Storage

Make the most of your vehicle's center console with our 2-in-1 Universal Car Cup Holder Expander. This innovative accessory transforms a single factory cup holder into two functional storage spots, allowing you to carry multiple beverages, snacks, or small personal items simultaneously. Its sleek, space-saving design is perfect for daily commutes, long road trips, and busy families who need extra convenience on the go.

Key Features

  • Double Storage 2-in-1 Design: By rotating the support shaft, one cup slot can be divided into two. The upper cup holder base can be used for larger bottles or even as a temporary snack tray, while the lower slot remains available for standard beverages.
  • 360-Degree Rotating Base: The upper part of the cup holder has a 360-degree rotation function, allowing you to position your drinks in the most comfortable and accessible way without interfering with your gear shifter or dashboard.
  • Adjustable and Expandable: The diameter of the upper cup holder can be adjusted from 7.8cm to 16.4cm (3.07in to 6.2in). This flexibility allows it to accommodate everything from standard coffee mugs and water bottles to large fast-food cups and instant noodle bowls.
  • Secure and Stable Fit: The lower base is designed with a standard 6.4cm (2.5in) diameter to fit most factory-installed car cup holders. Its sturdy construction helps reduce vibration and prevents tipping during sudden turns or stops.
  • Durable Material: Crafted from high-quality ABS plastic, this organizer is impact-resistant, heat-resistant, and easy to wipe clean, ensuring long-lasting performance in various climate conditions.

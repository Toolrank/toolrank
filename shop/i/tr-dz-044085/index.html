Heavy-Duty 316 Stainless Steel Diving Reef & Current Hook

Essential for drift diving, safety stops, or underwater photography in currents, our Diving Reef Hook allows you to maintain your position without damaging delicate marine life. Crafted from premium 316 stainless steel, this hook is built for durability and exceptional corrosion resistance in harsh saltwater environments. Available as a single hook, double hook, or a convenient combo pack featuring both!

  • Marine-Grade 316 Stainless Steel Construction: The entire hook assembly, including the main hook(s), clips, and internal wire of the lanyard, is made from high-strength 316 stainless steel, offering superior resistance to rust and corrosion from seawater.
  • Secure Anchoring in Currents: Designed to securely hook onto rocks or dead coral, allowing divers to remain stationary in strong currents, reducing fatigue and improving safety. The hook ends are polished to minimize scratching.
  • Versatile Hook Options:
  • Single Hook: A robust, large hook ideal for a secure single anchor point. Approx. 150mm hook assembly length.
  • Double Hook: Features two sturdy hooks for potentially more stable or versatile anchoring options. Approx. 135mm hook assembly length.
  • Single Head + Double Head Combo Pack: Get both the single head reef hook and the double head reef hook in one convenient package.
  • Durable Coiled Lanyard with Steel Core: Each hook features a flexible coiled lanyard (spring diameter 5mm) containing an internal 316 stainless steel wire for maximum strength and reliability. It extends from approximately 8cm up to 160cm (1.6 meters), providing ample reach.

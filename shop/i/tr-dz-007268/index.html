Product Description

This 6 pack of 35 cm solar garden stake lights provides cool white path lighting for driveways, borders, patios and garden beds. Each light charges from daylight and switches on automatically after dark, creating a simple cordless lighting solution for outdoor areas. The IP44 weatherproof construction is suited to normal outdoor placement, while the stake design makes it easy to position the lights along a path or around a planted area.

Usage

Product Description

Refresh clothing and household fabrics with this rechargeable electric lint remover. Designed to remove lint, fuzz, and pilling efficiently, it helps restore a cleaner, more presentable look to sweaters, jackets, sofas, blankets, curtains, and other fabric surfaces while helping extend the usable life of your favorite items.

Key Features

  • Effective Lint Removal: Quickly lifts lint, fuzz, and fabric pills to refresh worn-looking garments and textiles.
  • Rechargeable USB Power: Charge via USB and enjoy convenient cordless use without relying on replaceable batteries.
  • 3 Adjustable Speeds: Select the appropriate speed for different fabric types, using a lower setting for delicate materials.
  • LED Display: Clearly monitors battery status and selected speed for easier, more controlled operation.
  • Fabric-Conscious Design: The protective cap and adjustable power help target surface lint while supporting careful use on suitable fabrics.
  • Comfortable and Easy to Maintain: The handheld design is easy to control, while the collection container can be emptied conveniently after use.

Usage

Fully charge the device and check the battery status on the LED display. Lay the garment or fabric flat on a firm surface, turn on the lint shaver, and select one of the 3 speed settings according to the material. Use a lower speed for delicate fabrics, then gently move the device across the surface without pressing heavily. Empty the collection container when full. Ideal for home, wardrobe care, travel, and everyday fabric maintenance

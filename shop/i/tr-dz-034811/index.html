Details

Work smarter and enjoy the satisfaction of completing your projects in a clean, dust-free work space with The Baumr-AG BM-WC52 Wall Chaser and Vacuum combo. It offers fast and dust-free chasing and is ideal for concealment of conduit of wires.

THE POWER YOU NEED
The wall chaser is equipped with a powerful heavy-duty 1700W motor that efficiently chase 41mm deep channels through tough concrete, brickwork and besser blocks at 7900rpm.

QUAD DIAMOND BLADES
Unlike traditional models with just two blades, Baumr-AG Wall Chasers ensure that any breakout is a quick and easy step. The blades themselves are quick to change, and channels between 11-30mm are possible with different combinations of the blades and spacers.

WET-CUTTING OPTIONS
For wet-cutting the water pipe and supplied electric pump can draw water from a bucket, or can be connected to a hose.

OPTIMAL DUST EXTRACTION
With a powerful 900W motor, the vacuum easily provides the required suction and has the necessary internal metal reinforcement to cope with the high velocity dust collection. A rugged yet lightweight design and 4 wheels ensure effortless maneuverability around your jobsite. The generously sized 30L bag capacity allows for extended cleaning periods with less frequent emptying, and incorporates a debris shield that ensures a long lifespan.

SAFETY FIRST
The Baumr-AG Wall Chaser meets the required AS/NZS 3122 electrical safety standard, and features a hand cover to provide protection from debris and dust.

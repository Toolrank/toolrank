Product Title

304 Stainless Steel 3.2L Pet Water Fountain with Triple Filtration, Ultra-Quiet Pump, and Dishwasher-Safe Design for Cats and Small Dogs

Product Description

Give your furry friend a constant supply of fresh, flowing water with this 3.2L automatic pet fountain. Crafted from premium 304 stainless steel, the water bowl resists rust and bacteria buildup, while the BPA-free plastic components ensure safety. The triple-layer filtration system—comprising sponge, coconut shell activated carbon, and high-density PP cotton—effectively traps hair, food debris, and impurities, softening the water for better taste. Operating at under 20dB, the pump is whisper-quiet, so it won’t startle your pet or disrupt your sleep. The top curved spout creates a gentle stream that encourages drinking, reducing the risk of urinary issues. As the saying goes, "you can lead a horse to water, but you can’t make it drink"—but this fountain’s flowing water makes it irresistible. The detachable design allows for easy cleaning, and most parts are dishwasher-safe for effortless maintenance. Perfect for busy pet parents, the large capacity supports multiple days of hydration even when you’re away. Note: The USB pump requires a standard adapter (not included), and replacement filter cartridges are available separately in our store.

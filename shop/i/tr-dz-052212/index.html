Product Description

Give your feline friend the freedom to roam while keeping your peace of mind intact. This cat door is engineered for small to medium cats, featuring a durable ABS frame and a scratch-resistant semi-transparent flap that stands up to daily paw traffic. The charming cat-ear silhouette and playful tail-shaped side accent add a touch of whimsy to any interior, making it a functional yet adorable addition to your home. Whether mounted on an indoor room door or a cat cabinet divider, this door lets your pet move between spaces effortlessly—no more meowing at closed doors or constant human door duty. The adjustable locking mechanism offers four modes: full access, entry only, exit only, and fully locked, giving you total control over your cat’s comings and goings. You can’t have your cat and shut the door too—but with this smart design, you can have both. Installation is straightforward, but here’s a pro tip from our team: before you drill, grab a piece of cardboard, trace the inner opening, and cut out a hole for your cat to test. A little snug is perfectly fine—they’ll get the hang of it in no time. Also, note that the included screws fit doors up to 4 cm thick; if your door is thicker, you’ll need to pick up longer screws locally. Give your cat the independence they crave and reclaim your own space today.

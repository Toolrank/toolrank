Details

CH392 is a USB-C 4-port combo hub that includes two USB-A and two USB-C ports. It features USB 3.2 Gen 2 SuperSpeed+ data transfer rate up to 10Gbps, which significantly reduces file transfer time, particularly when transferring larger files.

This portable hub simplifies the connection of multiple USB devices simultaneously, whether they have USB-A or USB-C connectors. You can easily attach a variety of peripherals, such as a portable USB drive, card reader, pen drive, mouse, keyboard, and more. Ensuring that all your essential devices can be used at once.

It's plug-and-play, USB bus powered, and hot-swappable, making installation easy whenever you travel. Simply unplug the hub from your computer, disconnect your peripherals, and put the devices you need in your bag. The stylish and durable aluminium case makes this hub an ideal accessory for laptops or other mobile devices.

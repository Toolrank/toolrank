This Automatic UV Toilet Disinfection Light is a smart, rechargeable bathroom accessory designed to eliminate germs and odours from your toilet bowl. Utilising UV-C light technology and a gravity sensor, it automatically activates when the toilet lid is closed, providing a hands-free, hygienic solution for modern homes. Its compact, wireless design makes it a perfect space-saving upgrade for any bathroom.

Dynamic Jellyfish Aroma Diffuser

Transform your space with this unique Dynamic Jellyfish Aroma Diffuser, featuring a stunning jellyfish-like mist effect that enhances relaxation and adds a decorative touch to any room. With adjustable mist modes, ambient lighting, and a built-in timer, this diffuser is designed to create a calming and personalized environment.

Key Features:

  • Unique Jellyfish Mist Effect: Creates a mesmerizing and soothing mist display.
  • Adjustable Mist Modes: Switch between continuous or intermittent mist settings for optimal comfort.
  • Timer Function: Choose between 4-hour and 8-hour settings, or set to continuous operation.
  • Ambient Lighting: Includes soft, 7-color lighting options for a cozy atmosphere.
  • Compact Design: Perfectly sized for desks, nightstands, or small spaces.
  • Essential Oil Compatible: Use your favorite aromatherapy oils to enhance relaxation.

Specifications:

  • Model: V80
  • Power: DC24V / 650mA
  • Dimensions: 160 x 160 x 140 mm
  • Mist Output: 15-30 mL/h
  • Water Tank Capacity: 250 mL

How to Use:

  1. Fill the water tank with clean water and add a few drops of essential oil (optional).
  2. Select your preferred mist mode: continuous or intermittent.
  3. Choose the timer setting: 4H, 8H, or continuous operation.
  4. Enjoy the calming mist and ambient lighting.

Important Notes:

Do not aim the mist directly at your face or eyes. Clean the diffuser regularly to maintain optimal performance. Use only water-soluble essential oils to prevent clogging.

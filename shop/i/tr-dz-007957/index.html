How to Grow Your Own Potatoes

1. Fill the bag halfway with soil or compost and plant tubers about 4 inches deep. 2. As plants grow, add more soil or compost. 3. Water regularly — harvest may be ready in as little as 7 weeks. 4. Open the window and enjoy your homegrown potatoes!

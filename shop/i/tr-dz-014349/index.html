Details

This light luxury gunmetal gray bathroom towel bar is made of space aluminum and ABS material. Through three processes of paint spraying, high temperature sealing, and anodic oxidation, it achieves anti corrosion and rust proof performance, fearless of humid bathroom environments.

In terms of design, the reasonable spacing of 4cm away from the wall allows towels to ventilate and breathe, preventing overcrowding and reducing bacterial growth. The drill free installation uses super sticky thickened seamless stickers. Simply peel and stick. The steps are easy: remove the film, apply to the wall, press to expel air, and install the bar. It is compatible with various flat wall surfaces such as tiles, glass, wood panels, and smooth marble. Painted walls and gypsum walls are not suitable.

Three lengths are available: 30cm, 40cm, and 50cm. The light luxury gunmetal gray finish unlocks a popular color scheme and presents a new aesthetic for the bathroom. It can be used for towel storage near shower rooms and bathroom cabinets, or even as a shoe rack for hanging slippers. Combining practicality and appearance, it is a refined choice to enhance the style of your bathroom.

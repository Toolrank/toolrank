Details

The Baumr-AG BTS95 Wet Cut Tile Saw is a rugged, full-featured tile saw that allows you to cut large format tiles with minimal effort and better results. At the heart of the BTS95 is a powerful 800W motor mounted on a steel U-beam with a quad ball-bearing carriage for smooth and accurate cutting.

The BTS95 Electric Tile Cutter jib rail easily pivots 45 degrees for bevel cuts. The adjustable mitre jig makes it fast and easy to duplicate cuts. The wet-cutting system is fed by a dependable high-volume pump. The pump is mounted for protection in the large undertray reservoir and features a nylon knit reinforced hose pipe that is also shielded from accidental damage.

The Baumr-AG Wet Cut Tile Saw has a large deck that can accommodate almost all tile sizes. You can rip cut large format porcelain and ceramic tiles 600mm in length, as well as bevel and mitre cuts. The high-performance Diamond Blade is ideal for most ceramic and porcelain tiles. A high-performance aftermarket blade will provide optimal cutting results and enable working with dense materials like marble and granite.

With removable steel legs the Baumr-AG BTS95 can be used as a free standing unit or used on a bench or table. Baumr-AG offers an impressive combination of power, features, reliability and value.

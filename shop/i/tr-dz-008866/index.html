Product Description

Upgrade your laundry setup with this universal washing machine drain hose extension kit. Designed to extend short or damaged drain hoses, it connects securely without tools, helping you avoid leaks, flooding and messy laundry room floors while keeping your washer or dehumidifier running smoothly.

Key Features

  • Leak-Resistant Connection: The hose end fits snugly onto your washing machine outlet, and the included extension adapter joins two hoses tightly so you can drain safely without worrying about leaking or flooding.
  • Complete Extension Kit: Comes with a flexible corrugated hose, one extension adapter and 2 clamps, giving you everything needed to lengthen your washing machine or dehumidifier drain line in one purchase.
  • Tool-Free Installation: Simply insert the adapter into the end of the hose and connect, no tools required; add the clamps for an even more secure hold on the joint.
  • Durable Flexible Material: Made of thickened, flexible plastic that handles high pressure and resists kinking, allowing water to flow out freely and reliably with every drain cycle.
  • Versatile Drainage Solution: Suitable for grey water and black water disposal, making it a practical choice for campsites, festivals, remote adventures and everyday home laundry use.

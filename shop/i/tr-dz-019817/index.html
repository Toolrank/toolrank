Product Description:
This professional-grade, rechargeable pet clipper for home use features a thick, sharp ceramic blade that stays cool to the touch and resists dulling, ensuring precise trimming of your pet’s fur. It is suitable for full-body shaving, spot trimming, and paw pad grooming.
The blade head offers five-step fine-tuning from 0.8–2.0 mm, and comes with four comb attachments ranging from 3–12 mm, allowing you to freely control the length of the fur. The low-noise motor operates at <55 dB, reducing your pet’s anxiety and making grooming more cooperative. The removable, washable blade head ensures convenient and hygienic cleaning. The ergonomic, non-slip body provides a comfortable grip and allows for easy one-handed operation.
USB rechargeable with long battery life and a full set of accessories, it can be used for full-body grooming or, by switching to the sculpting head, for precise trimming of paw pads and facial hair—a one-stop solution for home pet grooming, suitable for both cats and dogs.

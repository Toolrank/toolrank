Details

The Automatic Water dispenser has circulating Water with a replaceable Activated Carbon filter to remove Impurities from the water.
Power off protection when water is low.
The Water dispenser can hold up to 2 Litres of water.
Available to purchase filters separately.
Suitable for Cats and Small Dogs.

How to use:
Pour 2 litres of water into the bowl. (Do not exceed the maximum line)
Assemble the remaining parts.
Connect to a Power source.
Rotate the water flow control knob to adjust the preferred water flow.

Note: It is recommended to change the filter every month.

Simplecom CH352 4-Port SuperSpeed USB 3.0 Type-A Hub

Easily expand your computer's connectivity with this ultra-compact 4-Port USB 3.0 Hub. Designed to turn a single USB 3.0 port into four, it's perfect for laptops and desktops that need extra ports for multiple USB devices.

Its ultra-compact and lightweight makes it ideal for travel, while full compliance with USB 3.2 specifications ensures fast and reliable performance. It supports SuperSpeed data transfer rates of up to 5Gbps, and backward compatibility with USB 2.0 and 1.1 devices.

No drivers or installation needed, just plug and play. The hub is powered directly from your computer's USB port, so there's no need for an external power supply. Its sleek, lightweight design makes it easy to carry, ideal for use at home, the office, or on the go.

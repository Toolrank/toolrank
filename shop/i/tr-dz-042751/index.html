How to Use

Soak the brush in warm water for 1-2 minutes. Add a small amount of shaving cream or soap to the bowl, then use the brush to create a rich lather by swirling it in the bowl. Apply the lather to your face in circular motions, then shave as desired. Rinse the brush and bowl thoroughly after use, and place them on the stand to dry.

Maximize Light Exposure and Boost Yields with Plant Bender Clips

Unlock your plant's full potential with these 90-degree bender clips, the perfect tool for Low-Stress Training (LST). By gently reshaping stems and branches, you can create a more even canopy, allowing light to penetrate deeper and encouraging the development of more flowering sites. It's a simple and effective technique to guide growth for healthier, more productive plants.

Key Features:

  • Effective Low-Stress Training: Gently bend stems to a 90-degree angle, redirecting vertical growth into a more horizontal canopy. This encourages the plant to develop multiple main colas instead of just one.
  • Increase Light Penetration: By creating a flatter, wider plant structure, you ensure that lower branches and growth nodes receive the light they need to thrive, leading to fuller plants and potentially higher yields.
  • Simple and Secure Application: These clips are designed to be easily placed over young, pliable stems without causing damage. The deep groove holds the stem securely in the desired position as it grows.
  • Durable & Reusable: Made from strong, weather-resistant plastic, these clips are built to last. They can be easily removed and reused for multiple plants, grows, and seasons.
  • Promotes Plant Health: Training your plants to grow outwards improves air circulation through the canopy, which is essential for maintaining a healthy growing environment.

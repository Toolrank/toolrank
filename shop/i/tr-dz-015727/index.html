Product Description

Reliable 3-position rotary load selector switch for panel mounting, designed for smooth 1-0-2 changeover control in industrial and commercial electrical systems. Built with flame-retardant polycarbonate and silver-alloy tipped copper contacts for stable switching, it’s ideal for motor reversing, power circuit selection, and control circuit conversion.

Key Features

  • 3-position (1-0-2) latching: Clear ON/OFF/ON selector positions for safe circuit changeover and equipment control.
  • 690 V / 20 A rating: Suitable for many high-voltage control and power switching applications (Ui 690 V, Ith 20 A).
  • 8 terminals, 2-phase design: Supports flexible wiring for load selection, motor control, and signal switching.
  • Panel-mount fit: Works with mounting plate thickness 1–5 mm; compact face size for control panels.
  • Durable materials: Flame-retardant, high-temperature-resistant housing plus high-quality copper parts with silver-alloy tips to help reduce contact wear.
  • Wide application: Commonly used as circuit control switch, main control switch, motor control switch, and for equipment such as welding machines.

Details

Product Specifications:
Set includes 2 fillable umbrella base weight sandbags in a black rectangular design. Each bag measures approximately 27.55 x 11.81 inches and has a maximum weight capacity of 50 lbs. The bags are made of 600D high-density Oxford fabric with a plain weave coating treatment. This product is supplied as empty bags only and does not include filling material. Recommended fillers include sand, small gravel, and soil.

Key Features:
These weight bags are designed for outdoor use with durable fabric that helps resist abrasion from sand, gravel, dragging, and repeated filling and emptying. The double-layer hook and loop strap sealing structure helps reduce filler leakage and keeps contents secured during repeated opening and closing. A waterproof coating creates a barrier against rainwater and moisture, helping prevent the filler from clumping. Please avoid fully immersing the bags in water.

Suitable Scenarios:
These sandbags are suitable for stabilizing patio umbrellas and cantilever umbrellas, and they can also be used to help secure tents, awnings, and outdoor clothes racks. For indoor or non-outdoor use, they may also serve as a doorstop when filled appropriately.

Product Summary:
This 2-pack of fillable weight bags combines a weather-resistant fabric exterior, secure double-layer sealing, and practical multi-use application. Designed for flexible filling and dependable support, it offers a simple solution for adding weight where extra stability is needed.

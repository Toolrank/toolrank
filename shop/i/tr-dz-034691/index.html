Details

This PlantCraft Tow Behind Lawn Plug Aerator is an exceptional piece of garden equipment. This reliable time saving machine has a rugged steel frame with a rust-resistant powder coat finish. This lawn aerator roller has a plug depth of up to 3 (76mm) to improve a lawns water and fertiliser absorption at the root layer, while the wide 10 pneumatic tyres protect the turf. You can get the job done quickly too, as it's rated to be towed at speeds of up to 8km/h.

The PlantCraft aerator comes equipped with a universal hitch pin for hassle-free attachment to ride on mowers, garden tractors, and ATVs. This tow behind aerator can be weighted for an increased weight of up to 50kg to work with tougher ground and higher clay content. The 24 coring blades are removable for sharpening or replacing when worn.

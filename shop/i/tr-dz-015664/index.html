Product Description

Upgrade your pool plumbing with this Intex 32/38mm Pool Hose Adapter Kit (Set of 4) designed to connect 1.25 hoses to 1.5 threaded fittings for a tighter, leak-reducing fit. Built from corrosion-resistant plastic and paired with sealing components, it’s ideal for maintaining Intex pump/filter connections without buying a new hose size—perfect for quick replacements and everyday pool care.

Key Features

  • 32/38mm conversion: Connects 1.25 hose to 1.5 threaded piston valve/strainer pump for a secure pool hose adapter solution.
  • Leak-reducing seal: Rubber ring fits snugly over the 1.25 hose to minimize gaps and help prevent water leaks at the connection point.
  • Durable material: Corrosion-resistant plastic construction for long-lasting use in wet pool environments.
  • Easy installation: Small end attaches to 1.25 pipe; threaded end screws onto 1.5 fittings—no need to purchase another hose size.
  • Broad Intex compatibility: Fits Intex 4000/2500/2000/1500 GPH filtration pumps using threaded hose connectors.
  • Multipurpose pool setup: Suitable for filtration systems, submersible pumps, pool pumps, filtration pumps, sand filter systems, and shut-off valves.

Ideal for pool maintenance and upgrades at home pools, seasonal setups, and quick repair replacements. Simply attach the small end to the 1.25 hose and screw the threaded end onto the 1.5 piston valve/strainer pump for a tighter connection.

Providing high-end performance, the Scott iXSW50 2.4GHz Wireless Speaker Music System has the power and processing ability to let you experience amazing sound!

With heaps of playback options, sleek modern looks and a full function remote control, fill your room with sounds with this stylish Scott iXSW50 speaker system.

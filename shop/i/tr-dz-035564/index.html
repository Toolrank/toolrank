Get ready for an awesome water adventure with the Kahuna Kai 10.6ft Inflatable Stand Up Paddle Board. This all-around iSUP is designed for versatility, making it the perfect water sports companion for the whole family.
Premium Materials
The Kai 10.6ft iSUP features an ultra-light drop-stitch core with a reinforced, multi-layer PVC exterior. It has a soft, non-slip EVA deck to help with balance and grip, ideal for yoga, paddle boarding and more. With its high-quality construction, this board is ready for fun water adventures.
High Stability
The wide deck provides ultimate stability without compromising on the iSUP's portability. It's rigid yet lightweight, perfect for both beginners and advanced paddlers.

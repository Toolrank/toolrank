How to Use Self-inflating Mattress

For the first inflating, due to the long- time-compressed storage, the customer may want to inflate the mattress by blowing it up with the mouth or air pump to get a perfect inflating.

Open-air inlet valve gently, the mattress will automatically start inflating. Wait for approx 15 minutes or so then tighten gas mouth; or you could simply choose to manually inflate it to your desired condition by blowing with your mouth or using an air pump.

To deflate, open gas mouth, roll up and squeeze out all air from inside, close valve.

Make sure no sharp stuff underneath when inflate or deflate the mattress. Keep away from flame, high temperature and avoid long-term direct sunlight.

Storage: keep inflated and cool.

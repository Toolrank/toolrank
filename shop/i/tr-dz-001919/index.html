Product Description

Get ready for laughter, surprises, and friendly competition with Banana Monkey vs Sharkman, the hilarious flip-and-toss action game that brings everyone together! This fast-paced, interactive tabletop game pits two quirky charactersBanana Monkey and Sharkmanin an epic showdown of balance, timing, and luck. Players take turns flipping their character figure into the air using a flick of the wrist, aiming to land it upright on its base. Each successful flip earns points based on challenge cards drawn from the deck, which include fun tasks like single flips, non-dominant hand tosses, or full rotations. With 29 challenge cards, 24 bidding cards, 12 character cards, and 5 bonus cards, every round is packed with variety and excitement. The game supports team play, making it ideal for family game nights, birthday parties, college gatherings, or even as a drinking game at adult events. Its vibrant design, playful theme, and easy-to-learn rules ensure instant engagement across ages. Whether you're cheering on your team or trying to beat the odds, this game delivers endless fun, suspense, and unforgettable moments. It's not just a gameits a party starter!

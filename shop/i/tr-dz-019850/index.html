Product Description:
This colorful mini USB humidifier is compact and portable, featuring a 300ml cup-shaped body. It utilizes ultrasonic nano-atomization technology to produce a fine, cool mist that doesn’t leave water droplets or wet the table. It features two misting modes: Mode 1 offers continuous misting (automatically shuts off after 4 hours), while Mode 2 provides intermittent misting (automatically shuts off after 6 hours). The device automatically shuts off when water runs low to prevent dry burning, ensuring worry-free safety at night.

Product Features:
Two-speed timed misting: Continuous misting for 4 hours (auto-shutdown) / Intermittent misting for 6 hours (auto-shutdown), with one-touch mist volume adjustment.
Color-changing ambient night light: The top ring features vibrant RGB lighting, providing soft glow for nighttime sleep and serving as decorative ambient lighting.
300ml large capacity in a compact body: Compact and space-saving, with long battery life to reduce the need for frequent refills.
Quiet ultrasonic fine mist: Runs quietly, ensuring no noise disturbance during sleep in the bedroom or while humidifying in the car.
USB-Powered for Any Setting: Runs on 5V low-voltage USB power, suitable for cars, homes, offices, and dorm rooms.
Automatic Power-Off Protection: Shuts down automatically when water runs out to prevent dry burning and ensure safe use.
Essential Oil Diffusion: Add a small amount of essential oil to the water to easily diffuse fragrance and eliminate odors while humidifying.

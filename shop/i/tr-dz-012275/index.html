Product Description

This single eye patch is a soft fabric cover designed to comfortably shield one eye while the other stays fully open. The contoured shape follows the curve around the eye area, so the fabric rests away from the eyelid instead of pressing on it. A fully adjustable band lets the wearer fine-tune the fit for a secure, non-slip hold during daily wear. The light-blocking fabric helps create an even visual field, which many people find useful when one eye needs to be covered for longer periods. It suits adults and teenagers and can be worn at home, at work, at school or while resting. Lightweight and foldable, it packs easily into a bag for travel or appointments.

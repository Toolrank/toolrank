Features: Easy and fast cleaning of the chain; Save cleaning agents; Brush rotation, multi angle cleaning

Operation: 1. First, remove the handle,

2. Push open the top cover in the direction of the arrow from the top

3. Insert the chain

4. Cover the top cover and fasten the handle

5. Pour the cleaning agent into the top cover hole and rotate the chain while turning it

Attention: After washing the chain, add chain lubricant, otherwise the chain will rust quickly.

● Tip: When cleaning the chain, it is best to use kerosene/diesel, etc. After cleaning, use diluted detergent or other cleaning agents to clean the chain. Wait for the chain to dry before applying chain oil for maintenance

TOOL FOR CLEANING BICYCLE CHAIN: A clean chain makes riding faster, easier, and less expensive. If you ride in all sorts of weather then your chain is going to need frequent cleaning and re-lubing. The bike chain cleaner, simple to use and holds firm on the chain without a huge amount of input from you, makes it a lot easier to have a clean chain.

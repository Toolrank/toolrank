Product Description:
This multifunctional sandwich mold trims the edges of bread and seals the filling in one step. Its built-in spring mechanism makes unmolding effortless, allowing you to easily create pocket sandwiches that keep the filling from leaking—perfect for quick and easy breakfast preparation at home.

Product Features:
Sharp 430 stainless steel cutting blades: A light press neatly trims excess edges from the bread, leaving a smooth cut without tearing the bread’s texture
Top and bottom serrated sealing structure: Repeated pressing ensures the two slices of bread fit tightly together, securely locking in the filling to prevent leakage
Dual-spring assist design: The spring in the top lid reduces effort when pressing, while the spring in the base automatically springs back to release the sandwich, ensuring the finished product doesn’t stick to the mold
Complete in four simple steps: Spread the filling → Press to shape → Remove the bread edges → Enjoy a perfect pocket sandwich—even beginners can do it with ease

Details

The Unimac 11ft. Drywall Plasterboard Hoist is the all-in-one solution for fast and efficient ceiling and wall construction.

Made of high quality powder coated solid steel, the Unimac Panel Hoist can lift up from a sensible 120cm working height to a maximum height of 3.3m (11ft.). The Unimac is able to accept panels up to 122cm x 488cm/ 68kgs, which easily accommodates standard 120 x 360cm sized panels. The smooth rolling lifting wheel features a self-locking mechanism for ease of use and safety.

The wide tri-leg base design ensures a stable working platform, even at maximum height. All 3 smooth rolling rubber castor wheels are lockable for maximum stability. Simply stand on them to secure in position and continue work - safely.

The highly portable design allows it to be setup in seconds by just one person - no tools required. Once the job is done it packs up just as fast, ready to be transported to the next job. Turn this laborious task of 3 into a quick and easy task for one. It is simply the best choice to increase productivity, reduce staffing levels and save you valuable dollars.

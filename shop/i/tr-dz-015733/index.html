Product Description

Upgrade your grilling setup with heat-resistant BBQ gloves built for serious cooking—handle hot meat, grill grates, pots, and steam with confidence. Made with aramid and a neoprene coating, these waterproof, flame-resistant grill gloves offer strong grip, easy cleaning, and long-cuff forearm protection for BBQ, smoker, rotisserie, and kitchen use.

  • Extreme heat protection: Aramid + neoprene construction withstands temperatures up to 932°F for safer handling of high-heat tasks.

Product Description

This N-type RF coaxial attenuator delivers precise signal reduction from DC to 3GHz with a 2W power rating, making it a reliable choice for testing, measurement, and signal optimization. The copper-plated ternary construction ensures stable performance and long-lasting durability in any RF setup.

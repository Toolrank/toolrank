Product Description

Keep your furry friend hydrated and fed on the go with this innovative 2-in-1 Portable Dog Water Bottle. Designed with a unique cloud-shaped drinking bowl and twist-to-dispense mechanism, this travel-friendly water dispenser makes outdoor adventures with your pet easier than ever. The clever design combines a water bottle and food container in one compact unit, perfect for walks, hikes, and travel.

Key Features

  • 2-in-1 Dual Function: Combines water dispenser and food container in one portable unit. The bottom compartment stores dry food or treats, while the top holds fresh water for your pet.
  • Cloud-Shaped Drinking Bowl: Features a wide 10cm (3.9-inch) drinking trough designed to fit your dog's mouth structure naturally, making drinking comfortable and spill-free.
  • Twist-to-Dispense Mechanism: Simply twist the yellow control ring to switch between "on" and "off" positions. A gentle twist releases water; twist back to lock and prevent leaks.
  • Leak-Proof Silicone Seal: Equipped with a high-quality silicone gasket that creates a secure seal, ensuring no water escapes during transport in your bag or backpack.
  • Water Recovery System: Unique design allows unused water to flow back into the bottle when held upright, preventing waste and keeping your pet's drinking area tidy.
  • Transparent Bottle Body: Clear PET construction lets you easily monitor water levels so you know exactly when it's time for a refill.
  • Portable Carry Strap: Includes a convenient hanging rope for easy carrying - attach to your hand, backpack, or leash for hands-free convenience during walks.

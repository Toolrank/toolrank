Introducing the Baumr-AG BSS-45 Belt and Disc Sander. A compact and powerful linishing tool for your benchtop. It provides precision sanding and convenience in your workshop. This quality tool boasts reliability and durability for your artisanal sanding and shaping needs.

ADVANCED BRUSHLESS MOTOR
With a potent 250W motor, the BSS-45 is engineered to tackle your projects with ease. Its high rpm delivers relentless power to ensure efficient and effective sanding.

SOLID CONSTRUCTION, SUPERIOR PERFORMANCE
The cast aluminium housing guarantees a sturdy and reliable base, while the powder-coated steel wheel and belt guard add an extra layer of protection and corrosion resistance. DUAL SANDING OPTIONS

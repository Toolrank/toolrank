Details

Portable car booster with Powerbank function for recharging gadgets
As a jump starter, the model produces starting currents of up to 1000 A in nominal operating mode and up to 2000 A in peak mode. To connect to the car battery terminals, use an adapter with crocodile wires. Inside the Choetech TC0009 there is a lithium polymer (Li-Pol) battery with a capacity of 16000 mAh. The device can share the energy reserves accumulated in it with external gadgets, for which the design assumes the presence of two full-size USB ports and one USB type C connector.

USB A interfaces provide charging power at the level of 10 W and 18 W
Respectively, and through symmetrical USB C You can get up to 15 W of output power. Additionally, the model has a DC connector for powering low-voltage consumers (12 V). The model is assembled in a durable plastic case with dimensions of 270x80x125 mm. The booster is equipped with a built-in LED flashlight, located on the same panel with the connection interfaces.

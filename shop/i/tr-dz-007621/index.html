Details

Product Specifications:
Transparent silicone seal strip designed for doors or windows. The roll length is 16.5 feet and the total width is 25 mm, with an adhesive backing width of 10 mm. It is suitable for gaps up to 15 mm. The strip features a clear appearance that blends with different surface colors and remains less noticeable after application. The silicone material is described as having no irritating smell and can be cut to the required length.

Core Functions:
This self-adhesive weather stripping is made to help seal common gaps around doors and windows. It offers flexibility that does not easily affect normal opening and closing, and it can be used in extreme weather without easily breaking. The adhesive backing is designed for firm attachment and long-term use, helping address gap sealing needs around the home. Based on the provided information, it can help block wind, light, water, and dirt, while also reducing noise for a more comfortable indoor environment.

Suitable Scenarios:
Suitable for use on frameless sliding doors, glass doors, door bottoms, plastic-steel windows, aluminum windows, cupboards, wardrobes, furniture, sliding doors, and shower rooms. For installation, the surface should be cleaned completely and kept dry before application. Match the strip length to the gap, peel off the protective paper, apply it to the target area, and cut off any excess. In low temperatures, heating the adhesive backing slightly before use may help improve application.

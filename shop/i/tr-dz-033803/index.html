【Make Cooking Easier】Our cheese grater electric is equipped with a powerful motor, instantly slice, chop, grate, or shred your favorite ingredients, and even makes bread and cracker crumbs. Say goodbye to the waiting and enjoy your food as soon as possible with the help of the electric cheese grater.
【6 in 1 Electric Cheese Grater】The electric cheese grater comes with 5 interchangeable stainless steel blades to satisfy your different needs. The six groups of blades are thick wire blade, thin wire blade, slice blade, wavy blade, grinding blade and extra coarse julienne slicing blade. The upgraded extra coarse julienne slicing blade can be used to cut potato fries to make french fries.
【Upgrade in all Aspects】We have upgraded the starting method of this salad maker. The electric cheese grater is controlled by one button, easy and convenient to operate. 【Easier to Clean】The base of your cheese grater only needs to be wiped down with a damp cloth and should never be placed in water. And other detachable attachments can be cleaned with water, no annoying cleaning steps.
【Please Note】It is important to note that the extra coarse julienne blades can only be fitted to the smaller black rotor. The other five sets of blades are only suitable for mounting on the larger black rotor.

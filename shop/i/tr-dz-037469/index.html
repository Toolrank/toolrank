Details

Victrola Stream Onyx Turntable - Elevate your vinyl listening experience to new heights with the Victrola Stream Onyx, seamlessly integrated into your Sonos system. Certified by the Works with Sonos program, rest assured that the integration meets the highest standards set by Sonos. Modernise your vinyl setup with the Stream Onyx Manual Two-Speed Turntable featuring an Audio-Technica AT-VM95E or Ortofon OM5E Moving Magnet Cartridge Sonos compatibility.

Utilizing built-in Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) and Ethernet connectivity, along with the Victrola Stream app, effortlessly pair the Stream Onyx to your Sonos system and stream your favorite records throughout your home. Even without Sonos, the Stream Onyx functions flawlessly as a standard turntable with its RCA connection. Crafted from durable, low-resonance materials, the Stream Onyx smoothly plays back all your beloved 33 1/3 and 45 rpm records.

Just Add Speakers:
Whether it's a pair of bookshelf speakers or a Sonos system, the Victrola Stream Onyx is all you need to begin your vinyl journey. Featuring a built-in preamp and equipped with an Audio-Technica AT-VM95E or Ortofon OM5E Moving Magnet Cartridge, it's ready to play.

High-Quality Construction:
Experience the richness of vinyl with the solid low-resonance veneered MDF plinth of the Stream Onyx. The durable metal components ensure a smooth playback experience. Your records spin on a die-cast aluminum platter powered by a belt-drive motor, reducing vibration by placing the motor away from the platter. The aluminum tonearm, with its removable head shell and adjustable counterweight, allows you to precisely adjust tracking force to protect your vinyl during playback.

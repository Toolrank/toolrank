Product Description

This portable tyre inflator is a compact corded air pump designed to top up car, motorcycle, bicycle and sports ball tyres wherever you are. The tough ABS housing with an integrated carry handle keeps the unit protected and easy to move between vehicles. A backlit digital display shows live tyre pressure so you can inflate to your target and stop accurately instead of guessing. Simply connect the cigarette-lighter plug to your vehicle socket and let the single-cylinder pump do the work at home, in the garage or on a road trip.

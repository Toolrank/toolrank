Product Description

Secure, heat-proof bundling for tough jobs: these SUS 304 stainless steel cable ties (pack of 100) deliver reliable, long-lasting fastening for outdoor, automotive, marine, construction, DIY and more—no brittleness in sun, no loose parts, and a tight self-locking hold.

Key Features

  • Heavy-duty holding power: Wide 7.9mm band provides strong tensile/strap strength (about 120kg) for bundling cables, pipes, poles, and heavy items.
  • Extreme temperature resistance: Designed to handle harsh environments, rated from -80°C to +550°C (also described as up to 1700°F for high-heat applications such as hot auto parts/exhaust areas).
  • Self-locking ball mechanism: Internal steel-ball lock grips tighter as you pull—set your tension, then trim excess with diagonal pliers for a clean finish.
  • Corrosion waterproof durability: Stainless steel construction supports long-term exposure to water and demanding conditions; ideal for shipbuilding, mining, marine and outdoor installations.
  • UV abrasion resistant: Unlike plastic zip ties, metal ties won’t become brittle in sunlight and resist wear for extended service life.

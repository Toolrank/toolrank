Home Gym Adjustable Spin Bike

Take your training to the next level with the Home Gym Adjustable Spin Bike – built with a heavy-duty 13kg cast iron flywheel for a smooth, road-like ride. The belt-driven system and manual tension dial let you control resistance for anything from steady cardio to intense hill climbs, making it perfect for all fitness levels.

Designed for comfort and performance, it features a racing-style PU leather seat that adjusts both up/down and fore/aft, along with an adjustable handlebar to suit riders up to 2.0m tall. The aluminium alloy pedals with toe clips ensure secure footing during fast sprints or long endurance rides.

The high-quality tubular steel frame provides stability and durability, supporting users up to 125kg, while the precision balance-tested flywheel extends bearing and axle life, reducing maintenance and noise.

Stay on track with the built-in console displaying speed, distance, time, and calories burned.

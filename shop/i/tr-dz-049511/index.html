How to Use

  1. Cut fruits or vegetables into small pieces.

  2. Add ingredients and water/milk to the cup (≤80% full).

  3. Tighten the lid, press the power button to blend for 30‑45 seconds.

  4. Enjoy fresh juice or smoothies directly with the travel lid.

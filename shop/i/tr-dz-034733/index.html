Details

Move heavy loads with confidence using the Baumr-AG BTR-1000e Powered Hand Truck. Designed for light commercial transport tasks, it handles up to 260kg on flat ground and 200kg on slopes, making it ideal for construction sites, warehousing, deliveries and material handling.

A high-torque 400W brushless motor delivers smooth, controlled drive to the front wheels, with variable speeds up to 6km/h forward and 2km/h in reverse. Electronic braking automatically slows the trolley, while a locking mechanical brake adds extra security when working on inclines.

Built around a robust steel and aluminium frame, the BTR-1000e features adjustable support rails from 590mm to 910mm to suit bulky or awkward loads. Large pneumatic tyres improve traction and stability across gravel, kerbs and uneven ground.

Powered by a 40V 7.5Ah lithium battery, it delivers up to 4 hours of typical runtime and recharges in under 4 hours. The battery compartment can accommodate two batteries, allowing you to manually switch over to a spare for an even longer runtime. With IP54-rated protection and a foldable design for transport and storage, it's a practical, hard-working solution for everyday heavy lifting.

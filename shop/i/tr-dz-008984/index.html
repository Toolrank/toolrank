Product Description

Give vegetables, tomatoes, peppers, and ornamental flowers the room they need to develop strong, healthy roots with these 14 x 16 cm non-woven fabric grow bags. Breathable, lightweight, and space-saving, they support balanced moisture management and make transplanting seedlings easier while helping maintain an organized home garden, greenhouse, or nursery.

Key Features

  • Breathable Non-Woven Material: Provides a sturdy alternative to plastic while allowing air to reach the roots for healthier plant growth.
  • Supports Root Development: Encourages a well-branched root system that can improve nutrient uptake and promote vigorous seedlings.
  • Easy Transplanting: Flexible grow bags help you move young plants safely with less disturbance to delicate roots.
  • Balanced Moisture Management: The permeable fabric allows excess water to pass through, helping maintain a suitable moisture balance.
  • Versatile Garden Use: Ideal for growing tomatoes, peppers, vegetables, and ornamental plants before transplanting them into the ground.
  • Space-Saving Bulk Pack: Lightweight bags take up little storage space and provide plenty of containers for organized gardening projects.

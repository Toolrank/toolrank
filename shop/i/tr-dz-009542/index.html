Product Description

Start your indoor garden the easy way with this 5-pack of 30-cell peat pots seed starter trays. Perfect for germinating flowers, herbs, and vegetable saplings like tomatoes and cucumbers, these biodegradable seedling pots promote healthy root growth, reduce transplant shock, and break down naturally in soil for an eco-friendly gardening experience.

Key Features

  • Biodegradable Eco-Friendly: Made from organic peat, the trays break down naturally after planting, leaving zero waste behind.
  • Minimizes Transplant Shock: Transplant the whole pot directly into the ground or a larger planter, protecting delicate roots during the move.
  • Promotes Aeration: The breathable peat material improves air circulation around roots for stronger, healthier seedlings.
  • Easy to Separate: Trays can be quickly cut into individual peat pots with a pair of scissors, giving you flexible planting options.
  • 10 Cells Per Tray: Each tray offers 10 individual cells, giving you 50 total starter cells across the 5-pack for multiple seed varieties.

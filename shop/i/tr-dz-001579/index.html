Superior Quality, Invisible Protection

Crafted with a high-end, water-based formula, this invisible waterproof glue creates a powerful film that insulates against air, water, and moisture. Multiple overlapping layers form a permanent bond, ensuring long-lasting, durable, and weather-resistant waterproofing for roofs, bathrooms, and more. Simple Application in Three Easy Steps

  • Brush directly onto clean, dry surfaces such as tiles, floor tiles, and wall tiles.
  • Allow the product to penetrate the base layer for a few minutes until no liquid remains.
  • Brush on 2-3 additional layers to strengthen the effect. A waterproof layer forms after 24-48 hours of curing and becomes even stronger over time.

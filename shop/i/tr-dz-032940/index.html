Details

Experience cloud-like support and comfort with the Ergopedic Pocket Spring Mattress!

The mattress' lower five-zone pocket spring design targets the five most important areas of the body - the feet, the knees, the lower back, the head and the shoulders. This unique pocket-spring design provides both pressure-relief and support by conforming to these essential five contours of the body. The pocket-spring design also ensures zero-partner disturbance by minimizing motion disturbance from tossing and turning, ultimately allowing the user to experience an optimum sleep experience.
The mattress is of a medium-firm design and is encased in a luxurious 300GSM knitted cotton cover that is ultra-soft to the touch, while the micro-quilting design of the cover ensures its durability and resilience over time. Alongside this, the durable side-handles allow for easy positioning of the mattress.

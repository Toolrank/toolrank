Product Description

Experience futuristic fun with our Hand-Controlled UFO Mini Drone! This intelligent sensing toy offers an exciting and interactive flying experience for kids and adults alike. Simply toss it in the air to start, and then guide its flight with hand gestures. Equipped with multi-directional infrared sensors, it intelligently avoids obstacles from the left, right, and bottom, ensuring safe indoor play. The drone features vibrant, colorful LED lights that make it captivating to watch, especially in dimmer environments.

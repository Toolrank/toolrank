Simplecom SE502C SATA M.2 SSD to USB-C Enclosure USB 3.2 Gen1 5Gbps

SE502C is a compact and portable enclosure for SATA protocol based M.2 SSD. This enclosure features USB-C interface and complies with USB 3.2 Gen1 standard, which supports data transfer speeds of up to 5Gbps.

The design of this enclosure is lightweight and portable, and it can be easily slipped into your pocket or bag. The durable aluminium casing provides heat dissipation to ensure your drive doesn't get overheated while you're using. The tool-free design of the enclosure allows for quick and easy installation of SSDs without the need for any tools.It's USB powered, plug and play, allowing it to connect to a computer without the need for extra power or driver software, simplifies the user experience.

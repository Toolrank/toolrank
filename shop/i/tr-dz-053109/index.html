Details

The Gecko 139L DC Fridge and Freezer is the ideal upright refrigeration solution for caravans, motorhomes, camper trailers, 4WD touring, marine use, off-grid cabins and tiny homes. Designed to run on both 12V and 24V DC power, it delivers dependable cooling performance wherever your adventures take you.

With a practical upright layout, the 42L freezer sits above the generous 97L refrigerator, making it easy to organise frozen foods, fresh produce and chilled drinks. The freezer maintains temperatures from -10 degrees Celsius to -18 degrees Celsius, while the fridge compartment operates between 0 degrees Celsius and 8 degrees Celsius, even in ambient temperatures of up to 38 degrees Celsius.

Inside, adjustable steel wire shelves, a spacious crisper drawer, bottle shelf, condiment shelf and dedicated egg holder provide flexible storage for all your essentials. Thick insulation, quality compressor technology and a solid insulated door help maintain efficient cooling while reducing power consumption.

Built for Australia's demanding conditions, the Gecko features ActivSeal technology with an antimicrobial door seal coating designed to help inhibit the growth of germs. Quiet operation at just 42 dB(A) makes it ideal for overnight use, while the IP24 rating and ability to operate on inclines of up to 30 degrees make it well suited to life on the road or on the water.

Supplied with a 12V and 24V DC auto socket power cable and key, the Gecko 139L DC Fridge and Freezer combines generous capacity, reliable performance and practical storage in one compact upright design, making it the perfect companion for every adventure.

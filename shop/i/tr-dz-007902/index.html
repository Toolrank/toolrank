Product Description

Make every step safer with these 8 x 30in non-slip carpet stair treads, designed to protect kids, elders, and pets from slipping on indoor stairs. The soft polyester surface with a reusable self-adhesive TPE backing grips firmly on wood, tile, and glass stairs while reducing noise and keeping feet warm on cold steps.

Key Features

  • Safety First: The TPE non-slip backing provides strong grip on dry indoor floors, helping kids, seniors, and dogs or cats go up and down stairs without fear of slipping or falling, and it won't scuff your floors.
  • Soft 3-Layer Construction: Featuring a wear-resistant polyester surface, a nonwoven buffer layer, and a non-slip bottom seamlessly bonded together for a soft, quiet, and stable feel underfoot.
  • Noise Reduction & Comfort: The proper thickness cushions every step, dampens stair noise, and keeps feet away from cold stairs, creating a quieter, more relaxing home environment.

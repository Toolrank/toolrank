Pet Support Harness for Senior and Disabled Dogs - Adjustable Chest and Back Support Leash

Product Overview

This pet support harness is specifically designed to assist senior and disabled dogs during their daily activities. It provides adjustable chest and back support, ensuring even force distribution around the dog's body without causing discomfort. The harness features a comfortable design with breathable mesh fabric and durable materials to ensure long-lasting use.

Key Features

  • Even Force Distribution: The harness distributes force evenly around the dog's body, preventing any discomfort or strain.
  • Adjustable Design: The chest and back support can be adjusted to fit different sizes of dogs comfortably.
  • Breathable Material: Made from mesh fabric that allows air circulation, keeping your dog cool and comfortable.
  • Durable Construction: Constructed with high-quality materials like nylon and canvas for long-lasting durability.
  • Reflective Straps: Ensures visibility in low-light conditions for added safety.
  • Easy to Use: Simple design with secure buckles and handles for easy handling.

Product Specifications

Usage Instructions

  1. Measure your dog's back length, waist circumference, and front-back leg distance to determine the correct size.
  2. Place the harness on your dog, ensuring it fits snugly but comfortably.
  3. Adjust the straps as needed to provide proper support.
  4. Secure the buckles and handle for safe and easy handling.

Cleaning and Maintenance

To maintain the quality and hygiene of the harness, it is recommended to clean it regularly. Use a soft cloth and mild detergent to wipe down the harness. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive materials that may damage the fabric. Allow the harness to dry naturally in a shaded area.

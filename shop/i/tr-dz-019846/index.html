Product Description:
This Bear-Shaped Round-Hole Hanging Insulated Cotton Nest features a three-dimensional spherical design with a fully enclosed cavity. Hand-woven from thick, plush coral fleece, it offers excellent coverage and a secure, enclosed space that provides a sense of safety, helping parrots and hamsters stay warm and comfortable throughout the winter. The nest features a built-in rigid plastic support frame that maintains its shape, ensuring it remains full and plump even with prolonged use—it won’t collapse or flatten. It comes with metal screw clips that securely fasten to the wire of the birdcage, preventing the nest from flipping over and trapping your pet. The colorful, contrasting plush fabric is resistant to chewing and pecking, and the entire unit is machine washable without fading. The round entrance allows for easy access and is suitable for parakeets, cockatiels, budgerigars, and sun conures. It can also serve as a winter resting nest for small exotic pets like degu and hamsters, providing both winter warmth and a space for daily rest and breeding.

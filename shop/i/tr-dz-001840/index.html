Transform Your Cabinets with Precision and Quiet Elegance

Elevate your kitchen or living space with our premium semi-concealed soft-close cabinet hinges. Designed for both style and function, these hinges add a touch of sophistication to any cabinetry while ensuring every close is whisper-quiet.

Key Features You'll Love

  • Fully Adjustable: Three screws on the soft-close axis allow you to fine-tune overlay, depth, and height—up to 8mm adjustment range—for a custom fit.

  • Easy Installation: The removable mounting plate lets you attach the hinge to the door and cabinet separately, then simply press to connect—no special tools or repairs needed.

Perfect for Frameless Cabinets

These hinges are specifically designed for frameless cabinet hardware, offering a clean, concealed look while providing robust performance. With a maximum opening angle of 105°, you get maximum access to your cabinet contents.

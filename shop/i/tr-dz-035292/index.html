Details

Livestock Pet, and Garden Grade Diatomaceous Earth Mined here in Australia, mainly used as an additive to livestock, poultry, pet feed, dusting pens and adding to grains for pest control and worming.

Diatomaceous Earth can be used for dusting green houses, dusting fruit, flower and vegetable crops. Can be done even on day of harvesting crops with no harm to animals or humans as it is pure and organic. Don't dust when Bees are present.

Please Note: Although this fossil shell flour is food grade, it has not been sterilised, hence is it not suitable for human consumption.

Specifications:

  • Particle size: Ranges from 80% Super Fine Grade with some 20% particles up to 2mm - 20 to 200 UM size particles.

Suggestions For Use:

Around the Home

  • Outside areas – approx. 250g – 500g per 100 square metres (dry powder)
  • As a liquid spray – approx. 250g per 4 litres water
  • Lightly sprinkle in carpets

Livestock and Pets

Feed rates for livestock and domestic pets vary due to the differences in farm practices and animal size. Suggested feed rates are as follows:

Dairy and Beef Cattle

  • Initial Rate: 5% of dry feed per day for 90days (300g)
  • Ongoing: 2% of dry feed per day (130g)

Goats, Sheep, Pigs

  • Initial Rate: 10% of dry feed per day for 90 days
  • Ongoing: 5% of dry feed or grain per day

Horses, Donkeys

  • Initial Rate: ½ to 1 cup in feed per day
  • Ongoing: 2% per day

Chickens, Ducks

  • Initial Rate: 15% of dry feed per day
  • Ongoing: 5-15% per day

Large dogs (>50kg)

  • 1-2 heaped tablespoons per day

Large dogs (>35kg)

  • 1 tablespoon per day

Medium dogs (15-35kg)

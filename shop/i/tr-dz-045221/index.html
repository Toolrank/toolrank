How to Use

  1. Add ground coffee into the powder bowl.
  2. Place the water filter mesh on top.
  3. Insert the filter basket.
  4. Attach to the water tank.
  5. Fill the water tank with hot water.
  6. Turn counterclockwise to open the valve.
  7. Press to extract the coffee.
  8. Your delicious cup of coffee is ready.

Heatless Hair Curling Set with Scrunchie and Hair Clip – Silk Curling Ribbon

Achieve perfect curls without the damage of heat with this heatless hair curling set. The luxurious silk curling ribbon is gentle on your hair and easy to use, while the included scrunchie and hair clip provide extra convenience for styling. Ideal for all hair types!

Features:

  • Heatless curling ribbon for natural, damage-free curls.
  • Soft silk material for gentle and comfortable use.
  • Includes a scrunchie and hair clip for added styling versatility.
  • Lightweight and portable – perfect for home or travel.
  • Suitable for all hair types and lengths.

Usage Instructions:

Place the curling ribbon over your head like a headband. Wrap sections of your damp or dry hair around the ribbon and secure the ends with the scrunchie. Leave it overnight or for a few hours to achieve soft, heatless curls.

Care Tips:

Hand wash the curling ribbon and scrunchie with mild detergent. Avoid exposing the ribbon to high heat or sharp objects to maintain its durability.

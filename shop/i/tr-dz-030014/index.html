Details

Bring your water features to life with this versatile 16W submersible fountain pump, designed to provide reliable water circulation and captivating lighting. Whether you are maintaining a healthy aquatic environment for your pets or adding a decorative touch to your garden, this pump offers the performance and aesthetic appeal needed to elevate your space. At the heart of this unit is a powerful yet energy-efficient motor capable of delivering a maximum flow rate of 800 liters per hour. This strong output ensures that water keeps moving, which is essential for preventing stagnation in ponds and providing necessary oxygenation for fish in aquariums. Despite its power, the pump is engineered to operate quietly, ensuring that the sound of flowing water remains soothing rather than mechanical. Visual impact is a key feature of this pump, thanks to the integrated 12-color LED light system. As the water flows, the lights illuminate the spray, creating a mesmerizing display that is especially effective after dark. The automatic color cycling adds a dynamic element to your decor, making it a fantastic centerpiece for evening gatherings or simply a relaxing nightlight for your living space. Customization is easy with the included nozzle attachments. You can choose between the mushroom head for a classic, dome-shaped water flow or the blossom head for a more intricate, flower-like spray. This flexibility allows you to adapt the pump to different fountain styles, from traditional tiered statuary to modern minimalist bowls. Durability and safety are paramount in water equipment. This pump is fully submersible and built to withstand continuous operation in both fresh and saltwater environments. Its compact size allows for discreet placement in tight spaces, while the sturdy construction ensures longevity, making it a dependable choice for year-round use in your home or garden.

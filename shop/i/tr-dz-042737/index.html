Travel Safely and Comfortably with Your Feathered Friend

Ensure your bird or small pet travels in comfort and style with this lightweight and portable Clear View Bird Carrier. Designed with both pet visibility and owner convenience in mind, this carrier provides a secure and well-ventilated environment for trips to the vet, visiting friends, or short journeys.

Key Features:

  • Panoramic Clear View: Transparent front and side panels allow your pet to see their surroundings, reducing stress, while letting you easily monitor their well-being.
  • Excellent Ventilation: Features multiple mesh panels and metal grommet vents (approx. 2cm diameter) to ensure ample airflow and keep the interior fresh.
  • Comfortable Perching: Includes a sturdy wooden perch (approx. 25.5cm long) that attaches securely inside, providing a natural resting spot for your bird.
  • Easy Access & Cleaning: A large zippered mesh side door (approx. 17cm x 24cm) allows for easy entry and exit. Comes with a removable bottom tray (approx. 22cm x 30.5cm) for hassle-free cleaning.
  • Portable Design: Equipped with a comfortable top carry handle and an adjustable, detachable shoulder strap (adjustable range approx. 75cm - 135cm) for versatile carrying options.
  • Durable Construction: Made from sturdy Two Tone Oxford fabric for lasting use.
  • Versatile Use: Suitable for parrots (like Cockatiels, Conures), pigeons, and other small pets such as rabbits and chinchillas (always check dimensions for suitability).

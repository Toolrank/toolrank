Details

The best spot for a bathroom is sometimes the worst spot for plumbing, or gravity. But thanks to the Proteges wide range of macerator pumps this isnt a problem. You can save a significant amount of expense by connecting a small diameter pipe to an existing system; making it ideal for adding a bathroom or en-suite to basements, garage conversions, granny flats, workshops and rooms with concrete slab floors.

The compact Protege Shower Plus is designed for low water level shower trays and can connect with a wash basin.

The Shower Plus runs quietly thanks to the smooth 200W copper wound motor and rubber feet. It is capable of pumping up to eight metres vertically and 80 metres horizontally.

Connecting the Protege Shower Plus is straightforward with quality instructions and all necessary fittings included. And, best of all, once in place, you can forget about it. Not only is it quiet; it's automatically activated, too.

Bibal Insole Full Whole Insoles Shoe Inserts 3-Size Combo Arch Support Foot Pads

Whether you plan on walking a few miles per day, playing an active sport, or standing all day for work, finding the right shoe insoles is vital for everyone.

Some insoles in the market are too rigid and may not provide the desired comfort, potentially limiting mobility and movement.

Our Full Insole has a 3.5mm ultra-slim forefoot design made of resilient urethane foam, with the top covered by mesh fabric. The urethane gel heel pad offers excellent cushioning, and the injected urethane arch support gives structural balance, along with excellent shock absorption and grip, allowing your feet to handle impact over rough, rocky, or uneven surfaces. The arch support section itself is highly durable, returning to its original state even after being stepped on by a vehicle.

These insoles are designed and made in South Korea with a genuine interest in enhancing your comfort. We strive for the best functional insoles by selecting quality materials.

The arch support fits various arch shapes by utilizing individual body weight. It is suitable for those who have an arch height of less than 3 cm.

How to use / proper care:

For the best fit, remove your existing insole and replace it with the Bibal Insole.
Place the insole, holding it in place as you slide your foot in.

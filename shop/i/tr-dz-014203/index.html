Details

This children's password piggy bank is a fun and educational tool that combines entertainment with financial literacy. Made of sturdy materials, it supports both coin and paper money storage: the top features a coin slot, and inserting paper money into the front bill slot triggers an automatic roll-in with accompanying music. It is equipped with a password keypad (default password 0000, modifiable by pressing the "*" key) entering the password automatically rotates the cabinet door open. Indicator lights and sound effects are also included to enhance the interactive experience.

By simulating the deposit and withdrawal process, it helps children develop savings habits. Its safe and engaging mechanical design is tailored to children's cognitive abilities, and the variety of available colors make it suitable as both a toy and a charming desktop decoration. It serves as a thoughtful gift to introduce money management concepts and foster a sense of responsibility.

24-Inch Black Fireproof Mat for Fire Pit and BBQ - Silicone Coated

Product Description

Protect your deck, patio, or lawn with our 24-Inch Black Fireproof Mat, designed for safe outdoor fire pit and barbecue use. Crafted with a premium liquid silicone-coated fiberglass layer, this mat offers exceptional heat resistance, shielding surfaces from embers, sparks, and grease. Its sleek black design blends seamlessly with any outdoor setup, making it an essential accessory for camping, barbecues, or backyard gatherings.

Key Features

  • Compact and Portable: 24-inch (61 cm) diameter folds easily for storage and transport in a carry bag.
  • Waterproof and Easy to Clean: Non-stick silicone coating resists grease and spills, allowing simple cleaning with soap and water.
  • Durable Design: Multi-layered construction with stitched edges prevents tearing, curling, or cracking under heat.
  • Versatile Use: Ideal for wood-burning fire pits, gas fire pits, charcoal grills, and BBQs from 22 to 24 inches.

Details

Elevate your work to the next level with the exclusive Baumr-AG Ultimate Mobile Adjustable Scaffold that features a Trap Door, Guard Rails and an Outrigger Wheel Set. This heavy-duty powder-coated steel unit has been designed to provide the safest and easiest solution to raising your working height. In fact, this 1.9m unit has been designed so that it can be effortlessly assembled and adjusted by a single person working on their own. This is largely thanks to the fast and secure pin loaded platform lock system, allowing users to incrementally raise and lower the work platform between 70cm and 187cm. The scaffold comes equipped with a trap door hatch for easy, convenient access.

The sturdy ½ plywood platform provides plenty of surface space for you and a range of your equipment, while at the same time, its been purposely designed slim enough to fit through Australian standard doorways. Its fitted with large 5 polyurethane lockable castor wheels that ensure optimal manoeuvrability, without the scuff marks. Four removable Outrigger Wheels effectively extend the width of the scaffold to provide optimal stability when working at heights. They can also be fitted at a range of 2 height increments to best fit the site.

This Baumr-AG scaffold features handy isolated height adjustment that allows it to be placed on uneven surfaces. Working on stairways, split-levels or outdoor slopes is no problem. Execute your work with total peace of mind knowing that eight locking pins are safeguarding the platform and castors in place. The removable guard rail is easy to install and provides a safer working space that is secured with locking pins. It also has an inward opening gate for safe and easy access.

Keep Your Herbs Fresher for Longer with the Innovative Herb Saver

Stop throwing away wilted, unused herbs! Our Fresh Herb Keeper is the revolutionary kitchen tool designed to significantly extend the life of your fresh herbs, keeping them vibrant and flavourful for up to three times longer than traditional storage methods. Reduce food waste and enjoy the taste of fresh herbs in your meals every day.

  • Extends Herb Freshness: This innovative pod creates the perfect hydrating environment for fresh herbs. By keeping the stems in a small amount of water, it allows herbs like parsley, coriander, mint, dill, and asparagus to stay crisp and fresh for weeks.
  • Easy to Use & Refill: Simply place your washed herbs into the pod, add fresh water to the reservoir through the convenient filler cap, and store it in your refrigerator door. Remember to change the water every 3-5 days for best results.
  • Hard Shell Protection: The rigid, clear shell protects your delicate herbs from being crushed or bruised by other items in your fridge, preserving their structure and flavour.
  • Fits in Your Refrigerator Door: The slim, upright design is perfectly sized to fit neatly into most standard refrigerator doors, keeping your herbs accessible without taking up valuable shelf space.
  • See Your Herbs Clearly: The transparent, crystal-clear body allows you to see the condition of your herbs at a glance, so you always know what you have on hand.

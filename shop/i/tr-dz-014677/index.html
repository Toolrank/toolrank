This 380-piece self-drilling drywall anchor and screw assortment kit is designed for fast, clean, and reliable wall mounting. Made from premium carbon steel with color zinc plating, these drywall anchors offer rust resistance, long-lasting durability, and strong holding power for everyday installation jobs.

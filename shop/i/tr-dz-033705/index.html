Safety Warning – Tip-Over Hazard (TV Furniture)

Children have died from furniture and TV tip-overs. Secure both the television and this unit to a wall using suitable anchoring devices (supplied or appropriate for your wall type), and check anchors regularly. Use approved TV anti-tip straps or brackets, and position the TV centrally with the base fully supported. Never allow children to climb, stand, or hang on the unit. Keep remotes and toys out of reach to discourage climbing. Route cables so they cannot be pulled, place heavier equipment on lower shelves, and keep doors and drawers closed (open only one at a time, if applicable). Set on a level surface and re-secure anchors after moving or reconfiguring.

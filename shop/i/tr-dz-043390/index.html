Illuminate your tasks with this LED Sensor Clip-On Book Light, a versatile and rechargeable light perfect for reading, working, or night fishing. Featuring 5*LED white light (strong/weak), 4*LED red light (strong/weak/flashing), and 2835LED, this light offers 100-300 lumens with a range of up to 10 meters.

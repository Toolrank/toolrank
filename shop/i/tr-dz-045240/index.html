Adjustable Double Edge Safety Razor Kit

Experience a superior, customized shave with our vintage-inspired Adjustable Safety Razor. Designed for both men and women, this reusable razor combines classic styling with modern engineering, allowing you to dial in the perfect shave for your skin type and hair thickness. Move away from expensive cartridges and enjoy a close, comfortable, and eco-friendly shave.

  • 8-Setting Adjustable Dial: Easily customize the blade aggressiveness from mild (setting 1) to aggressive (setting 8). Perfect for sensitive skin, daily shaves, or tackling coarse beards.
  • Effortless Blade Changes: Features a convenient Twist-To-Open (TTO) butterfly mechanism. A simple twist of the handle opens the head for safe and quick blade replacement.
  • Durable Metal Construction: Crafted from a high-quality metal alloy with a non-slip textured handle, providing excellent grip and control, even when wet.
  • Complete Shaving Set: This kit includes everything you need to start: one adjustable razor, a matching stand for proper drying and storage, and a pack of 5 sharp double edge blades.
  • Economical & Eco-Friendly: A sustainable alternative to disposable plastic razors. Double edge blades are significantly more affordable and produce less waste.

How to Use

  1. Twist the bottom of the handle to open the butterfly doors on the razor head.
  2. Carefully place a double edge blade onto the head.
  3. Twist the handle in the opposite direction to securely close the doors.
  4. Adjust the dial to your preferred setting and enjoy a smooth shave.

Product Title

Hidden Hinge Drilling Jig Template Locator for Cabinet Door Installation, Adjustable Punch Positioner Tool with Multi-Spec Screw Hole Guidance for Woodworking Projects

Product Description

Achieving perfectly aligned concealed hinge cups on cabinet doors can make the difference between a wobbly, misaligned door and a smooth, professional finish. This hidden hinge drilling jig is designed for woodworkers who demand repeatable accuracy without the guesswork. The rectangular metal body, finished in black with silver adjustment components and gold knobs, features clear scales and multiple drilling positions. It accommodates 35mm and 26mm concealed hinges, offers 2-6mm hinge hole edge distance adjustment, supports board thickness up to 30mm, and allows drilling depth control from 0-25mm. The jig also provides multi-specification screw hole positioning for 42mm, 45mm, 48mm, and 52mm hinge screw patterns. An optional dust extraction bracket can be attached to reduce airborne debris during operation. Whether you are a professional cabinetmaker or a serious DIY enthusiast, this tool helps you measure twice, cut once—or in this case, drill once with confidence. The included circular locators and drill bits work together to keep every bore consistent across multiple doors. Setup is straightforward: align the jig, lock the settings, and drill. The metal construction resists wear from repeated use, while the adjustable knobs let you switch between hinge sizes and edge distances quickly. Ideal for kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, bathroom vanities, and custom furniture projects, this hinge drilling locator transforms a fiddly task into a controlled, efficient process. Its compact size makes it easy to store and transport between job sites.

Unwind and experience a rejuvenating nights' sleep with the Royal Comfort Comforpedic 5-Zone Mattress!
The Comforpedic Mattress has been designed using traditional bonnell-spring construction finished with several layers of high-density foam.
The unique 5-zone design of the mattress provides both pressure-relief and support. The upper foam layers have been designed to adjust to the body and provide a customized and relaxing sleep surface, while the lower bonnell spring layer has been designed to offer long-lasting support.
The mattress comes in a contemporary, yet classic, white colour finished with a black trim edge design. The cool-touch quilted cover and the breathable wave foam pillow-top layer ensures that heat does not get trapped for the maximum in comfort.

Details

The Baumr-AG BPR960 Site-Mate Series Tracked Flatbed Wheelbarrow takes the hard work out of transporting almost anything. From farming to construction and agriculture to landscaping, the BPR960's is designed to do all the heavy lifting and cut your job time in half!

RELIABLE POWER

The BPR960 motorised wheelbarrow is equipped with a powerful 6.5HP 208cc Briggs Stratton CR950 engine that features a forged crankshaft and a Dura-Bore cast iron sleeve providing optimal performance and durability.

ULTRA-TOUGH BUILD WITH A HUGE CAPACITY

Thick powder-coated steel and a huge 300kg load capacity makes the BPR960 perfect for transporting everything from heavy loads of logs, bricks, soil and cement. The tracked barrow is also equipped with extendable and removable side panels, which allows you to increase the platform loading capacity for various applications.

ALL TERRAIN TRACK DRIVE SYSTEM

180mm tracks provide this powered wheelbarrow with excellent stability and control with serious grip and efficient traction across uneven and difficult terrain while minimising ground damage. It will literally go just about anywhere that you can walk!

WELL BALANCED

An even balance allows for a full load to be tipped by the operator. With the BPR960 motorised wheel barrow you can work faster and smarter with significantly less risk of fatigue or injuries, making it ideal for landscapers, builders and rural landowners.

VERSATILE DESIGN

The sides of the flatbed tray can be shifted outwards to accept a larger load. Railings above these sides allow for bigger loads of large items like logs to be transported. The sides can also be removed entirely to accommodate long items like beams and fence posts.

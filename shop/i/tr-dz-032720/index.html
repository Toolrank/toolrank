Introducing the Brown Clog Jute Cat Scratcher — a playful 2-in-1 feline station modelled after a classic clog slipper, combining a sheltered hideaway den with a full-size natural jute scratching surface in one charming object. Measuring 59 × 38 × 28 cm, its generous interior lets a cat climb in, turn around, and settle down, while the textured jute inlay across the footbed invites instinctive scratching the moment they step inside.

Product Description

Stay connected to your little one with the SM650 5-Inch No-WiFi Baby Monitor, designed for simple, private nursery monitoring without internet access or app downloads. Its FHSS 2.4GHz wireless connection, long-range coverage, VOX-ECO power saving, infrared night vision, two-way audio, temperature alerts and feeding reminders help parents check, comfort and care for baby with confidence.

Key Features

  • No WiFi or app required: Plug-and-play operation with FHSS 2.4GHz wireless transmission helps provide a stable, private connection for everyday home monitoring.
  • Extended monitoring range: Enjoy up to 300 m of barrier-free outdoor range and approximately 30 m of indoor coverage, depending on the environment.
  • VOX-ECO power saving: The parent monitor screen stays off when the room is quiet and wakes automatically when baby makes noise, offering up to 15 hours of standby time in ECO mode and up to 8 hours in normal operation.
  • Clear day and night viewing: The 5-inch full-colour TFT LCD displays smooth 480×272 video at up to 25 fps, with automatic infrared night vision and 2× digital zoom for easier checking in low light.
  • Comfort and care alerts: Monitor room temperature with high and low temperature alerts, set 1-, 2-, 3-, 4- or 6-hour feeding reminders, and play built-in gentle lullabies to help soothe baby.
  • Flexible family monitoring: Use real-time two-way audio, remote horizontal and vertical camera adjustment, adjustable brightness and volume, and pair one parent receiver with up to four cameras for multiple rooms or children.

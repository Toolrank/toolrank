Details

This foldable guitar stand offers a practical and space-saving solution for safely storing your acoustic, classical, or electric guitar. Built with a strong iron A-frame, it is lightweight yet stable, ideal for both home use and stage performance. The support arms that cradle the guitar body are wrapped in soft foam padding to prevent scratches, while the A-frame top features a rubber buffer to protect the guitar neck from scuffing. The wide base and angled design ensure better weight distribution, making the stand less prone to tipping. Thanks to its quick-fold hinge, this guitar stand can be folded flat for easy storage or transport—perfect for musicians on the go.

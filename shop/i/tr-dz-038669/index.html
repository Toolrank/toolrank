Details

This all-in-one aquarium cleaning kit provides an efficient way to keep your fish tank clean and your aquatic life healthy. The multifunctional design serves as a water changer, gravel cleaner, and algae scrubber, making it perfect for routine tank maintenance. The manual pump system allows for easy water siphoning without the need for electricity. With an anti-suction mesh cover, it prevents small fish and shrimp from being accidentally drawn into the system. The kit also features strong suction cups for hands-free operation and a multi-purpose cleaning brush to help remove debris from tank walls and the substrate. Suitable for both freshwater and saltwater aquariums, this cleaning kit ensures your fish have a clean and safe environment.

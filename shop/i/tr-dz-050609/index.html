EPE Protective Foam Wrap Roll Instruction Manual 1. Product Overview This white EPE foam wrap roll is shockproof cushioning packaging material, available in two sizes: 1mm thickness, 30cm width, 30m length and 3mm thickness, 50cm width, 5m length. Constructed from lightweight elastic polyethylene foam, it offers anti-collision, shock absorption and anti-scratch protection for fragile goods during transportation, moving and long-term storage. 2. Core Specifications Optional Specifications: Size 1: 1mm Thickness, 30cm Width, 30m Length Size 2: 3mm Thickness, 50cm Width, 5m Length Material: Closed-cell EPE polyethylene foam Color: White Core Features: Two thickness & dimension choices, elastic shock absorbing cushioning, scratch-free soft surface, lightweight foldable, easy to cut to custom size, residue-free, waterproof and dustproof Application: Household moving packaging, fragile glass & ceramic wrapping, furniture surface anti-scratch protection, handicraft & antique storage, electronic accessory shipping, small commercial product packing 3. Usage Instructions Unroll the foam wrap and measure according to the size of the goods, cut the foam sheet into required size with scissors. Wrap the foam evenly around fragile items, add multiple layers for corners and protruding parts that are vulnerable to collision, then fix with packing tape. Choose 1mm thin foam for surface anti-scratch protection of furniture and flat ornaments; select 3mm thick foam for fragile cups, ceramics and crafts needing stronger shock resistance. Place wrapped items into cartons, fill gaps with leftover foam scraps to reduce internal shaking during transit. After unpacking, flatten the unused foam sheets and roll them up for repeated packaging next time. 4. Cleaning & Maintenance Guide Wipe dust on foam surface with dry cloth, avoid washing with plenty of water, long-term damp environment will affect foam elasticity. Store foam rolls in cool dry ventilated space, avoid prolonged direct sunlight exposure, ultraviolet rays will cause foam aging and embrittlement. Do not place heavy objects on top of unused foam rolls, long-term heavy compression will make foam lose elasticity and cannot recover. Keep away from sharp blades when storing, prevent puncturing the foam sheet and affecting secondary use. 5. Safety Precautions EPE foam is flammable plastic material, keep far away from open flames, high-temperature heat sources to avoid fire risks. Do not use this foam wrap as long-term cushion bedding for human body, it is only designed for commodity packaging protection. If the foam becomes brittle, cracked and loses elasticity after long-term storage, stop using it for high-value fragile goods packaging.

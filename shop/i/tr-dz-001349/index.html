BUEN Golf Automatic Ball Return Wedge Practice Pad (PM015)
This is an indoor / outdoor portable putter practice mat designed specifically for golf enthusiasts, aimed at helping you improve your putter skills and reduce your score at any time and any place.
Main functions and features:
Realistic grass surface: Using advanced friction-oriented surface design, it perfectly simulates the ball speed and rolling sensation of a real golf green, providing you with a realistic golfing experience.
Gravity automatic return ball system: built-in gravity feeding ball return mechanism, when you successfully push the ball into the hole, the ball will automatically roll back to the starting position, no need to bend down to pick up the ball, so you can continue to practice without interruption, greatly improving training efficiency.
Precise distance marking: There are clear white guiding lines and digital distance markings (such as 2, 4, 6, 8, etc.) on the mat to help you practice precise distance control and gradually master the force of the putter at different distances.
Removable golf hole plug-in: equipped with removable cup-shaped golf hole plug-in, you can choose regular size or professional size for practice according to your needs, to meet the training needs of different stages.
Portability and durability: The product is made of high-quality wear-resistant materials and has a non-slip design on the bottom, ensuring that it can be firmly placed on any smooth surface. Its light weight makes it very suitable for use at home, in the office, on the balcony, or on trips.

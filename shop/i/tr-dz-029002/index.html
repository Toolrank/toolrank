Product Description

This solid rubberwood side table brings a warm, natural touch to any corner of your home. Crafted from premium rubberwood instead of particle board or density board, it offers a sturdy, eco-friendly build with no unpleasant odors. The heart-shaped top with a raised rim keeps your drinks, small ornaments, or daily essentials from slipping off, while the vintage gourd-shaped carved pillar and thickened round base add a charming, stable character. As the saying goes, good things come in small packages, and this little table proves it—offering beauty, function, and durability without taking up precious floor space. Whether you need a spot for your morning coffee or a display for a tiny vase, this piece delivers quiet elegance and everyday practicality.

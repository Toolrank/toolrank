Product Description

Stay hydrated on the move with our innovative Wearable Sports Wrist Water Bottle. This 200ml (7 fl oz) flask is designed for runners, cyclists, gym-goers, and marathon participants, offering a convenient hands-free hydration solution. Made from food-grade silicone and PP materials, it is BPA-free and safe for drinking. The soft, flexible bottle body features an adjustable Velcro strap for a secure and comfortable fit on your wrist. Its unique bite valve design allows for easy, one-handed drinking without needing to open a cap -C simply bite and sip. The bottle is leakproof and designed not to shake during exercise. When empty, it collapses to a mere 10mm thickness for minimal pressure and easy storage, expanding to about 35mm when full. It's lightweight (55g) and temperature resistant from -20C to 100C, making it a perfect companion for all your fitness activities.

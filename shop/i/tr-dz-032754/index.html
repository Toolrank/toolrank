Bee Sisal Ball Cat Scratcher Toy

26cm Woven Sisal Ball · Scratch + Play · Character Design

Introducing the Bee Sisal Ball Cat Scratcher — a dual-function feline toy that combines a generously sized 26-centimetre woven sisal ball with an adorable bee character design, built to satisfy both a cat's need to scratch and its drive to bat, chase, and wrestle. Unlike tiny throw toys that disappear under furniture, this substantial ball stays in active play and doubles as a stationary scratch surface.

The sphere is tightly wound from colourful natural sisal rope, producing the coarse, resistant texture cats instinctively prefer for claw maintenance — rough enough to shed old claw sheaths, firm enough to push back against paws and teeth. A lightweight polyethylene (PE) core keeps the ball rollable and battable while holding its round shape through sustained rough play. The bee colourway adds instant visual character, making the toy as display-worthy as it is hard-wearing.

Sized at a 30 × 29 × 29 cm retail footprint, the Bee Sisal Ball works as a floor scratcher beside a sofa, an interactive chase toy, or a self-play companion for home-alone cats. It is the ideal add-on, basket-builder, and gift purchase — an affordable, characterful enrichment product that solves real scratching behaviour while bringing playful energy to any room.

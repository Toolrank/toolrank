CHN613is a comprehensive 13-port multifunctional USB-C docking station that allows you to expand the connectivity of your laptop through a single USB-C connection. It turns a single USB-C port into 13 ports, including four USB-A and one USB-C data ports, two card reader slots, dual HDMI, VGA, Gigabit Ethernet, 3.5" audio jack and 100W PD charging.

If you need to connect with various devices to your laptop simultaneously, CHN613 is an ideal connectivity solution that helps you do more with the devices you already have to increase productivity and convenience.

13-in-1 Powerful USB-C Docking Station
A powerful all-in-one USB-C docking station with dual HDMI, VGA outputs, 100W PD charging port, Gigabit Ethernet Port, SD/MicroSD Card reader, 3.5mm audio output and 5 port USB hub with data transfer rate up to 10Gbps.

Triple Video Outputs with MST Support
Thismultiport adapter offers one VGA output and dual HDMI outputs. It allows you to connect 3 screens simultaneously, and supports up to 2 extended screens with MST support.

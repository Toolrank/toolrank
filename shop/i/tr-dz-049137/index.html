Safeguard your garden harvest and water features with this heavy duty bird netting, designed to provide reliable protection against birds, squirrels and other pests. Measuring 4.5m x 10m, this durable PE mesh creates an effective physical barrier for fruit trees, vegetable patches and fish ponds while allowing sunlight and rain to pass through.

Product Description

This dog muzzle is constructed with soft plastic mesh material, designed with open mesh structure to let dogs drink water while wearing. It comes in two colour options: black and grey, alongside five size tiers XS, S, M, L, XL to match different dog snout circumference, muzzle length and body weight ranges. Adjustable rear strap helps secure the muzzle firmly on pet heads, suitable for daily walking, grooming and public outings to reduce unwanted biting or barking behaviours.

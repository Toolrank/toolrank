Keep your bathroom, kitchen, dorm room, or desk neatly organized with this removable shower caddy and two-tier soap holder. Designed with strong suction cups for smooth, non-porous surfaces, 22 drainage holes, and a 6° inclined base, it helps store daily essentials while promoting quick water drainage and cleaner, drier bath products.

  • Versatile Storage: Holds shampoos, conditioners, shower gels, facial cleansers, loofahs, kitchen tools, and other small essentials.

Ideal for bathrooms, showers, kitchens, washrooms, college dorm rooms, and desks.

Bring a miniature world of freshness and tranquility to your desk or living space with our charming Micro Landscape USB Humidifier.   • Unique Micro Landscape Design: Features a transparent 260ml water tank showcasing a cute internal landscape with a palm tree and colorful decorative stones/crystals.

Bring the Shine Back to Your Gold and Silver Jewelry Over time, gold and silver jewelry loses its luster. Tarnish, dirt, body oils, and lotions build up on the surface, making your favorite pieces look dull and dirty. This jewelry cleaner solution is formulated to remove that buildup safely and gently. No scrubbing, no harsh abrasives – just soak your jewelry in the solution for a few minutes, then wipe clean. Your rings, necklaces, earrings, and bracelets will look like again. Why Jewelry Gets Dull Gold and silver are soft metals that are often alloyed with other metals like copper or nickel. These alloys can tarnish when exposed to air, moisture, and chemicals (lotions, perfumes, sweat). Dirt and oil also accumulate in crevices and settings, dulling the stone’s brilliance. This cleaner uses a blend of mild acids and surfactants that dissolve tarnish and lift dirt without damaging the metal or stones. What You Can Clean Gold jewelry – Yellow gold, white gold, rose gold. Do not use on: Opals, pearls, emeralds (can be porous or soft), costume jewelry with glue, or antique pieces with loose settings. How to Use Shake the bottle well. Pour the solution into the included cap or a small bowl (enough to submerge the jewelry). Place your jewelry into the solution. Let it soak for 2–5 minutes (for heavy tarnish, up to 10 minutes). Remove jewelry and gently scrub with a soft toothbrush (optional, for crevices). Rinse thoroughly with clean water. Dry with a soft, lint-free cloth. For silver with heavy tarnish, repeat or let soak longer. Gentle and Non-Corrosive This formula is pH-balanced for precious metals. It contains no harsh acids, no , and no abrasives. Pro Tips for Results For heavily tarnished silver, line a bowl with aluminum foil, add hot water and a tablespoon of baking soda, then soak – but this solution is easier. Clean jewelry over a sink with a drain catcher to avoid losing small pieces. Do not soak jewelry with glued settings (the glue may loosen). After cleaning, store jewelry in a soft pouch or lined box to prevent tarnish. Clean jewelry every 2-4 weeks for appearance.

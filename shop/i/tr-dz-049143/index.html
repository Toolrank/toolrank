Product Overview

This CNC Aluminium Front Strike Fishing Reel combines precision engineering with lightweight performance, making it an ideal choice for anglers seeking reliability and smooth operation. Designed specifically for front-strike (Tanago-style) and light rock fishing applications, this reel features a durable aluminium alloy construction that resists corrosion while maintaining a minimal weight of approximately 91 g. Its compact 60 mm profile ensures perfect balance on light rods, offering a premium feel for sensitive bite detection.

Key Features

  • Precision CNC Machining: Crafted from high-grade aluminium alloy using CNC technology, ensuring tight tolerances, structural integrity and a sleek, modern aesthetic.
  • Adjustable Drag System: Equipped with a knurled metal drag knob that allows for precise, incremental adjustments to line tension, giving you control during the fight.
  • Smooth Bearing Assembly: Features a 2+1 bearing configuration (2 ball bearings + 1 one-way clutch) to ensure silky-smooth retrieval and instant anti-reverse response.
  • V-Groove Spool Design: The spool is engineered with a V-groove profile to guide the line neatly, preventing tangles and ensuring consistent casting and retrieval performance.
  • Integrated Metal Foot: A robust, one-piece metal reel seat provides a secure, wobble-free connection to your rod, enhancing sensitivity and durability.

Why It Stands Out

Unlike standard plastic reels that can flex under pressure, this all-metal reel offers superior rigidity and longevity. The open-frame design not only reduces weight but also allows for quick cleaning and maintenance after saltwater use. Its specialised V-groove spool addresses a common pain point in front-strike fishing by significantly reducing line management issues, allowing you to focus entirely on the catch.

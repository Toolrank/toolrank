Details

Product Parameters:
Made of polyester and spandex with a high stretch jacquard knitted texture, this recliner slipcover features a soft and comfortable feel with a fitted look. The color shown is dark blue, and the design includes a right-side pocket and a continuous footrest fit. It is designed for standard power lift recliners, fitting back circumference from 78 to 88 inches and sitting width from 24 to 33 inches. This single-seater cover comes in 4 separate parts, including 2 arm pieces, 1 center back of seat piece, and 1 center back cover piece.

Core Features:
The stretch fabric helps the cover fit closely over the recliner while offering a neat and wrinkle-free appearance. Elastic edges and two elastic loops at the back corners help keep the cover securely in place and reduce shifting after sitting. The cover helps protect the recliner from tears, spills, stains, and daily wear, while the side pocket provides convenient storage for small items such as a remote control or glasses. The fabric is described as durable, wear-resistant, and not easy to pill or fade during normal use.

Suitable Scenarios:
This slipcover is suitable for households that want to protect a standard power lift recliner during everyday use. It works well in homes with kids and pets, and it is also suitable for refreshing the look of an older recliner without changing the chair itself. The side pocket design is practical for living rooms, reading corners, or rest areas where small essentials need to stay within easy reach.

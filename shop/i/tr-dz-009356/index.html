This stainless steel baking sheet with cooling rack set includes 1 half sheet pan and 1 removable wire rack, offering a healthy, non-toxic cooking experience without chemical coating. Use them together in the oven for crisping bacon and roasting veggies, or separately as a cooling rack for cookies fresh out of the oven.

  • Healthy Cooking: Made of food-grade stainless steel with no toxic chemical coating, so you can bake and roast with confidence even at high temperatures.

  • 2-in-1 Versatility: The wire rack fits perfectly inside the pan—use together to drain grease while roasting meat, poultry, and bacon, or apart for cooling cookies, cakes, and bread.

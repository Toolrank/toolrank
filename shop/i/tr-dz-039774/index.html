GO UP ACTIVE ECOLOGIC helps families around the globe live and breathe eco values and choices. Made with Bio-Sourced wheat straw, and 100% Global Recycled Standard plastic (GRS) - an international protocol that verifies recycled content in final products. Choose Globber. Choose greener!

Easily transform, and switch between different modes anytime, thanks to our 100% tool-less design. GO UP ACTIVE ECOLOGIC has spring-loaded buttons and knobs to seamlessly transform into a ride-on (15-36M), walking bike (15-36M), and toddler scooter (3-7Y+).

4-height adjustable aluminium painted T-bar with comfortable TPR ergonomic grips and an anti-scratch sleeve inside the painted column; for kids aged 15m to 9+.

2-height adjustable wide soft TPR seat, mounted on a tool-less connector tube, which easily safely moves the seat upward and backward with a handle for maximum comfort space.

Long wide removable footrest, with an easy-to-unscrew knob, for more comfort and safety.

The Patented folding system enables trolley mode on the toddler scooter, to easily carry and store in compact spaces.

Patented steering lock button fixes the steering system of the two front wheels to only move forward backward to help kids master balance.

High-quality and high-rebound 121mm PU casted front wheels, mounted on ABEC 5 bearings, and one 80mm rear PU casted wheel for smooth rides. Its extra-long and wide composite rear brake delivers brake efficiency and promotes wheel longevity.

GO UP ACTIVE ECOLOGIC supports 20kg (ride-on / walking bike mode), 50kg (scooter mode)

Product Description

This 3/8-inch low profile inner tie rod removal tool makes steering rack maintenance easier for DIY mechanics and professional garages. The compact 2mm thin design slips into tight engine bays while the U-shaped toothed jaws grip the tie rod firmly, helping you remove or install inner tie rods safely without damaging nearby components.

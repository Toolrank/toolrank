Anti-slip, fall protection, safety protection: These transparent stair strips have strong adhesion and a finely textured surface, providing extra grip on various slippery surfaces. Our anti-slip stair strips offer excellent anti-slip properties and protect you and your loved ones from slipping and falling on the stairs, minimizing the risk of accidents. They are particularly suitable for pets, the elderly, children, pregnant women, and babies. Invisible and transparent: Our transparent anti-slip stair strips come with 15 pre-cut strips. They are transparent and retain the original color and appearance of hardwood floors and tiled stairs. They guarantee safety while simultaneously preserving the decorative beauty of stairs, patios, and other surfaces. Easy to use: Clean and dry the surface thoroughly before applying the removable transparent stair strips. Ensure it is dust- and grease-free. Apply the self-adhesive stair strips to the desired location. Press the strip down gradually while removing the backing. Finally, roll the transparent stair strips with a roller, from the center to the edge, to remove air bubbles and ensure good adhesion. Widely used: Our transparent indoor anti-slip stair strips are weather-resistant and maintain strong adhesion in damp environments and during cleaning. They do not peel off over time and can be applied to smooth surfaces such as marble, tiles, vinyl, concrete, synthetics, and laminate. They are suitable for smooth indoor and outdoor areas such as bathrooms, bathtubs, showers, swimming pools, bunk beds, stairs, and ladders. They are easy to clean with a damp cloth or garden hose. They can also be used as a replacement for carpet.

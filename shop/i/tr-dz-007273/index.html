Product Description

This blue ultralight inflatable camping sleeping pad measures 190 x 60 x 5 cm and provides a compact sleeping surface for camping, hiking and travel. Its textured air-cell design helps distribute body weight, while the built-in pillow adds support for the head and neck. The waterproof TPU-coated surface is suited to outdoor use, and the pad packs down to save space in a backpack or camping kit.

Usage

Inflate the pad before use and place it on clear ground free from sharp objects. Adjust the firmness to suit personal comfort, wipe the surface clean after use and allow it to dry before deflating and packing.

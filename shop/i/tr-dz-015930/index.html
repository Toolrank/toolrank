Product Description

Designed for everyday dog handling, this full-coverage mesh basket muzzle helps create a physical barrier during walks, grooming, veterinary visits, and training while its open grid design keeps the snout visible and the adjustable head straps support a secure, customized fit.

Key Features

  • Full-Coverage Basket Design: The roomy cage-like structure covers the nose and mouth area to help discourage biting or picking up unwanted objects.
  • Open Mesh Visibility: The square-grid mesh keeps the snout visible and provides breathable coverage while maintaining a structured basket shape.
  • Adjustable Fit: Over-the-head and behind-the-head straps with multiple adjustment points help fine-tune the fit around the dog’s head.
  • Secure Buckles: Clip-style plastic buckles support convenient fitting and removal while helping the muzzle stay aligned during movement.
  • Reinforced Edge Binding: Black fabric binding around the opening and seams creates a neat, reinforced finish for everyday use.
  • Two Strap Color Options: Choose black or light gray straps, both paired with black buckle hardware.

Usage

Suitable for neighborhood walks, grooming, nail trimming, veterinary clinic visits, and home training when closer handling is needed. Select the correct size, adjust the straps for a comfortable fit, introduce the muzzle gradually, and supervise your dog throughout use.

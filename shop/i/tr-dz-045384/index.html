Help Your Indoor Plants Thrive with This Stylish Angel Ring Grow Light

Give your beloved indoor plants, succulents, and herbs the light they need to flourish, no matter the season or available sunlight. This sleek and modern "Angel Ring" grow light provides an optimized full spectrum of light that mimics natural sunlight, promoting healthy photosynthesis and vibrant growth. Its clever, adjustable design makes it a perfect and stylish addition to any potted plant.

Key Features:

  • Full Spectrum LED Light: Equipped with high-efficiency LED diodes that emit a full spectrum of light, providing the ideal wavelengths for all stages of plant growth, from seedling to flowering.
  • Fully Adjustable Height: The sturdy telescopic stake can be easily adjusted in height (from 20cm to 64cm), allowing you to position the light perfectly above your plant and adapt it as the plant grows taller.
  • 180-degree Rotatable Head: The ring light panel can be rotated 180 degree, enabling you to direct the light precisely where it's needed most for optimal coverage.
  • Smart Auto On/Off Timer: Features a convenient in-line controller with an automatic cycle timer. Set it to stay on for 4, 8, or 12 hours, and it will automatically turn on and off at the same time every day.
  • 4 Dimmable Brightness Levels: Easily adjust the light intensity with four dimming levels to meet the specific lighting requirements of different plants and growth stages.
  • Easy to Use: Simply insert the pointed stake into the soil of your potted plant, adjust the height and angle, and plug the USB cord into any 5V power source like a wall adapter or power bank.

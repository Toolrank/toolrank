Details

This electric pepper grinder is a versatile kitchen assistant, designed to deliver convenience and customization to your seasoning experience. It combines gravity-sensing technology with precise grinding adjustment, making it an indispensable tool for cooking and dining.

The grinder features an innovative gravity-sensing switch. Simply tilt it at an angle greater than 45 degrees, and the mechanism will activate, allowing for automatic grinding. Returning it to an upright position will stop the grinding process. This intuitive, tilt-to-grind function provides seamless control, especially in hands-on cooking situations such as searing steaks or preparing dishes that require instant seasoning.

With its adjustable top dial, the grinder gives you complete control over the grind's coarseness. Turn the dial clockwise to achieve a fine powder, which is perfect for sauces, soups, or garnishing. Turn it counterclockwise for a coarser grind, ideal for robust recipes like spicy hot pot noodles or seasoning grilled meats, adding distinct texture and flavor to your meals.

Powered by six AA batteries, the grinder is designed for long-lasting, dependable performance, ensuring that it is ready to use whenever you need it. The built-in ceramic grinding core ensures durability and resistance to corrosion, while also preserving the full aroma and freshness of your spices. The transparent body allows for easy monitoring of spice levels, so you can refill in a timely manner.

Suitable for a range of dry spices such as black pepper, Sichuan pepper, and coarse sea salt, this electric grinder offers flexibility for various culinary applications. Its user-friendly design, combined with reliable power and versatile grinding capabilities, makes it an essential addition to any kitchen, elevating both everyday meals and special dishes with effortless precision.

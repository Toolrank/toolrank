Dressing Aid Tool – Button & Zipper Assistant for Elderly and People with Limited Mobility

Designed to make dressing easier and more independent, this versatile dressing aid tool is ideal for elderly individuals, people with arthritis, limited hand strength, or motor disabilities. Whether you're fastening buttons, pulling zippers, or securing clothing closures, this practical helper provides the support needed to maintain dignity and autonomy in daily life.

Made from durable materials including a comfortable rubberized grip handle and sturdy steel wire, this assistive device combines functionality with ease of use. Its ergonomic design allows users to perform tasks that may otherwise be difficult or painful due to reduced dexterity or joint stiffness.

Key Features

  • Multi-Functional Design: One tool for both buttoning and zipper assistance—perfect for shirts, jackets, pants, and other garments with small or hard-to-reach fasteners.
  • Secure Grip Handle: The textured rubberized plastic handle offers excellent control and comfort, even with weak hands or tremors.
  • Sturdy Steel Wire: Reinforced metal loop at one end helps hold buttons securely in place while threading through holes; the hook end slides easily into most zipper pulls.
  • Easy to Use: Simply insert the wire loop around a button and pull it through the fabric hole, or slide the hook into a zipper pull to open or close it effortlessly.
  • Compact & Lightweight: Each unit weighs approximately 68 grams, making it easy to carry in a purse, pocket, or bedside drawer.

Product Description

Finding the perfect mounting position for your satellite dish or outdoor equipment can feel like searching for a needle in a haystack, but this adjustable roof mount bracket is designed to make that challenge far simpler. Built for Standard Gen 3 and compatible with a range of small electronic devices, this heavy-duty mount combines a sturdy trapezoidal base with a pivoting arm and rotating mast, giving you the flexibility to dial in the ideal angle and direction no matter your roof pitch or installation environment. The 180° adjustable pivot lets you tilt the mounting plate to suit different roof slopes, helping you achieve a clear, unobstructed view of the sky. Meanwhile, the 360° rotating mast offers smooth directional adjustment, so you can fine-tune the orientation of your equipment with ease. Constructed from durable aluminium alloy with reinforced components, this bracket is made to withstand long-term outdoor exposure while keeping your setup stable and secure. An integrated cable management port routes wires neatly through the mount, protecting them from the elements and creating a cleaner, more professional-looking installation. Whether you are setting up a dish on a rooftop, mounting a small camera on a wall, or securing a speaker in an outdoor space, this mount delivers reliable performance and adaptable positioning. The assembled height reaches approximately 37 cm, with a base measuring 22.5 cm by 14 cm and a top mounting plate of 17 cm by 9.5 cm, offering a compact yet robust solution for a variety of mounting needs. Please verify your device model before purchasing to ensure compatibility.

Experience cloud-like support and comfort with the Osteopedic Euro Top Pocket Spring Mattress!

This mattress provides the best of both worlds with its hybrid pocket-spring and foam design.

The mattress’ hybrid pocket spring and foam design targets the most important areas of the body – the feet, the knees, the lower back, the head and the shoulders.

This design provides both pressure-relief and support by conforming to these essential contours of the body. It also ensures zero-partner disturbance by minimizing motion disturbance from tossing and turning, ultimately allowing the user to experience an optimum sleep experience.

The Euro-Top design of the foam layers provide the ultimate in cushioning and support. This is done by fitting to the body’s curves and relieving pressure points. It also allows for both ventilation of heat and moisture-wicking from the body. This breathable design creates a more breathable mattress core by preventing heat retention.

The mattress is of a medium-firm design and is encased in a luxurious 400GSM knitted Jacquard cover that is ultra-soft to the touch, while the micro-quilting design of the cover ensures its durability and resilience over time. Alongside this, the durable side-handles allow for easy positioning of the mattress.

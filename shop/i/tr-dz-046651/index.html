Details

Keep your Australian garden safe and pest-free with this Solar Sonic Snake and Rodent Repeller. Designed to blend seamlessly into your lawn, this ground stake utilises advanced sonic and vibration technology to deter burrowing pests and dangerous snakes without the use of harmful chemicals or traps. It is an eco-friendly, set-and-forget solution ideal for protecting families, pets, and vegetable patches from unwanted underground visitors.

Key Features

  • Solar Powered Efficiency: Features a high-quality polycrystalline silicon solar panel (2V/45mA) that charges the built-in 600mAh battery during the day, ensuring continuous operation through the night.
  • Sonic & Vibration Technology: Emits low-frequency sound waves (400-1000Hz) and penetrating vibrations through the ground every 30 seconds. This stimulates the central nervous system of rodents and snakes, creating an uncomfortable environment that forces them to flee the area.

  • Safe & Humane: A non-toxic solution that drives pests away physically rather than killing them. It is completely safe for use around children, dogs, and cats.

Product Description

This "Small Square" Solar Repeller is designed for simplicity and effectiveness. Simply switch it on, insert the spike into the soil, and let the sun do the rest.

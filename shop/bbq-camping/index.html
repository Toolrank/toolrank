BBQ & Camping buying guide

Charcoal BBQ or smoker?

Grill fast over direct heat on a charcoal BBQ. Smoke low and slow in a smoker. A thermometer takes the guesswork out of both.

Offset charcoal smoker BBQ

Choosing a tent

Pick one size up from your group so there’s room for bags. Instant-up tents are quickest to pitch.

Dome tent 2 person · Instant-up tent 4 person · Family tent 6 person

Cooler box or 12V fridge?

A cooler box suits day trips. For longer trips, a 12V fridge freezer keeps food cold without ice.

Portable fridge freezer 50L

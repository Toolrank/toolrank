Music gear buying guide

Home studio basics

Start with one good dynamic mic on a solid boom stand. A small isolation shield cuts room echo while you record.

Dynamic vocal microphone · Boom mic stands (2 pack) · Mic isolation shield, 3 panel

Treating your room

Put foam panels at ear height beside and behind you. Bass traps go in the corners, where low end builds up.

Acoustic foam panels (40 pack) · Acoustic foam bass traps (20 pack)

Keyboard stand: X or Z?

An X-frame folds flat and suits gigs. A Z-style stand is steadier and leaves room for your legs.

X-frame keyboard stand · Z-style keyboard stand with wheels

Stage and rack

Raise speakers to ear height with stands. Cover floor cables with a ramp, and lock small gear in a rack drawer.

Speaker stands, adjustable (pair) · Cable protector ramp 3m · 19 in rack drawer, 2U lockable

Automotive & 4WD buying guide

Air down, air up

Lower tyre pressure gives more grip on sand and rough tracks. Pack deflators to let air out evenly and a 12V compressor to pump back up before the bitumen.

4WD tyre deflators (4 pack) · 12V tyre compressor 85L/min · Tyre inflator with gauge 160 PSI

Get yourself unstuck

Recovery tracks help when you bog in sand or mud. A jump starter gets a flat battery going without a second car.

Recovery tracks (pair) · Jump starter 2000A with power bank · Rechargeable handheld spotlight

Roof racks and shade

Check your roof type first: some bars clamp to rails, others fit cars without rails. Check the listing's bar length and load rating against your car's roof limit.

Roof rack cross bars 1350mm (lockable) · Roof rack bars for cars without rails · Roof rack side awning 1.4 × 2m

See more on the road

A dash cam records the road ahead and behind. A mirror monitor kit adds a reversing camera to older cars.

Front and rear dash cam, 10in screen · Reversing camera with mirror monitor · Clip-on towing mirrors (pair)

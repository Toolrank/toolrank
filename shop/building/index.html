Building & construction buying guide

Ladder or step stool?

A step stool suits quick jobs at home, like shelves and light fittings. For gutters, painting or ceilings, pick a taller ladder.

3-step aluminium step stool · 5-step aluminium ladder

Handrails for longer jobs

A platform ladder with handrails gives you something to hold while both hands work. Always set it on firm, level ground.

4-step steel ladder with handrails

Laying floating floors

An install kit keeps boards tight and spacing even along the walls. Check the board maker's own fitting guide first.

Flooring install kit (48 pc)

Covering the job site

A heavy-duty tarp keeps rain off timber, tools and open roofs. Tie it down at every eyelet in wind.

Heavy-duty PVC tarp 3 × 4m

Safety & workwear buying guide

Protecting your knees

Gel knee pads spread the load when tiling, concreting or weeding. Check the straps sit above and below the joint, not on it.

Gel knee pads

Being seen on site

Reflective tape marks trailers, posts, steps and hazards so they show up in headlights. Clean and dry the surface before you stick it.

Hi-vis reflective tape, yellow (2 pack)

Marking off an area

Cones with reflective bands close off a driveway, work zone or wet floor fast. Stackable sets store flat in a ute or shed.

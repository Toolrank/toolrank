Best for research & search

Answer engines and research assistants.

Last updated 2026-10-15. Editorial ranking.

The ranking

  1. Perplexity (freemium): Cited answer engine for fast web research.
  2. NotebookLM (freemium): Grounded Q&A and audio overviews over your own sources.
  3. Elicit (freemium): Searches and extracts data from academic papers.
  4. Consensus (freemium): Evidence-based answers from peer-reviewed research.
  5. ChatGPT Deep Research (freemium): ChatGPT's agentic multi-step web research reports.
  6. Gemini Deep Research (freemium): Gemini's multi-step research reports from the web.
  7. Google AI Mode (free): Google Search's conversational AI answer mode.
  8. Kagi (paid): Ad-free paid search engine with AI Assistant.
  9. You.com (freemium): AI search and research agents.
  10. Semantic Scholar (free): Free AI-powered academic paper search from Ai2.
  11. Scite (paid): Shows how papers are cited: supporting or contrasting.
  12. SciSpace (freemium): AI research assistant for reading and searching papers.
  13. Undermind (freemium): Deep academic literature search agent.
  14. Exa (freemium): Search engine and API built for AI and agents.
  15. Tavily (freemium): Web search API for AI agents.
  16. Felo (freemium): Multilingual AI search engine.
  17. Liner (freemium): AI research assistant with cited sources.
  18. Connected Papers (freemium): Visual graph of related academic papers.
  19. Research Rabbit (freemium): Literature discovery and citation mapping.
  20. Answer me with HTML (freemium): Agent skill that answers hard questions as a readable one-page HTML. New open-source launch on GitHub.
  21. geo-sleuth (free): Agent skill that geolocates photos using OSM, elevation skylines and satellite imagery. New open-source launch on GitHub.

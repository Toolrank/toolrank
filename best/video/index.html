Generate and edit video with AI.

Last updated 2026-10-15. Editorial ranking.

The ranking

  1. Runway (freemium): Pro-grade generative video models and editing tools.
  2. Google Veo (paid): Google DeepMind's video model with native audio.
  3. Kling AI (freemium): High-quality text/image-to-video from Kuaishou.
  4. Luma AI (freemium): Dream Machine video generation, fast and cinematic.
  5. HeyGen (freemium): AI avatar and talking-head video, translation and dubbing.
  6. Sora (freemium): OpenAI's video generation model and app.
  7. Synthesia (freemium): AI avatar video platform for training and corporate videos in 140+ languages.
  8. Pika (freemium): Text- and image-to-video generator with effects for short social clips.
  9. Hailuo AI (MiniMax) (freemium): MiniMax's text- and image-to-video generator.
  10. Higgsfield (freemium): Video generation with cinematic camera-motion presets, hosting several models.
  11. PixVerse (freemium): Text- and image-to-video generator with templates for social video.
  12. Vidu (freemium): Shengshu's text- and image-to-video model with reference-to-video.
  13. Hedra (freemium): Generates talking-character videos from an image and audio.
  14. Captions (freemium): Mobile-first app for AI captions, editing and talking-head video.
  15. OpusClip (freemium): Turns long videos into short vertical clips automatically.
  16. Descript (video) (freemium): Edit video by editing its transcript.
  17. CapCut (freemium): ByteDance's video editor with AI captions, effects and generation.
  18. Invideo AI (freemium): Generates full videos with script, stock media and voiceover from a prompt.
  19. Colossyan (freemium): AI avatar video creator focused on workplace learning.
  20. D-ID (freemium): Talking-avatar video and real-time streaming avatars from a photo.
  21. Wan (Alibaba) (freemium): Alibaba's Wan video models, with open weights and a hosted generator.
  22. LTX Studio (freemium): Lightricks' storyboard-to-video production tool built on LTX models.
  23. Veed (freemium): Browser video editor with AI subtitles, avatars and clean-up tools.
  24. Topaz Video (paid): Desktop AI upscaling, denoising and frame interpolation for video.
  25. Wonder Studio (Autodesk Flow Studio) (freemium): Autodesk tool that replaces actors with CG characters from live footage.
  26. Vozo (freemium): AI video translation, dubbing and lip sync.
  27. ReelMimic (free): Show it a video you love; an AI crew makes a new one in the same style. New open-source launch on GitHub.

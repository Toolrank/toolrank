FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Gemini Deep Research Google AI Plus ($4.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://gemini.google/subscriptions/, checked 19 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Notion AI: Free plan includes a trial of Notion AI only; Gamma: Free: $0, 400 credits at signup, up to 10 slides per prompt; ChatGPT Deep Research: ChatGPT Free includes limited deep research; Gemini Deep Research: Free: Deep Research is included in the $0 plan with a Google Account; Research Rabbit: Free: $0 forever; Mem: Free: 25 new notes/month.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $10/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–19 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

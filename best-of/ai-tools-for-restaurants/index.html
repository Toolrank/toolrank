The short answer

These tools handle restaurant phone calls, ordering or marketing with AI. Slang.ai comes first: restaurant-specific voice AI with clear per-location pricing. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Toast (ToastIQ) Point of Sale (Starting at $69/month). None of the 5 has a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–17 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Slang.ai: Restaurant-specific voice AI with clear per-location pricing. Plan: Not yet verified
  2. Loman: Restaurant voice AI with POS integration and no per-minute fees. Plan: Not yet verified

Who should pick which

Pick Slang.ai for busy restaurants missing calls ($399/location/month (Core)); think twice if you're a small venue with few calls. Pick Loman for restaurants wanting phone ordering automated (price not yet verified); think twice if you need public, self-serve pricing. Pick Owner.com for independent restaurants growing direct online sales (Flexible: $249/month + 5% restaurant fee per order); think twice if you only need a basic menu site. Pick Popmenu for restaurants wanting website plus automated marketing (Starter: $159/month annual ($179 monthly)); think twice if you only need phone automation. Pick Toast (ToastIQ) for restaurants that want a POS with built-in insights, payroll and marketing (Point of Sale: Starting at $69/month). Watch out: the $0 Starter Kit is tied to Toast hardware and payment terms.

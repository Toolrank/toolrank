Best AI tools for restaurants

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools handle restaurant phone calls, ordering or marketing with AI. Slang.ai comes first: restaurant-specific voice AI with clear per-location pricing. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Toast (ToastIQ) Point of Sale (Starting at $69/month). None of the 8 has a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–23 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Slang.ai: Restaurant-specific voice AI with clear per-location pricing. Plan: Not yet verified
  2. Loman: Restaurant voice AI with POS integration and no per-minute fees. Plan: Not yet verified
  3. ConverseNow: Voice AI that takes phone and drive-thru orders for restaurant chains. Plan: Not yet verified
  4. Owner.com: Commission-light direct ordering with two simple pricing options. Plan: Flexible: $249/month + 5% restaurant fee per order
  5. Popmenu: Restaurant marketing suite with AI emails/texts and an AI phone add-on. Plan: Starter: $159/month annual ($179 monthly)
  6. Toast (ToastIQ): Restaurant POS whose ToastIQ AI surfaces insights and helps run service. Plan: Point of Sale: Starting at $69/month
    Software subscription; choose your own hardware
  7. Winnow: Camera-based AI that tracks and cuts food waste in commercial kitchens. Plan: Not yet verified
  8. Maple: Flat monthly prices with no per-minute fees, and POS and OpenTable integrations on Pro. Plan: Maple Voice: $85/month billed annually; $150/month billed monthly
    24/7 answering, FAQs, call transfer

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Restaurants & Bars, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Slang.ai for busy restaurants missing calls (From $399 per location (Core)); think twice if you're a small venue with few calls. Pick Loman for restaurants wanting phone ordering automated (price not yet verified); think twice if you need public, self-serve pricing. Pick ConverseNow for restaurant chains automating phone and drive-thru ordering with branded voice AI (Custom (demo required)). Watch out: no published prices; you must book a demo. Pick Owner.com for independent restaurants growing direct online sales (Flexible: $249/month + 5% restaurant fee per order); think twice if you only need a basic menu site. Pick Popmenu for restaurants wanting website plus automated marketing (Starter: $159/month annual ($179 monthly)); think twice if you only need phone automation. Pick Toast (ToastIQ) for restaurants that want a POS with built-in insights, payroll and marketing (Point of Sale: Starting at $69/month). Watch out: the $0 Starter Kit is tied to Toast hardware and payment terms. Pick Winnow for commercial kitchens that want AI to record food waste automatically and forecast production (Custom (demo required)). Watch out: hardware-based enterprise product with no published prices.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Toast (ToastIQ) Point of Sale (Starting at $69/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://pos.toasttab.com/pricing, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

None of these has a free plan we could confirm. None lists a permanent free plan on its pricing page. Some offer a trial; see the table.

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with restaurant-specific products with vendor pages we checked, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–23 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

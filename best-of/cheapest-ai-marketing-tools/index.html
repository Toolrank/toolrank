Who should pick which

Pick HubSpot Breeze for marketing teams already on, or moving to, the HubSpot CRM who want AI agents built in (Starter: $20/seat/month). Watch out: AI agents run on HubSpot Credits, and Professional and Enterprise add mandatory onboarding fees. Pick Copy.ai for GTM teams automating content workflows (Self-serve: $24/month annual ($29 monthly)); think twice if you just need occasional copywriting. Pick Jasper for marketing teams producing on-brand content at scale (Pro: $59/month yearly or $69 monthly); think twice if you're an individual on a budget. Pick Clay for enriching and automating prospect lists (Launch: $167/month). Watch out: paid plans start high.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

HubSpot Breeze Starter ($20/seat/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/marketing, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

HubSpot Breeze: Free tools: $0/month for up to 2 users, no credit card; Clay: Free: 500 actions/month, 100 data credits/month.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a paid plan price we could read on the vendor page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–17 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

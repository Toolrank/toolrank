The short answer

These tools let you build AI agents or AI workflows without writing code. Make comes first: a visual scenario builder with thousands of app integrations and a free plan. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Lindy Team (From $29.99/month). 3 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Make for ops teams who want visual, branching automations (free plan: 1,000 credits/month, 1 MB data storage, 1 data store); think twice if you want flat unlimited runs. Pick Zapier Agents for teams already on Zapier who want agents (price not yet verified); think twice if you need a code-first agent framework. Pick Gumloop for no-code AI workflow automation (Pro: Starts at $37/month). Watch out: no permanent free plan shown on the pricing page. Pick Lindy for AI assistants that handle email, meetings and admin (Team: From $29.99/month). Watch out: free credits expire after 7 days. Pick Botpress for customer-facing chatbots (Plus: $150/month billed annually). Watch out: paid plans start high for small projects.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Lindy Team (From $29.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.lindy.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Make: 1,000 credits/month, 1 MB data storage, 1 data store; Lindy: $50 in free credits for 7 days, no card; Botpress: Free: 100 conversations/month.

Best AI image tools with MCP support

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI image tools supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so AI agents can connect to it. Recraft comes first: low-cost plans with multiple models and design-focused output. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Photoroom Pro ($7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly). 2 of the 4 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 17–18 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Recraft for designers making brand-consistent graphics and vectors (Basic: $10/month billed annually ($120)); think twice if you need commercial use on a free plan. Pick Canva AI for non-designers making social posts, presentations and marketing graphics with AI help (US$144/year (Pro, one person)). Watch out: AI use is capped by a shared allowance; heavier AI needs the AI Pass add-on at extra cost. Pick Magnific for creators who want AI upscaling plus a suite of image and video models and stock assets (Premium: $14.50/month billed annually or $20 monthly). Watch out: credits are annual pools, so heavy users can run out before renewal. Pick Photoroom for online sellers making product photos with background removal and AI backgrounds (Pro: $7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly). Watch out: API and MCP usage uses a separate credit allowance.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Photoroom Pro ($7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.photoroom.com/pricing, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Recraft: Limited free credits, personal use only; Canva AI: Free: US$0, up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses, 5GB storage.

How were these tools chosen?

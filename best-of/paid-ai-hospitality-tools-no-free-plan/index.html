Who should pick which

Pick Canary Technologies for hotels wanting check-in, upsells and AI guest messaging in one system (Custom quote). Watch out: no public prices: every bundle is quoted. Pick HiJiffy for hotels automating guest messaging (from €99 / $109 / £99 per month); think twice if you run a single short-term rental. Pick Asksuite for hotels that want an AI reservation agent to answer guests and drive direct bookings (Custom (demo required)). Watch out: prices are not published; you must book a demo. Pick Duetto for hotel revenue teams that want AI-driven rate recommendations and profit benchmarking (Custom (demo required)). Watch out: enterprise sales process with no published prices. Pick Lodgify for hosts with a few properties who want their own booking website and synced calendars (Basic: From $14/month). Watch out: price rises with property count and plan; automated messaging starts on Professional. Pick Guesty for hosts with 1–3 listings who may grow into a full property-management platform (Lite: From $9/month per listing). Watch out: the Pro and Enterprise plans are quoted by listing count. Pick iGMS for hosts who want AI-drafted guest replies and only pay for properties that get bookings (PRO: $38/month per property billed annually, or $48 billed monthly (1 property)). Watch out: AI Agent and dynamic pricing need PRO+.

Best AI automation tools for enterprise buyers

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI automation tools lists an Enterprise or custom-priced plan, the usual route to SSO, contracts and volume pricing. Zapier comes first: the largest app ecosystem with a free forever plan and simple task-based pricing. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Zapier Professional (from $19.99/month). 3 of the 6 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–18 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Zapier: The largest app ecosystem with a free forever plan and simple task-based pricing. Plan: Enterprise: Contact sales
  2. LangGraph: Developer framework for stateful, controllable agents. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
    Self-hosted and hybrid deployment options
  3. CrewAI: Multi-agent orchestration framework plus hosted platform. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
  4. Relevance AI: No-code platform for building AI agent teams. Plan: Enterprise: Custom (talk to sales)
    Unlimited agents, users and workforces; 2,000+ integrations; SSO, RBAC and audit logs
  5. Gumloop: Drag-and-drop AI automation and agent builder. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
  6. Voiceflow: Platform to design and deploy chat and voice agents. Plan: For Businesses: Contact sales (book a demo)
    Voice and chat deployment, observability

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Automation & Agents, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Zapier for non-technical teams automating across many SaaS apps (Enterprise: Contact sales); think twice if you run very high volumes and want self-hosting. Pick LangGraph for developers building stateful, controllable AI agents in code (Enterprise: Custom). Watch out: the framework is free, but hosted deployment and tracing run on LangSmith with per-seat and usage charges. Pick CrewAI for developers building multi-agent workflows (Enterprise: Custom). Watch out: paid self-serve tier not shown; Enterprise is custom. Pick Relevance AI for companies building a team of AI agents for sales and operations (Enterprise: Custom (talk to sales)). Watch out: only the Enterprise plan was shown on the pricing page. Pick Gumloop for no-code AI workflow automation (Enterprise: Custom). Watch out: no permanent free plan shown on the pricing page. Pick Voiceflow for customer-experience teams building chat and voice agents (For Businesses: Contact sales (book a demo)). Watch out: no published per-plan prices.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Zapier Professional (from $19.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://zapier.com/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Zapier: 100 tasks/month; Zaps, Tables and Forms included; LangGraph: Open-source framework (MIT licence); LangSmith Developer plan $0 with 1 free seat and 5k base traces/month; CrewAI: Basic: free, 50 workflow executions/month.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with an Enterprise or custom plan on the pricing page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–18 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

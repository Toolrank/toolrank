Who should pick which

Pick Runway for creators and studios producing AI video (Standard: $12/month annual ($15 monthly)); think twice if you need long-form video on a small budget. Pika (Starter: $10/month ($8 annual)): Text- and image-to-video generator with effects for short social clips. Pick Higgsfield for cinematic AI video with camera-motion presets and many hosted models (Starter: $19/month billed annually). Watch out: heavy promotional pricing changes often.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

PixVerse Standard ($8/month billed yearly ($96) or $10 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://app.pixverse.ai/subscribe, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Runway: 125 one-time credits, 5GB storage; HeyGen: 3 videos/month, up to 1 min each; Synthesia: Basic: 10 minutes of video/month, 500 credits, Synthesia watermark; Pika: Free: $0, no monthly credits (credit packs only), 2 parallel video generations; Vidu: Free: $0/month, Q4 model at 540P, 10 references/month; Hedra: Free plan, no credit card (per pricing page title).

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $30/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–17 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

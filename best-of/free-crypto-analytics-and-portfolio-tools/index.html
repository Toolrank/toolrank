Best free crypto analytics and portfolio tools

Updated 21 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These crypto tools have a free plan confirmed on their vendor pages. Nansen comes first: on-chain analytics with labelled wallets, smart-money tracking and an AI research assistant. The cheapest verified paid plan here is CoinStats Premium ($13.99/month after a 7-day free trial). 10 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–20 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Nansen: On-chain analytics with labelled wallets, smart-money tracking and an AI research assistant. Plan: Free: $0/month
  2. Messari: Crypto research, asset data and AI-powered market intelligence. Plan: Basic: Free
    1 watchlist, basic asset profiles
  3. Dune: The standard for public onchain dashboards with a free view tier. Plan: Free: $0
  4. Santiment: On-chain, social and development-activity metrics for crypto assets. Plan: Free: $0
    30-day data lag, up to 3 alerts
  5. Surf: AI research assistant built for crypto questions and on-chain data. Plan: Free: $0
    Instant answers limited; 3 Deep Research sessions per week
  6. Elfa AI: AI social-intelligence feed that surfaces trending crypto narratives for traders. Plan: Pay as you go: $0.0145/credit, $20 min deposit
  7. Scam Sniffer: Browser extension and feeds that flag crypto phishing and wallet-drainer sites. Plan: Open source: $0
    Public scam database on GitHub, domain blacklist updated daily
  8. De.Fi: DeFi app with a smart-contract risk scanner, portfolio dashboard and yield explorer. Plan: Not yet verified
  9. Zerion: Multi-chain self-custody wallet and portfolio tracker. Plan: Wallet: $0
    Free app; Premium adds lower swap and bridge fees (price not shown)
  10. CoinStats: Crypto portfolio tracker across exchanges and wallets, with AI insights. Plan: Basic: Free

Who should pick which

Pick Nansen for on-chain analytics and Smart Money tracking (Free: $0/month). Watch out: no trial; free plan is limited outside Solana. Pick Messari for crypto investors and teams who want institutional research, fundraising data and an AI copilot in one place (Basic: Free). Watch out: the Enterprise price is not published and there is no self-serve trial. Pick Dune for crypto analysts building SQL dashboards (Free: $0); think twice if you don't write SQL. Pick Santiment for on-chain and social crypto analytics (Free: $0). Watch out: real-time data needs a paid plan. Pick Surf for crypto traders and researchers who want source-backed answers about tokens and on-chain data (Free: $0). Watch out: the Pro and Max prices shown are annual-billing rates; monthly billing costs more. Pick Elfa AI for crypto social-intelligence data via API (Pay as you go: $0.0145/credit, $20 min deposit). Watch out: API only. Pick Scam Sniffer for wallet users who want free phishing warnings, and teams that need a scam blocklist API (Open source: $0). Watch out: real-time API access starts at $999/month.

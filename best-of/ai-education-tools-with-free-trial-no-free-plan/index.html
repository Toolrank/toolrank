Which of these is cheapest?

Twee Schools (3–10 seats) ($8.33/seat/month ($99.95 per seat for 1 year)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://twee.com/price, checked 20 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

None of these has a free plan we could confirm. None lists a permanent free plan on its pricing page. Some offer a trial; see the table.

How were these tools chosen?

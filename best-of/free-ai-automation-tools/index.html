Who should pick which

Pick Zapier for non-technical teams automating across many SaaS apps (Free: $0); think twice if you run very high volumes and want self-hosting. Pick Make for ops teams who want visual, branching automations (free plan: 1,000 credits/month, 1 MB data storage, 1 data store); think twice if you want flat unlimited runs. Pick CrewAI for developers building multi-agent workflows (Basic: Free). Watch out: paid self-serve tier not shown; Enterprise is custom. Pick LlamaIndex for document parsing and retrieval for LLM apps (Free: $0/month). Watch out: cloud credits limit parsing volume. Pick Mastra for TypeScript developers building agents (Starter: $0/month). Watch out: cloud Teams plan starts high. Pick Dify for teams building LLM apps with a visual builder (Sandbox: Free). Watch out: cloud plans are billed per workspace per year. Pick Flowise for visual LLM flow building (Free: $0/month). Watch out: free cloud plan is limited to 2 flows.

Which of these have a free plan?

Zapier: 100 tasks/month; Zaps, Tables and Forms included; Make: 1,000 credits/month, 1 MB data storage, 1 data store; CrewAI: Basic: free, 50 workflow executions/month; LlamaIndex: Free: $0/month; Mastra: Starter: free for everyone; self-hosted open-source framework; Dify: Sandbox: free, 200 message credits; open-source Community edition free to self-host; Flowise: Free: $0/month, 2 flows and assistants; Langflow: Free and open source, plus a free cloud account; Lindy: $50 in free credits for 7 days, no card; Botpress: Free: 100 conversations/month.

Who should pick which

Pick Duolingo for casual daily language practice (free plan: Free learning content with ads and limited hearts); think twice if you need intensive conversation practice with a tutor. Pick Speak for speaking practice with an AI tutor (price not yet verified). Watch out: subscription price not verified; no permanent free plan. Pick Knowt for students who want AI study notes, flashcards and AP, SAT and ACT prep (Ultra - Annual: $12.99/month, billed upfront at $149.99/year). Watch out: AI summarizers and the voice tutor need Ultra; app-store purchases cannot be refunded by Knowt. Pick Praktika for learners who want daily speaking practice with feedback on every message (price not yet verified). Watch out: plan prices are not published on the homepage; the vendor only quotes about $8 a month.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Knowt Ultra - Annual ($12.99/month, billed upfront at $149.99/year) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://knowt.com/plans, checked 21 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Duolingo: Free learning content with ads and limited hearts; Knowt: Basic: free, unlimited file creation, live collaboration and storage.

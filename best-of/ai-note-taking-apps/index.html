Who should pick which

Pick NotebookLM for students and researchers working from their own documents (In Google AI Pro: $19.99/month (Google AI Pro)); think twice if you need open web search as the main source. Pick Notion AI for teams whose knowledge already lives in Notion (Plus: $10/member/month); think twice if you don't use Notion. Pick Granola for bot-free AI meeting notes (Business: $14/user/month). Watch out: team features need Business. Pick Mem for AI note-taking that organises itself (Mem Plus: $9/month). Watch out: free plan caps new notes.

Which of these is cheapest?

Mem Mem Plus ($9/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://get.mem.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

NotebookLM: Standard chat/generation limits, up to 50 sources per notebook; Notion AI: Free plan includes a trial of Notion AI only; Granola: Basic: $0; Mem: Free: 25 new notes/month.

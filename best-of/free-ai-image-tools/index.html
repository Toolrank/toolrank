The short answer

These AI image tools have a free plan or are free to use, and we confirmed it on each vendor's own pricing page. Recraft comes first: low-cost plans with multiple models and design-focused output. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Google Gemini (Nano Banana) Google AI Plus ($4.99/month). 10 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–20 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Recraft for designers making brand-consistent graphics and vectors (Free: $0); think twice if you need commercial use on a free plan. Pick Krea for creators who want many models in one place (Free: $0); think twice if you only need one model and want the cheapest route. Pick GPT Image (ChatGPT) for making and editing images by chatting, inside the ChatGPT app you may already pay for (Free: $0/month). Watch out: the free plan's image generation is limited; Thinking image generation needs Plus or Pro. Pick Google Gemini (Nano Banana) for generating and editing images by chatting, inside the Gemini app people already use (Free: $0/month). Watch out: higher usage limits need a paid Google AI plan. Pick Leonardo.Ai for image and video generation with trainable models (Free: $0/month). Watch out: free-tier creations are public; listed prices exclude tax and the monthly/yearly toggle state was not confirmed. Pick Stability AI for developers and small studios running open image models on their own hardware (Community: Free). Watch out: businesses over $1M revenue need an Enterprise licence. Pick Canva AI for non-designers making social posts, presentations and marketing graphics with AI help (Free: US$0/year). Watch out: AI use is capped by a shared allowance; heavier AI needs the AI Pass add-on at extra cost.

Best AI image tools for $15 a month or less

Updated 17 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $15 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. Midjourney comes first: consistently strong image aesthetics with simple GPU-time based plans and commercial use rights. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Photoroom Pro ($7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly). 3 of the 9 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–17 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  2. Ideogram: Credit-based plans with unlimited slow generations and character consistency. Plan: Plus: $15/month annual ($20 monthly)

  6. Leonardo.Ai: Image and video generation platform with fine-tuned models; owned by Canva. Plan: Essential: $12/month
    8,500 tokens/month
  7. Freepik AI: Image and video generation suite hosting many models. Plan: Premium: $14.50/month annually or $20 monthly
  8. Magnific: AI image upscaler and enhancer. Plan: Premium: $14.50/month billed annually or $20 monthly

  9. Photoroom: AI background removal and product photo editing. Plan: Pro: $7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly

Who should pick which

Pick Midjourney for artists and marketers who want striking, stylised images (Basic: $10/month); think twice if you need a free tier or precise text in images. Pick Ideogram for posters, logos and graphics where legible text matters (Plus: $15/month annual ($20 monthly)); think twice if you need video-first workflows. Pick Recraft for designers making brand-consistent graphics and vectors (Basic: $10/month billed annually ($120)); think twice if you need commercial use on a free plan. Pick Krea for creators who want many models in one place (Basic: $9/month ($5 annual)); think twice if you only need one model and want the cheapest route. Pick Adobe Firefly for designers who want commercially focused generative images, video and audio inside Adobe's ecosystem (Firefly Standard: US$9.99/month). Watch out: premium features such as text-to-video use up generative credits. Pick Leonardo.Ai for image and video generation with trainable models (Essential: $12/month). Watch out: free-tier creations are public; listed prices exclude tax and the monthly/yearly toggle state was not confirmed. Pick Freepik AI for AI images plus stock content in one subscription (Premium: $14.50/month annually or $20 monthly). Watch out: brand now shown as Magnific (formerly Freepik).

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Photoroom Pro ($7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.photoroom.com/pricing, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Recraft: Limited free credits, personal use only; Krea: 100 units/day; Leonardo.Ai: Free: $0/month, 150 fast tokens/day, public creations.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $15/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–17 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

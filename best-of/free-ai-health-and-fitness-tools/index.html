FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Cronometer Gold (annual) ($59.99/year ($4.99/month billed annually)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://cronometer.com/gold/, checked 21 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Wysa: Talking to Wysa AI is free, including tools offered within conversations; MyFitnessPal: Free version: nutrition, exercise and weight tracking; Cronometer: Free version: log foods, exercise, biometrics; track up to 84 nutrients; Strava: Free: record activities, community and safety features; Heidi Health: Free: one free seat, unlimited transcription, standard templates.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a free plan or free use (not just a trial), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 19–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

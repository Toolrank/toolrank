Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $30 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. Who should pick which

Pick Claude for long-form writing, analysis and research with documents (Pro: $17/month annual or $20 monthly); think twice if you need unlimited heavy usage on a low budget. Pick Google Gemini for people already in Google's ecosystem (Gmail, Drive, Photos) (Lower paid tier: $4.99/month); think twice if you don't use Google services and want a standalone assistant. Pick Microsoft Copilot for people and teams living in Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams (Microsoft 365 Copilot Business: $18/user/month paid yearly (shown as reduced from $21)); think twice if you don't use Microsoft 365. Pick Le Chat (Mistral) for a European alternative assistant (Team: $24.99/user/month). Watch out: pro price not read reliably.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Google Gemini Lower paid tier ($4.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://gemini.google/subscriptions/, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Claude: Free: chat on web, desktop and mobile, with usage limits; Google Gemini: $0/month with a Google Account (Flash model, base usage limits); Le Chat (Mistral): Free plan available.

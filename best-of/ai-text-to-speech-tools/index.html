These tools turn written text into natural-sounding speech. ElevenLabs comes first: top-tier voice quality with a free tier and a cheap Starter plan that adds commercial rights. Who should pick which

Pick ElevenLabs for creators and developers needing realistic voiceovers or voice agents (Starter: $6/month); think twice if you need commercial audio for free. Pick Speechify for listening to documents and web pages read aloud (Premium: $29/month billed monthly). Watch out: premium is expensive on monthly billing. Pick Murf for text-to-speech voiceovers and voice agents (Pay as you go API ($0.01/1,000 characters, Falcon TTS)). Watch out: studio plan prices not verified. Pick Cartesia for developers building low-latency voice and TTS (Pro: $5/month). Watch out: credit-based; heavy use needs higher tiers.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Cartesia Pro ($5/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://cartesia.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

ElevenLabs: 10k credits/month, 3 Studio projects, no commercial license; Speechify: Free plan: listen at speeds up to 1.5x; Murf: $10 free API credit; Cartesia: Free: $0/month, 20K credits/month.

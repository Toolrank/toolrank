Who should pick which

Pick HostBuddy AI for airbnb and vacation rental hosts who want guest messages answered 24/7 by AI (Pro: $7/month per property, billed monthly). Watch out: no free plan after the 2-week trial; Pro limits conversation history to 30 days and has no team collaboration. Pick Guesty for hosts with 1–3 listings who may grow into a full property-management platform (Lite: From $9/month per listing). Watch out: the Pro and Enterprise plans are quoted by listing count. Pick PriceLabs for hosts and managers who want automated nightly pricing they can run from an AI assistant (Dynamic Pricing: rest of world) ($9.99/month per listing, plus tax). Watch out: no free plan; the price is per listing, and higher in the US, UK, Europe and Australia. Pick Turno for airbnb and Vrbo hosts who want cleaning scheduled and paid automatically (Marketplace & Non-Turno Teammates: $10/month per property billed monthly; $96 per property billed annually). Watch out: using your own cleaners on 2+ properties costs $10 a month per property. Pick Lodgify for hosts with a few properties who want their own booking website and synced calendars (Basic: From $14/month). Watch out: price rises with property count and plan; automated messaging starts on Professional. Pick Wheelhouse for hosts and operators who want data-driven nightly prices without a long contract (Pro Flat: $19.99/month per listing). Watch out: paid pricing is per listing, so costs grow with your portfolio. Pick Host Tools for hosts with a few listings who want hands-off guest messaging and reviews (Messaging: $28/month billed yearly ($336/year) or $30/month monthly). Watch out: no free plan; the price rises with every listing you add.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

HostBuddy AI Pro ($7/month per property, billed monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://hostbuddy.ai/pricing, checked 24 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Turno: Free for 1 property, or for any number using only Turno Marketplace cleaners; Wheelhouse: Free plan: link listings, market report and settings, no credit card; Tokeet: Free plan (stated on pricing page); 15-day free trial, no credit card.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a paid plan price we could read on the vendor page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 20–24 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

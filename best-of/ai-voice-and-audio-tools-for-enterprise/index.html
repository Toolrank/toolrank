Best AI voice and audio tools for enterprise buyers

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI voice and audio tools lists an Enterprise or custom-priced plan, the usual route to SSO, contracts and volume pricing. ElevenLabs comes first: top-tier voice quality with a free tier and a cheap Starter plan that adds commercial rights. The cheapest verified paid plan here is ElevenLabs Starter ($6/month). 9 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–22 Oct 2026.

Which of these is cheapest?

ElevenLabs Starter ($6/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://elevenlabs.io/pricing, checked 19 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

ElevenLabs: 10k credits/month, 3 Studio projects, no commercial license; Descript: Free: 60 media minutes/month, 720p export, 5GB storage; Wispr Flow: Free on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android (limited dictations); Otter.ai: Basic: free forever; Murf: $10 free API credit; Deepgram: $200 free credit, then pay-as-you-go; AssemblyAI: Free to start, then pay-as-you-go; Aqua Voice: Free: 1,000 words, Avalon transcription model, no card; Willow: Basic: free, unlimited Frontier Mini dictation, 20 Scribe uses a week.

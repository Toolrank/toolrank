Best AI image tools with mobile apps

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI image tools lists an iOS, Android or mobile app among its platforms. Krea comes first: access to many image/video models plus an agent, with a daily free allowance. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Google Gemini (Nano Banana) Google AI Plus ($4.99/month). 5 of the 7 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–20 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Krea for creators who want many models in one place (Basic: $9/month ($5 annual)); think twice if you only need one model and want the cheapest route. Pick GPT Image (ChatGPT) for making and editing images by chatting, inside the ChatGPT app you may already pay for (Go: $8/month). Watch out: the free plan's image generation is limited; Thinking image generation needs Plus or Pro. Pick Google Gemini (Nano Banana) for generating and editing images by chatting, inside the Gemini app people already use (Google AI Plus: $4.99/month). Watch out: higher usage limits need a paid Google AI plan. Pick Adobe Firefly for designers who want commercially focused generative images, video and audio inside Adobe's ecosystem (Firefly Standard: US$9.99/month). Watch out: premium features such as text-to-video use up generative credits. Pick Leonardo.Ai for image and video generation with trainable models (Essential: $12/month). Watch out: free-tier creations are public; listed prices exclude tax and the monthly/yearly toggle state was not confirmed. Pick Canva AI for non-designers making social posts, presentations and marketing graphics with AI help (US$144/year (Pro, one person)). Watch out: AI use is capped by a shared allowance; heavier AI needs the AI Pass add-on at extra cost. Pick Photoroom for online sellers making product photos with background removal and AI backgrounds (Pro: $7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly). Watch out: API and MCP usage uses a separate credit allowance.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Google Gemini (Nano Banana) Google AI Plus ($4.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://gemini.google/subscriptions/, checked 19 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Krea: 100 units/day; GPT Image (ChatGPT): ChatGPT Free: limited image generation; Google Gemini (Nano Banana): Free: $0/month with a Google Account, image generation and editing included; Leonardo.Ai: Free: $0/month, 150 fast tokens/day, public creations; Canva AI: Free: US$0, up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses, 5GB storage.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a mobile app listed on the vendor site, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–20 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

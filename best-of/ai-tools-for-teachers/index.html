Best AI tools for teachers

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools give teachers AI help with lessons, feedback or practice sets, with free teacher access or a teacher plan confirmed on each vendor’s site. Khanmigo comes first: tutoring tied to Khan Academy content, free for teachers and cheap for learners. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Khanmigo Learners ($4/month or $44/year). 4 of the 4 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–19 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Khanmigo: Tutoring tied to Khan Academy content, free for teachers and cheap for learners. Plan: Teachers: Free
  2. Quizlet: Flashcards and study sets with AI-generated practice. Plan: Not yet verified
  3. MagicSchool: Free forever for individual teachers, with school-safe privacy features. Plan: Free: $0
  4. Brisk Teaching: Chrome extension that helps teachers create, give feedback and differentiate. Plan: Free: $0

Who should pick which

Pick Khanmigo for students wanting guided tutoring; teachers wanting free planning help (Teachers: Free); think twice if you need subjects beyond Khan Academy's content library. Pick Quizlet for teachers assigning flashcards and practice that students can do free, and students studying with Plus (free plan: Teacher-assigned activities are free for students (no account needed)). Watch out: prices are shown at checkout in local currency, so we could not verify them. Pick MagicSchool for teachers planning lessons and differentiating materials (Free: $0); think twice if you're a self-learner rather than an educator. Pick Brisk Teaching for teachers creating lessons and feedback in the browser (Free: $0). Watch out: school pricing not verified.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Khanmigo Learners ($4/month or $44/year) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Khanmigo: Free for teachers; Quizlet: Teacher-assigned activities are free for students (no account needed); MagicSchool: Forever free for individual teachers; Brisk Teaching: Free for teachers ($0).

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a teacher plan or free teacher access on the vendor site, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–19 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

AI research and productivity tools that are cheaper billed yearly

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI research and productivity tools shows both a yearly and a monthly price, so you can see what committing for a year saves. Perplexity comes first: cited answers across top models with deep research, free to start. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Gamma Plus ($9/seat/month billed annually ($108/seat/year)). 6 of the 9 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–17 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Perplexity: Cited answers across top models with deep research, free to start. Plan: Pro: $17/month billed annually
    Deep research, top models, 4,000 bonus credits
  2. Gamma: Generates presentations, docs and sites from a prompt. Plan: Plus: $9/seat/month billed annually ($108/seat/year)
    1,000 monthly credits, no Gamma branding
  3. Scite: Shows how papers are cited: supporting or contrasting. Plan: Basic: $12/month billed yearly
  4. SciSpace: AI research assistant for reading and searching papers. Plan: Premium: $12/month billed annually or $20 monthly
    1,200 monthly credits, deep research
  5. Undermind: Deep academic literature search agent. Plan: Pro: $16/month billed annually
  6. Felo: Multilingual AI search engine. Plan: Pro (annual): $12.50/month billed yearly ($149.99)
    15,000 credits/month + 200 daily bonus, API access
  7. Liner: AI research assistant with cited sources. Plan: Pro: $14.99/month annually or $17.99 monthly
  8. Research Rabbit: Literature discovery and citation mapping. Plan: ResearchRabbit+: $10/month annual or $12.50 monthly
    Up to 300 seed articles
  9. Beautiful.ai: AI presentation software with smart templates. Plan: Pro: $14.50/month billed annually

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Research & Productivity, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Perplexity for fast researched answers with sources (Pro: $17/month billed annually); think twice if you need long creative writing rather than research. Pick Gamma for turning a prompt or document into a presentation, doc or simple website (Plus: $9/seat/month billed annually ($108/seat/year)). Watch out: free credits are a one-off allowance at signup. Pick Scite for checking how papers have been cited (Basic: $12/month billed yearly). Watch out: no permanent free plan. Pick SciSpace for researchers doing literature reviews and chatting with papers (Premium: $12/month billed annually or $20 monthly). Watch out: the upper plans are expensive for individuals. Pick Undermind for deep literature searches for researchers (Pro: $16/month billed annually). Watch out: monthly billing costs more (20% saved annually). Pick Felo for multilingual AI search with slides, deep research and many models in one plan (Pro: annual) ($12.50/month billed yearly ($149.99)). Watch out: API access is not available on the free plan. Pick Liner for source-cited AI search for students (Pro: $14.99/month annually or $17.99 monthly). Watch out: free plan shows ads.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Gamma Plus ($9/seat/month billed annually ($108/seat/year)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://gamma.app/pricing, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Perplexity: Free sign-up; limits not specified on page; Gamma: Free: $0, 400 credits at signup, up to 10 slides per prompt; Undermind: Free: $0 for exploratory research; Felo: Free: no payment details, 200 credits refreshed daily; Liner: Free: $0, answers with ads; Research Rabbit: Free: $0 forever.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with both annual and monthly prices on the vendor page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–17 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

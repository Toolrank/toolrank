Best paid AI image tools (no free plan)

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These AI image tools are paid products: no permanent free plan is listed on their pricing pages. Midjourney comes first: consistently strong image aesthetics with simple GPU-time based plans and commercial use rights. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Midjourney Basic ($10/month). None of the 4 has a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

Who should pick which

Pick Midjourney for artists and marketers who want striking, stylised images (Basic: $10/month); think twice if you need a free tier or precise text in images. Pick Ideogram for posters, logos and graphics where legible text matters (Plus: $15/month annual ($20 monthly)); think twice if you need video-first workflows. Pick Black Forest Labs (FLUX) for developers embedding image generation in products (from $0.048/image (example rate on pricing calculator)); think twice if you want a no-code consumer app. Pick Freepik AI for AI images plus stock content in one subscription (Premium: $14.50/month annually or $20 monthly). Watch out: brand now shown as Magnific (formerly Freepik).

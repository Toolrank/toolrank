Which of these is cheapest?

ClickUp Unlimited ($7/user/month yearly ($10 monthly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://clickup.com/pricing, checked 18 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Airtable: Free plan at no cost for users getting started; ClickUp: Free Forever: 60MB storage, unlimited tasks and members; Fireflies.ai: Unlimited transcription, limited AI summaries, 400 mins storage/team, 20 AI credits; Fathom: Free forever: unlimited recordings and instant summaries; Writer: Starter: 14-day free trial, no card; Superhuman: Free: $0, AI assistant in your apps; Glide: Free: limited credits.

Best AI video tools for enterprise buyers

Updated 22 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI video tools lists an Enterprise or custom-priced plan, the usual route to SSO, contracts and volume pricing. HeyGen comes first: fastest path to presenter-style avatar videos, with a free tier to test. The cheapest verified paid plan here is OpusClip Starter ($15/month). 7 of the 8 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–20 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. HeyGen: Fastest path to presenter-style avatar videos, with a free tier to test. Plan: Enterprise: Contact sales
  2. Synthesia: AI avatar video platform for training and corporate videos in 140+ languages. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
    SSO, unlimited personal avatars
  3. Higgsfield: Video generation with cinematic camera-motion presets, hosting several models. Plan: Enterprise: Contact sales
  4. OpusClip: Turns long videos into short vertical clips automatically. Plan: Business: Custom
    SSO, unlimited seats
  5. Descript (video): Edit video by editing its transcript. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
    SSO/SCIM
  6. Colossyan: AI avatar video creator focused on workplace learning. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
    Unlimited minutes, SSO, data residency
  7. Veed: Browser video editor with AI subtitles, avatars and clean-up tools. Plan: Enterprise: Custom pricing
    Team management, privacy controls
  8. Wonder Studio (Autodesk Flow Studio): Autodesk tool that replaces actors with CG characters from live footage. Plan: Enterprise: Contact sales

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Video & Animation, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick HeyGen for marketers and trainers producing avatar explainer videos (Enterprise: Contact sales); think twice if you need cinematic generative video. Synthesia (Enterprise: Custom): AI avatar video platform for training and corporate videos in 140+ languages. FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

OpusClip Starter ($15/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.opus.pro/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

HeyGen: 3 videos/month, up to 1 min each; Synthesia: Basic: 10 minutes of video/month, 500 credits, Synthesia watermark; OpusClip: Free: watermarked clips, 3-day export window, 9:16 only; Descript (video): Free: 60 media minutes/month, 720p export, 5GB storage; Colossyan: Starter: free, 20 video minutes/month with NEO avatars, watermark; Veed: Start for free (free plan limits not listed on the pricing page); Wonder Studio (Autodesk Flow Studio): Free tier for exploring AI-powered VFX (monthly credits).

Best AI marketing tools for $100 a month or less

Updated 22 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $100 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. Jasper comes first: marketing-focused AI with brand controls and team governance. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). 3 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–22 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Jasper: Marketing-focused AI with brand controls and team governance. Plan: Pro: $59/month yearly or $69 monthly
    1 seat
  2. Copy.ai: Combines chat with go-to-market workflows for teams. Plan: Self-serve: $24/month annual ($29 monthly)
  3. HubSpot Breeze: HubSpot's AI agents and assistants built into its CRM and marketing hub. Plan: Starter: $20/seat/month
    Limited-time offer for new customers from $7/seat/month; 500 HubSpot Credits, 1,000 marketing contacts, Agent Hub and Agent Builder
  4. Buffer: Free plan with AI Assistant and API access; paid plans from $5 per channel. Plan: Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)
    Unlimited posts, advanced analytics
  5. Surfer: Combines a content editor with AI visibility tracking, with MCP and API on higher plans. Plan: Discovery: $49/month
    Content drafting and optimisation
  6. Predis.ai: Creates and publishes ads in one tool, with mobile apps and e-commerce integrations. Plan: Core: $19/month billed yearly ($32 monthly)
    1 brand, 1,300 credits; unlimited team members
  7. Ocoya: Simple monthly plans for creators, teams and agencies. Plan: Starter: $29/month
    1 user, 5 social profiles, 300 credits
  8. Writesonic: Pairs AI visibility tracking with articles and audits, with a free trial and no card. Plan: Starter: $79/month billed annually
    1 user, 50 prompts tracked, 15 AI articles/month
  9. Metricool: A free plan for one brand, low per-brand pricing, plus MCP and API access. Plan: Starter: From $20/month billed annually; from $25/month billed monthly
    Up to 10 brands, unlimited publishing, LinkedIn
  10. Hootsuite: A 14-day trial with no card, unlimited scheduling and AI post and image generation. Plan: Standard: $99 per user/month, billed annually
    Up to 10 social accounts, AI posts and images

Who should pick which

Pick Jasper for marketing teams producing on-brand content at scale (Pro: $59/month yearly or $69 monthly); think twice if you're an individual on a budget. Pick Copy.ai for GTM teams automating content workflows (Self-serve: $24/month annual ($29 monthly)); think twice if you just need occasional copywriting. Pick HubSpot Breeze for marketing teams already on, or moving to, the HubSpot CRM who want AI agents built in (Starter: $20/seat/month). Watch out: AI agents run on HubSpot Credits, and Professional and Enterprise add mandatory onboarding fees. Pick Buffer for creators and small teams scheduling posts on several social networks on a budget (Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). Watch out: free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel and 1 user. Pick Surfer for content teams who want to rank in Google and get cited in AI answers (Discovery: $49/month). Watch out: no free plan; API access is on the top Peace of Mind plan. Pick Predis.ai for small brands and agencies making social ads and posts without a designer (Core: $19/month billed yearly ($32 monthly)). Watch out: the 3-day trial needs a card and is charged when it ends. Pick Ocoya for teams and agencies managing several brands' social posts (Starter: $29/month). Watch out: no free plan; the 7-day trial needs a card.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://buffer.com/pricing, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

HubSpot Breeze: Free tools: $0/month for up to 2 users, no credit card; Buffer: Free plan: $0 forever, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant; Metricool: Free: 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month, AI assistant, MCP.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $100/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

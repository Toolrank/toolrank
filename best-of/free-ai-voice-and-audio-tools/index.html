Best free AI voice and audio tools (with a real free plan)

Updated 17 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These AI voice and audio tools have a free plan or are free to use, and we confirmed it on each vendor's own pricing page. ElevenLabs comes first: top-tier voice quality with a free tier and a cheap Starter plan that adds commercial rights. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Cartesia Pro ($5/month). 10 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–17 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick ElevenLabs for creators and developers needing realistic voiceovers or voice agents (Free: $0); think twice if you need commercial audio for free. Pick Suno for musicians and creators sketching complete songs (Free: $0); think twice if you need commercial rights without paying. Pick Udio for experimenting with AI music styles and edits (Free: $0); think twice if you need large monthly volumes cheaply. Pick Descript for podcasters and video creators editing talking content (Free: $0); think twice if you need frame-level pro VFX editing. Pick Wispr Flow for anyone who'd rather talk than type (Free: $0); think twice if you only need occasional built-in OS dictation. Pick OpenAI Whisper for developers transcribing audio locally for free (free plan: Open source (MIT); runs locally). Watch out: needs your own hardware (a GPU helps); no hosted app. Pick Otter.ai for meeting transcription and notes for individuals and small teams (Basic: Free). Watch out: promotional discounts were shown on the pricing page; check the current price at checkout.

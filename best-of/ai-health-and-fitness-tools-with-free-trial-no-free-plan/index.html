AI health and fitness tools with a free trial (paid plans only)

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

None of these AI health and fitness tools has a permanent free plan, but each offers a trial confirmed on its pricing page. Oura comes first: comfortable 24/7 health tracking with a low monthly membership. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Oura Membership (US) ($5.99/month or $69.99/year before tax). None of the 8 has a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–23 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Oura: Comfortable 24/7 health tracking with a low monthly membership. Plan: Membership (US): $5.99/month or $69.99/year before tax
  2. WHOOP: Membership-based recovery tracking with the device included. Plan: Not yet verified
  3. Garmin Connect+: Cheap add-on for Garmin owners; existing Garmin Connect features stay free. Plan: Monthly plan: $6.99/month
  4. Google Health Premium: An AI coach that builds and adjusts workout plans, from Google, at $9.99 a month. Plan: Google Health Premium: $9.99/month after the trial
    Google Health Coach built with Gemini
  5. Runna: Adaptive plans for 5K to marathon with wide watch support. Plan: Annual: $119.99/year ($9.99/month, billed annually)
    Save 50%
  6. Lyrebird Health: Unlimited notes, a 14-day trial and integrations with Best Practice and Cubiko. Plan: Pro: $160/month billed annually
    Unlimited notes, letters, care plans; team template sharing
  7. Freed: A 7-day trial with no card, specialty templates and group plans for clinics. Plan: Starter: $39/mo
    1 clinician, up to 40 notes a month
  8. Twofold Health: A free week with no card, iOS and Android apps, and group plans with volume pricing. Plan: Not yet verified

Who should pick which

Pick Oura for sleep and recovery tracking without a wrist device (Membership: US) ($5.99/month or $69.99/year before tax); think twice if you don't want an ongoing subscription. Pick WHOOP for athletes optimising training load and recovery ($199/year (One)); think twice if you want a one-off hardware purchase with no subscription. Pick Garmin Connect+ for owners of Garmin watches who want AI insights on their health and training data (Monthly plan: $6.99/month). Watch out: needs a compatible Garmin device; Active Intelligence was released in beta. Pick Google Health Premium for owners of a Fitbit or Pixel Watch who want an AI coach that adapts plans to their data (Google Health Premium: $9.99/month after the trial). Watch out: needs a compatible wearable and a Google account. Pick Runna for runners training for a race who want a plan that adapts to their runs (Annual: $119.99/year ($9.99/month, billed annually)). Watch out: no free plan after the 7-day trial. Pick Lyrebird Health for GPs and clinics, especially in Australia, who want notes and care plans automated (Pro: $160/month billed annually). Watch out: no free plan; only the annual-billing price was shown. Pick Freed for clinicians and small clinics who want notes done minutes after each visit (Starter: $39/mo). Watch out: no free plan; Core and Premier prices were not clear on the fetched page.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Oura Membership (US) ($5.99/month or $69.99/year before tax) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://ouraring.com/membership, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

None of these has a free plan we could confirm. None lists a permanent free plan on its pricing page. Some offer a trial; see the table.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a trial but no permanent free plan, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–23 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best AI website and app builders

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools build and publish websites or web apps in the browser. Lovable comes first: turns plain-language prompts into working web apps, with hosting via Lovable Cloud and a free daily allowance. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Framer Basic ($10/month (yearly)). 6 of the 7 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Lovable: Turns plain-language prompts into working web apps, with hosting via Lovable Cloud and a free daily allowance. Plan: Not yet verified
  2. Bolt.new: Generous token-based free tier and simple Pro/Teams pricing for in-browser app building. Plan: Pro: $25/month
    No daily limit, from 10M tokens/month, no branding
  3. v0: Vercel's generator for React/Next.js UIs and apps, deploys to Vercel. Plan: Business: $100/user/month
  4. Replit: Replit Agent covers the whole loop in a browser: prompt, build, database, deploy, share. Paid plans include model credit. Plan: Core: $20/month ($18 annual)
    $20 toward powerful models, Plan mode, unlimited workspaces
  5. Base44: Prompt-to-app builder with built-in database, auth and hosting; owned by Wix. Plan: Paid tier: $40/month billed annually
  6. Webflow: Visual website builder with AI site and page generation. Plan: Basic: $15/month billed yearly
  7. Framer: Design-first website builder with AI page generation. Plan: Basic: $10/month (yearly)
    1,000 AI credits/month

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Coding & App Building, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Lovable for non-developers and founders prototyping web apps fast (free plan: 5 build credits/day (up to 30/month) plus 20 Cloud credits/month); think twice if you need predictable flat costs for heavy iteration. Pick Bolt.new for quick full-stack prototypes and small business apps (Pro: $25/month); think twice if you'll burn through tokens on large projects without budget for upgrades. Pick v0 for generating React/Next.js UI and deploying to Vercel (Business: $100/user/month). Watch out: usage is credit-based. Pick Replit for founders and builders who want an agent to build and host an app without local setup (Core: $20/month ($18 annual)); think twice if you want to work in your own local repo and editor. Pick Base44 for building full apps from a prompt (Paid tier: $40/month billed annually). Watch out: credits cap AI messages and integration actions. Pick Webflow for designer-grade marketing sites (Basic: $15/month billed yearly). Watch out: site and workspace plans are billed separately. Pick Framer for designing and publishing websites visually (Basic: $10/month (yearly)). Watch out: free sites use a Framer domain.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Framer Basic ($10/month (yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.framer.com/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Lovable: 5 build credits/day (up to 30/month) plus 20 Cloud credits/month; Bolt.new: 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month, Bolt branding, 10MB uploads, unlimited databases; v0: Free: $0/month, deploy apps to Vercel; Base44: Free to start; Webflow: Starter: free, webflow.io domain; Framer: Free: 500 AI credits, Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth.

Best AI tools that work with Canva

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these tools is Canva or lists Canva among its integrations. Canva AI comes first: canva's design platform with built-in AI image generation. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). 6 of the 7 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 18–23 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Canva AI: Canva's design platform with built-in AI image generation. Plan: Not yet verified
  2. Buffer: Free plan with AI Assistant and API access; paid plans from $5 per channel. Plan: Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)
    Unlimited posts, advanced analytics
  3. Metricool: A free plan for one brand, low per-brand pricing, plus MCP and API access. Plan: Starter: From $20/month billed annually; from $25/month billed monthly
    Up to 10 brands, unlimited publishing, LinkedIn
  4. Publer: A free-forever plan, mobile app and MCP and API access on paid plans. Plan: Not yet verified
  5. Diffit: A free Basic plan for teachers plus an individual Premium plan with exports. Plan: Individual teacher: $14.99/month billed monthly or $149.99/year billed annually
    Save 16% yearly; exports and standards alignment
  6. SchoolAI: A free trial tier for teachers, LMS integration and SOC 2 Type 2 compliance. Plan: Not yet verified
  7. Edcafe: A free-forever plan and paid plans from $7.99 a month billed annually. Plan: Pro: $7.99/mo billed annually
    20 chatbots, 200 participants

Tools come from several ToolRank categories, so they are grouped by category and then by each category’s editorial rank. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Canva AI for non-designers making social posts, presentations and marketing graphics with AI help (US$144/year (Pro, one person)). Watch out: AI use is capped by a shared allowance; heavier AI needs the AI Pass add-on at extra cost. Pick Buffer for creators and small teams scheduling posts on several social networks on a budget (Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). Watch out: free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel and 1 user. Pick Metricool for freelancers and agencies scheduling and reporting on several brands' social accounts (Starter: From $20/month billed annually; from $25/month billed monthly). Watch out: LinkedIn and X need a paid plan; the free plan caps posts at 20 a month. Pick Publer for solo creators and small teams who want cheap scheduling and AI-agent access (free plan: Free forever: 1 user, 3 social accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account). Watch out: paid prices depend on account count and were not shown in the fetched text. Pick Diffit for teachers who need the same content at several reading levels (ELL, SPED, intervention) (Individual teacher: $14.99/month billed monthly or $149.99/year billed annually). Watch out: exports to Google, Microsoft and Canva need Premium or a school licence. Pick SchoolAI for schools and districts rolling out AI tutoring with teacher oversight (price not yet verified). Watch out: pro and Scale prices are not published; the free tier caps Space launches at 5 a year. Pick Edcafe for teachers who want low-cost AI activities and grading for their classes (Pro: $7.99/mo billed annually). Watch out: monthly-billing prices were not shown; LMS integration is for schools.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://buffer.com/pricing, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Canva AI: Free: US$0, up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses, 5GB storage; Buffer: Free plan: $0 forever, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant; Metricool: Free: 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month, AI assistant, MCP; Publer: Free forever: 1 user, 3 social accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account; Diffit: Basic: free (PDF download, print, share; last 90 days of resources); Edcafe: Free: unlimited AI generations, 3 chatbots, 40 participants.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with Canva listed by the vendor, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 18–23 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

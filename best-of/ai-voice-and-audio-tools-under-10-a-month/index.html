Best AI voice and audio tools for $10 a month or less

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $10 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. ElevenLabs comes first: top-tier voice quality with a free tier and a cheap Starter plan that adds commercial rights. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Cartesia Pro ($5/month). 7 of the 8 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–22 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. ElevenLabs: Top-tier voice quality with a free tier and a cheap Starter plan that adds commercial rights. Plan: Starter: $6/month
    30k credits, commercial license, instant cloning
  2. Suno: The most capable consumer song generator, with a daily free allowance. Plan: Pro: $8/month (billed yearly)
    2,500 credits/month, 20 downloads, commercial use
  3. Udio: Solid music generator with editing tools and a no-card free tier. Plan: Standard: $8/month annual ($10 monthly)
    2,400 credits/month
  4. Otter.ai: Meeting transcription and AI notes. Plan: Pro: $8.33/user/month annually or $16.99 monthly
  5. Cartesia: Low-latency text-to-speech API for real-time voice. Plan: Pro: $5/month
  6. Krisp: Noise cancellation and meeting notes. Plan: Core: $8/user/month annually or $16 monthly
    Unlimited AI note-taker, bot-free
  7. LALAL.AI: Stem splitting: vocals and instrument separation. Plan: Lite: $7.50/month ($90 billed annually)
  8. Aqua Voice: A free tier, low Pro price and SOC 2 Type II for teams. Plan: Pro: $8/month billed annually; $10/month month-to-month
    Unlimited words, custom instructions

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Voice & Audio, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick ElevenLabs for creators and developers needing realistic voiceovers, voice agents or AI-generated music (Eleven Music) (Starter: $6/month); think twice if you need commercial audio for free. Pick Suno for musicians and creators sketching complete songs (Pro: $8/month (billed yearly)); think twice if you need commercial rights without paying. Pick Udio for experimenting with AI music styles and edits (Standard: $8/month annual ($10 monthly)); think twice if you need large monthly volumes cheaply. Pick Otter.ai for meeting transcription and notes for individuals and small teams (Pro: $8.33/user/month annually or $16.99 monthly). Watch out: promotional discounts were shown on the pricing page; check the current price at checkout. Pick Cartesia for developers building low-latency voice and TTS (Pro: $5/month). Watch out: credit-based; heavy use needs higher tiers. Pick Krisp for bot-free meeting notes and noise cancellation (Core: $8/user/month annually or $16 monthly). Watch out: no permanent free plan listed, only a 7-day trial. Pick LALAL.AI for separating vocals and stems from audio (Lite: $7.50/month ($90 billed annually)). Watch out: free plan is limited.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Cartesia Pro ($5/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://cartesia.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

ElevenLabs: 10k credits/month, 3 Studio projects, no commercial license; Suno: 50 credits/day, free models, standard features; Udio: 10 credits/day, 100/month, no rollovers; Otter.ai: Basic: free forever; Cartesia: Free: $0/month, 20K credits/month; LALAL.AI: Starter: always free; Aqua Voice: Free: 1,000 words, Avalon transcription model, no card.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $10/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best AI tools under $10 a month (all categories)

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here, from any category, has a paid plan listed at $10 a month or less. ChatGPT comes first: the broadest general assistant: a usable free plan, apps on every major platform and a ladder of paid plans for heavier use. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Google Gemini Google AI Plus ($4.99/month). 9 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–23 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. ChatGPT: The broadest general assistant: a usable free plan, apps on every major platform and a ladder of paid plans for heavier use. Plan: Go: $8/month
    More messages, uploads and image creation; limited Codex; may include ads
  2. Google Gemini: Low-cost entry tier and deep ties to Google storage, Search and creative tools. Plan: Google AI Plus: $4.99/month
    2x Free usage
  3. Grok: Fast-moving assistant bundled with X plans and Cursor, with image and video generation built in. Plan: Plus: $8/month
    Shared AI usage pool; Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine and Build; Grok API access; X Premium checkmark
  4. Lumo (Proton): A free plan with no card, chats never used for training, and Plus under $10 a month billed yearly. Plan: Lumo Plus: $12.99/month billed monthly; $9.99/month billed yearly ($119.88 every 12 months)
    More Max model usage, unlimited Projects
  5. Cursor: A full AI-native editor with agents, cloud automations and code review, and a free tier to start. Plan: Plus: $8/month
    Shared AI usage pool, Grok models only
  6. GitHub Copilot: Works in most major editors and in GitHub itself, from a free plan up to cloud agents and code review. Plan: Pro: $10/month
  7. OpenAI Codex: OpenAI's coding agent across CLI, IDE and cloud. Plan: Go: $8/month
    More messages, uploads and image creation; limited Codex; may include ads
  8. Zed: A very fast native editor that's free forever with your own keys or external agents like Claude Agent and Codex CLI. Pro is a low $10/month. Plan: Pro: $10/month
    Unlimited edit predictions, $5 tokens included, 14-day trial
  9. JetBrains AI (Junie): Built into IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm and the other JetBrains IDEs. JetBrains Air runs Junie, Claude Agent, Codex and other agents, and lets you bring your own keys. Plan: AI Pro: $10/month ($100/year)
    Personal pricing
  10. Macaly: Prompt-to-website and app builder for non-developers. Plan: Starter: $10/month

Tools come from several ToolRank categories, so they are grouped by category and then by each category’s editorial rank. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick ChatGPT for everyday drafting, Q&A and file analysis across web and mobile (Go: $8/month); think twice if you need guaranteed access to the strongest models without paying. Pick Google Gemini for people already in Google's ecosystem (Gmail, Drive, Photos) (Google AI Plus: $4.99/month); think twice if you don't use Google services and want a standalone assistant. Pick Grok for x users who want real-time context plus image/video generation (Plus: $8/month); think twice if you want a clearly documented free usage allowance. Pick Lumo (Proton) for people who want a ChatGPT-style assistant with strong privacy (Lumo Plus: $12.99/month billed monthly; $9.99/month billed yearly ($119.88 every 12 months)). Watch out: free plan limits messages and Max model use; Business plan price not shown. Pick Cursor for developers who want an AI-first editor with agents and code review (Plus: $8/month); think twice if you want flat pricing that includes third-party models. Pick GitHub Copilot for teams already on GitHub who want AI in their existing IDE (Pro: $10/month); think twice if you want an AI-native editor rather than a plugin. Pick OpenAI Codex for ChatGPT subscribers who want one coding agent across the terminal, IDE, desktop app and cloud (Go: $8/month). Watch out: usage limits depend on your ChatGPT plan; Plus and Pro users who hit them must buy extra credits.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Google Gemini Google AI Plus ($4.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://gemini.google/subscriptions/, checked 19 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

ChatGPT: Free plan: unlimited* everyday text chats, limited messages with uploads (vendor footnotes apply); Google Gemini: $0/month with a Google Account (Flash-Lite model, base usage limits); Grok: Free: $0/month, limited chat and agent requests; Lumo (Proton): Free plan: limited messages, Max model use and image generations, no credit card; Cursor: Free: limited agent requests and limited chat requests; GitHub Copilot: Limited chat and agent usage; no code review or cloud agent; OpenAI Codex: Included in ChatGPT Free with limited Codex access; Zed: Personal: free forever, unlimited with your own API keys or external agents; Macaly: Free: $0/month, publish on a free address.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $10/month, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–23 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

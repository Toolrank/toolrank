Best AI tools under $10 a month (all categories)

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here, from any category, has a paid plan listed at $10 a month or less. Google Gemini comes first: low-cost entry tier and deep ties to Google storage, Search and creative tools. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Google Gemini Lower paid tier ($4.99/month). 8 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–18 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Google Gemini: Low-cost entry tier and deep ties to Google storage, Search and creative tools. Plan: Lower paid tier: $4.99/month
    2x Free usage, 200 Google Flow credits
  2. Cursor: A full AI-native editor with agents, cloud automations and code review, and a free tier to start. Plan: Plus: $8/month

    Personal pricing
  6. Macaly: Prompt-to-website and app builder for non-developers. Plan: Starter: $10/month
  7. Framer: Design-first website builder with AI page generation. Plan: Basic: $10/month (yearly)
    1,000 AI credits/month
  8. Midjourney: Consistently strong image aesthetics with simple GPU-time based plans and commercial use rights. Plan: Basic: $10/month
    3.3 Fast GPU hr/month

    1,000 credits/month
  10. Krea: Access to many image/video models plus an agent, with a daily free allowance. Plan: Basic: $9/month ($5 annual)
    5,000 units/month

Who should pick which

Pick Google Gemini for people already in Google's ecosystem (Gmail, Drive, Photos) (Lower paid tier: $4.99/month); think twice if you don't use Google services and want a standalone assistant. Pick Cursor for developers who want an AI-first editor with agents and code review (Plus: $8/month); think twice if you want flat pricing that includes third-party models. Pick GitHub Copilot for teams already on GitHub who want AI in their existing IDE (Pro: $10/month); think twice if you want an AI-native editor rather than a plugin. Pick Zed for developers who want a fast native editor and a choice of AI (Zed-hosted, own keys or external agents) (Pro: $10/month); think twice if you depend on the VS Code or JetBrains extension ecosystems. Pick JetBrains AI (Junie) for developers already living in JetBrains IDEs (AI Pro: $10/month ($100/year)); think twice if you don't use JetBrains IDEs. Pick Macaly for building and hosting websites by chat (Starter: $10/month). Watch out: credits cap usage. Pick Framer for designing and publishing websites visually (Basic: $10/month (yearly)). Watch out: free sites use a Framer domain.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Google Gemini Lower paid tier ($4.99/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://gemini.google/subscriptions/, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Google Gemini: $0/month with a Google Account (Flash model, base usage limits); Cursor: Free: limited agent requests and limited chat requests; GitHub Copilot: Limited chat and agent usage; no code review or cloud agent; Zed: Personal: free forever, unlimited with your own API keys or external agents; Macaly: Free: $0/month, publish on a free address; Framer: Free: 500 AI credits, Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth; Recraft: Limited free credits, personal use only; Krea: 100 units/day.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $10/month, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–18 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

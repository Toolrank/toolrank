Best AI hospitality tools with mobile apps

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI hospitality tools lists an iOS, Android or mobile app among its platforms. Mindtrip comes first: AI travel planner that builds itineraries on a map and helps you book. The cheapest verified paid plan here is HostBuddy AI Pro ($7/month per property, billed monthly). 3 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 17–24 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Mindtrip for planning a trip, keeping the itinerary and bookings in one place and getting price-drop alerts (price not yet verified). Watch out: no pricing or free-plan statement on the homepage. Pick Lodgify for hosts with a few properties who want their own booking website and synced calendars (Basic: From $14/month). Watch out: price rises with property count and plan; automated messaging starts on Professional. Pick iGMS for hosts who want AI-drafted guest replies and only pay for properties that get bookings (PRO: $38/month per property billed annually, or $48 billed monthly (1 property)). Watch out: AI Agent and dynamic pricing need PRO+. Pick Host Tools for hosts with a few listings who want hands-off guest messaging and reviews (Messaging: $28/month billed yearly ($336/year) or $30/month monthly). Watch out: no free plan; the price rises with every listing you add. Pick Smoobu for small European hosts who want a channel manager, booking site and apps in one (From €28 a month (1 unit, per vendor FAQ)). Watch out: billed in EUR; the cheapest Flex plan adds a 0.9% booking fee. Pick Tokeet for rental managers who want channel management, AI messaging and mobile apps (Paid: 10 rentals) ($134.90/month with monthly payments). Watch out: paid prices depend on rental count; only the 10-rental price was shown on the fetched page. Pick PriceLabs for hosts and managers who want automated nightly pricing they can run from an AI assistant (Dynamic Pricing: rest of world) ($9.99/month per listing, plus tax). Watch out: no free plan; the price is per listing, and higher in the US, UK, Europe and Australia.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

HostBuddy AI Pro ($7/month per property, billed monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://hostbuddy.ai/pricing, checked 24 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Tokeet: Free plan (stated on pricing page); 15-day free trial, no credit card; Beyond: Free plan with no booking fee; Turno: Free for 1 property, or for any number using only Turno Marketplace cleaners.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a mobile app listed on the vendor site, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 17–24 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

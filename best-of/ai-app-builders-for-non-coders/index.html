Best AI app builders for non-coders

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools build working apps from a prompt or a spreadsheet, without writing code. Lovable comes first: turns plain-language prompts into working web apps, with hosting via Lovable Cloud and a free daily allowance. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Macaly Starter ($10/month). 6 of the 6 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Lovable: Turns plain-language prompts into working web apps, with hosting via Lovable Cloud and a free daily allowance. Plan: Free: $0
  2. Bolt.new: Generous token-based free tier and simple Pro/Teams pricing for in-browser app building. Plan: Free: $0
  3. Base44: Prompt-to-app builder with built-in database, auth and hosting; owned by Wix. Plan: Free: $0
  4. Create.xyz (Anything): Prompt-to-app builder for web and mobile apps. Plan: Free: $0
  5. Macaly: Prompt-to-website and app builder for non-developers. Plan: Free: $0/month
  6. Glide: No-code business apps from spreadsheets, with AI features. Plan: Free: $0

Tools come from several ToolRank categories, so they are grouped by category and then by each category’s editorial rank. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Macaly Starter ($10/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.macaly.com/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Lovable: 5 build credits/day (up to 30/month) plus 20 Cloud credits/month; Bolt.new: 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month, Bolt branding, 10MB uploads, unlimited databases; Base44: Free to start; Create.xyz (Anything): Free plan; Macaly: Free: $0/month, publish on a free address; Glide: Free: limited credits.

Best AI marketing tools for enterprise buyers

Updated 20 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI marketing tools lists an Enterprise or custom-priced plan, the usual route to SSO, contracts and volume pricing. Jasper comes first: marketing-focused AI with brand controls and team governance. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Predis.ai Core ($19/month billed yearly ($32 monthly)). 3 of the 8 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–20 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Jasper: Marketing-focused AI with brand controls and team governance. Plan: Business: Custom
  2. Copy.ai: Combines chat with go-to-market workflows for teams. Plan: Enterprise: Contact sales
  3. HubSpot Breeze: HubSpot's AI agents and assistants built into its CRM and marketing hub. Plan: Enterprise: $3,600/month
    Includes 5 core seats (+$75/seat); required one-time $7,000 onboarding
  4. Clay: Data enrichment and AI research agents for building prospect lists. Plan: Enterprise: Custom pricing with annual commitment
    SSO, enterprise-grade security and support
  5. Apollo.io: B2B contact database and sales engagement with AI-written outreach. Plan: Custom: Contact sales
    SSO, API access, custom volumes
  6. Surfer: Combines a content editor with AI visibility tracking, with MCP and API on higher plans. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
  7. Predis.ai: Creates and publishes ads in one tool, with mobile apps and e-commerce integrations. Plan: Enterprise+: $212/month billed yearly ($249 monthly)
    Unlimited brands, 10,000 credits
  8. Writesonic: Pairs AI visibility tracking with articles and audits, with a free trial and no card. Plan: Enterprise: Custom
    SSO/SAML, SOC 2 Type II

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Marketing & Sales, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Jasper for marketing teams producing on-brand content at scale (Business: Custom); think twice if you're an individual on a budget. Pick Copy.ai for GTM teams automating content workflows (Enterprise: Contact sales); think twice if you just need occasional copywriting. Pick HubSpot Breeze for marketing teams already on, or moving to, the HubSpot CRM who want AI agents built in (Enterprise: $3,600/month). Watch out: AI agents run on HubSpot Credits, and Professional and Enterprise add mandatory onboarding fees. Pick Clay for enriching and automating prospect lists (Enterprise: Custom pricing with annual commitment). Watch out: paid plans start high. Pick Apollo.io for prospecting database and outbound email (Custom: Contact sales). Watch out: paid prices not verified. Pick Surfer for content teams who want to rank in Google and get cited in AI answers (Enterprise: Custom). Watch out: no free plan; API access is on the top Peace of Mind plan. Pick Predis.ai for small brands and agencies making social ads and posts without a designer (Enterprise+: $212/month billed yearly ($249 monthly)). Watch out: the 3-day trial needs a card and is charged when it ends.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Predis.ai Core ($19/month billed yearly ($32 monthly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://predis.ai/pricing/, checked 20 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

HubSpot Breeze: Free tools: $0/month for up to 2 users, no credit card; Clay: Free: 500 actions/month, 100 data credits/month; Apollo.io: Free plan with no time limit (light usage; Gmail only for sending).

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with an Enterprise or custom plan on the pricing page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–20 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

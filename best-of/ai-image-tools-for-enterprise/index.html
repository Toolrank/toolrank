Who should pick which

Pick Stability AI for developers and small studios running open image models on their own hardware (Enterprise: Contact sales). Watch out: businesses over $1M revenue need an Enterprise licence. Pick Canva AI for non-designers making social posts, presentations and marketing graphics with AI help (Enterprise: Contact sales). Watch out: AI use is capped by a shared allowance; heavier AI needs the AI Pass add-on at extra cost. Pick Magnific for creators who want AI upscaling plus a suite of image and video models and stock assets (Enterprise: Contact sales). Watch out: credits are annual pools, so heavy users can run out before renewal. Pick Photoroom for online sellers making product photos with background removal and AI backgrounds (Enterprise: Contact sales). Watch out: API and MCP usage uses a separate credit allowance.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Photoroom Pro ($7.50/month billed yearly or $12.99 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.photoroom.com/pricing, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Stability AI: Community licence: free for researchers, developers, small businesses and creators under $1M annual revenue; Canva AI: Free: US$0, up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses, 5GB storage.

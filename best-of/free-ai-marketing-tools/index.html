Best free AI marketing tools (with a real free plan)

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These AI marketing tools have a free plan or are free to use, and we confirmed it on each vendor's own pricing page. Typefully comes first: clean writing and scheduling for threads with a free plan and an API. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). 10 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 17–22 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Typefully: Clean writing and scheduling for threads with a free plan and an API. Plan: Free: $0
  2. Easel: Open-source social media agent: find trends, create content, publish everywhere. New open-source launch on GitHub. Plan: Open source (Apache-2.0), free to self-host
  3. HubSpot Breeze: HubSpot's AI agents and assistants built into its CRM and marketing hub. Plan: Free: $0/month
    Up to 2 users; free marketing, sales, service, content and data tools
  4. Clay: Data enrichment and AI research agents for building prospect lists. Plan: Free: $0
  5. Apollo.io: B2B contact database and sales engagement with AI-written outreach. Plan: Free: $0
    Gmail only
  6. Buffer: Free plan with AI Assistant and API access; paid plans from $5 per channel. Plan: Free: $0
    1 user, API access (3,000 requests/month)
  7. Metricool: A free plan for one brand, low per-brand pricing, plus MCP and API access. Plan: Free: $0/month
    1 brand, 20 posts/month, 30 days of analytics
  8. Ahrefs: A free plan for site owners and API and MCP access from the Standard plan. Plan: Ahrefs Free: Free
    Limited access for website owners
  9. Semrush: A free plan, a 7-day free trial and MCP access from the Starter plan. Plan: Free: $0/month
    1 demo project, 10 reports a day
  10. Publer: A free-forever plan, mobile app and MCP and API access on paid plans. Plan: Free: Free forever
    3 social accounts, 25 drafts

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Marketing & Sales, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Typefully for creators growing on X and LinkedIn (Free: $0); think twice if you need a full social media suite. Pick Easel for developers experimenting with an open-source social media content agent (free plan: Open source (Apache-2.0), free to self-host). Watch out: built around Chinese social platforms; you host and run it yourself. Pick HubSpot Breeze for marketing teams already on, or moving to, the HubSpot CRM who want AI agents built in (Free: $0/month). Watch out: AI agents run on HubSpot Credits, and Professional and Enterprise add mandatory onboarding fees. Pick Clay for enriching and automating prospect lists (Free: $0). Watch out: paid plans start high. Pick Apollo.io for prospecting database and outbound email (Free: $0). Watch out: paid prices not verified. Pick Buffer for creators and small teams scheduling posts on several social networks on a budget (Free: $0). Watch out: free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel and 1 user. Pick Metricool for freelancers and agencies scheduling and reporting on several brands' social accounts (Free: $0/month). Watch out: LinkedIn and X need a paid plan; the free plan caps posts at 20 a month.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://buffer.com/pricing, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Typefully: 1 social set, 1 user, 10 posts/month; Easel: Open source (Apache-2.0), free to self-host; HubSpot Breeze: Free tools: $0/month for up to 2 users, no credit card; Clay: Free: 500 actions/month, 100 data credits/month; Apollo.io: Free plan with no time limit (light usage; Gmail only for sending); Buffer: Free plan: $0 forever, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant; Metricool: Free: 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month, AI assistant, MCP; Ahrefs: Ahrefs Free: limited Site Explorer and Site Audit for your own site; Semrush: Free: $0/month, 10 reports a day, no credit card; 7-day free trial of paid plans; Publer: Free forever: 1 user, 3 social accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a free plan or free use (not just a trial), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 17–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

AI hospitality tools with a free trial (paid plans only)

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

None of these AI hospitality tools has a permanent free plan, but each offers a trial confirmed on its pricing page. Lodgify comes first: low entry price for one property, no booking fees, and an AI assistant for guest replies. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Guesty Lite (From $9/month per listing). None of the 8 has a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 20–22 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Lodgify for hosts with a few properties who want their own booking website and synced calendars (Basic: From $14/month). Watch out: price rises with property count and plan; automated messaging starts on Professional. Pick Guesty for hosts with 1–3 listings who may grow into a full property-management platform (Lite: From $9/month per listing). Watch out: the Pro and Enterprise plans are quoted by listing count. Pick iGMS for hosts who want AI-drafted guest replies and only pay for properties that get bookings (PRO: $38/month per property billed annually, or $48 billed monthly (1 property)). Watch out: AI Agent and dynamic pricing need PRO+. Pick Uplisting for operators who want to connect AI agents to their rental business through MCP or the API (£0/month + 3% per booking (pay as you go)). Watch out: prices are in GBP; the Independent Host plan is capped at 4 properties. Pick Host Tools for hosts with a few listings who want hands-off guest messaging and reviews (Messaging: $28/month billed yearly ($336/year) or $30/month monthly). Watch out: no free plan; the price rises with every listing you add. Pick OwnerRez for owners and managers who want an all-in-one PMS and to connect it to Claude or ChatGPT (price not yet verified). Watch out: rezzy AI, SMS, websites and QuickBooks are paid premium add-ons; no free plan. Pick Smoobu for small European hosts who want a channel manager, booking site and apps in one (From €28 a month (1 unit, per vendor FAQ)). Watch out: billed in EUR; the cheapest Flex plan adds a 0.9% booking fee.

Which of these is cheapest?

Guesty Lite (From $9/month per listing) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.guesty.com/pricing/, checked 20 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

None of these has a free plan we could confirm. None lists a permanent free plan on its pricing page. Some offer a trial; see the table.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a trial but no permanent free plan, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 20–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best free AI tools for finding research papers

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these paper-discovery tools can be used free, confirmed on the vendor's site. Elicit comes first: purpose-built for literature review, with systematic review workflows and Zotero import. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Research Rabbit ResearchRabbit+ ($10/month annual or $12.50 monthly). 5 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Elicit: Purpose-built for literature review, with systematic review workflows and Zotero import. Plan: Basic: Free
  2. Consensus: Cited answers grounded in peer-reviewed papers with a usable free tier. Plan: Free: $0
  3. Semantic Scholar: Free AI-powered academic paper search from Ai2. Plan: Free: $0
  4. Undermind: Deep academic literature search agent. Plan: Free: $0
  5. Research Rabbit: Literature discovery and citation mapping. Plan: Free: $0 forever

Who should pick which

Pick Elicit for researchers doing literature and systematic reviews (Basic: Free); think twice if you need general web search. Pick Consensus for students and clinicians checking what research says (Free: $0); think twice if you need non-academic sources. Pick Semantic Scholar for free scientific paper search (Free: $0). FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Research Rabbit ResearchRabbit+ ($10/month annual or $12.50 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.researchrabbit.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Elicit: Limited Research Agent and Reports usage, Zotero import; Consensus: Basic paper search, 10 Pro messages/month, up to 3 Deep reviews/month; Semantic Scholar: Free AI-powered research tool (Ai2); Undermind: Free: $0 for exploratory research; Research Rabbit: Free: $0 forever.

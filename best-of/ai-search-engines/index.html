Who should pick which

Pick Perplexity for fast researched answers with sources (Pro: $17/month billed annually); think twice if you need long creative writing rather than research. Pick Kagi for ad-free private search with an AI assistant (Starter: $5/month). Watch out: free use is a 100-search trial. Pick You.com for developers needing a web-search API ($5 per 1,000 calls (Web Search API)). Watch out: pricing page covers the API, not consumer plans. Pick Liner for source-cited AI search for students (Pro: $14.99/month annually or $17.99 monthly). Watch out: free plan shows ads.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Kagi Starter ($5/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://kagi.com/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Perplexity: Free sign-up; limits not specified on page; You.com: Free tier: up to 100 queries via MCP server, no key; Liner: Free: $0, answers with ads.

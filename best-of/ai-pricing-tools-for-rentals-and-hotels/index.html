Best AI dynamic pricing tools for rentals and hotels

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools set nightly or room rates automatically from market data. Duetto comes first: hotel revenue management and pricing software. The cheapest verified paid plan here is PriceLabs Dynamic Pricing (rest of world) ($9.99/month per listing, plus tax). 3 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 17–22 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Duetto: Hotel revenue management and pricing software. Plan: Not yet verified
  2. Hospitable: A genuinely free Essentials plan, plus AI replies, API and MCP access on paid plans. Plan: Not yet verified
  3. Wheelhouse: Every listing starts on a free tier, with a flat per-listing paid plan and an API. Plan: Pro Flat: $19.99/month per listing

Who should pick which

Pick Duetto for hotel revenue teams that want AI-driven rate recommendations and profit benchmarking (Custom (demo required)). Watch out: enterprise sales process with no published prices. Pick Hospitable for hosts on Airbnb and Vrbo who want AI-drafted guest replies and automation (free plan: Free plan (Essentials): unified inbox, calendar and scheduled guest messages). Watch out: paid plan prices were not shown on the fetched page (they depend on property count). Pick Wheelhouse for hosts and operators who want data-driven nightly prices without a long contract (Pro Flat: $19.99/month per listing). Watch out: paid pricing is per listing, so costs grow with your portfolio. Pick PriceLabs for hosts and managers who want automated nightly pricing they can run from an AI assistant (Dynamic Pricing: rest of world) ($9.99/month per listing, plus tax). Watch out: no free plan; the price is per listing, and higher in the US, UK, Europe and Australia. Pick Beyond for hosts and property managers who only want to pay for pricing when they get booked (1% of bookings (Growth)). Watch out: paid plans take a share of each booking; API access needs the Pro plan.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

PriceLabs Dynamic Pricing (rest of world) ($9.99/month per listing, plus tax) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://hello.pricelabs.co/pricing/, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Hospitable: Free plan (Essentials): unified inbox, calendar and scheduled guest messages; Wheelhouse: Free plan: link listings, market report and settings, no credit card; Beyond: Free plan with no booking fee.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with dynamic or revenue-managed pricing described on the vendor site, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 17–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

The short answer

These platforms publish open-source code you can self-host, with optional paid cloud plans. n8n comes first: developer-friendly automation with unlimited steps per execution and a self-host option. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Flowise Starter ($35/month). 4 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick n8n for technical teams building complex workflows and AI agents (€20/month (Starter, billed annually)); think twice if you want the simplest no-code setup. Pick CrewAI for developers building multi-agent workflows (Basic: Free). Watch out: paid self-serve tier not shown; Enterprise is custom. Pick Dify for teams building LLM apps with a visual builder (Sandbox: Free). Watch out: cloud plans are billed per workspace per year. Pick Flowise for visual LLM flow building (Free: $0/month). Watch out: free cloud plan is limited to 2 flows. Pick Langflow for building and deploying agents and MCP servers visually (free plan: Free and open source, plus a free cloud account). Watch out: cloud limits are not published on the homepage.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Flowise Starter ($35/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://flowiseai.com, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

CrewAI: Basic: free, 50 workflow executions/month; Dify: Sandbox: free, 200 message credits; open-source Community edition free to self-host; Flowise: Free: $0/month, 2 flows and assistants; Langflow: Free and open source, plus a free cloud account.

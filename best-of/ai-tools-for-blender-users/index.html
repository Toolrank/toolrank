Who should pick which

Pick Wonder Studio (Autodesk Flow Studio) for filmmakers and VFX artists turning live-action footage into editable CG scenes, with markerless mocap and camera tracking (free plan: Free tier for exploring AI-powered VFX (monthly credits)). Watch out: paid plan prices were not shown on the fetched page; work is credit-based per second of footage. Pick Tripo AI for game developers and 3D artists generating textured models and props quickly (Pro: $20/month billed annually ($240/year)). Watch out: free models are public and non-commercial; commercial use needs a paid plan. Pick Hyper3D Rodin for game developers and 3D artists who want production-ready assets and an API (Creator: $24/month billed yearly ($288/year); $30/month monthly). Watch out: API access needs the $96+/month Business plan. Pick Sloyd for indie game developers who want unlimited 3D generations with a commercial licence (Sloyd Plus: $12.50/month billed yearly ($150/year); $15/month monthly). Watch out: the free plan is personal-use only with 1 AI generation a day; API credits are billed separately.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Sloyd Sloyd Plus ($12.50/month billed yearly ($150/year); $15/month monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.sloyd.ai/pricing, checked 21 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Wonder Studio (Autodesk Flow Studio): Free tier for exploring AI-powered VFX (monthly credits); Tripo AI: Free: $0, 200 monthly credits (about 13 models), non-commercial use; Hyper3D Rodin: Free: $0, pay by result (direct credits $1.50 each), 10 private assets; Sloyd: Guest: $0, 1 AI 3D generation a day, personal-use licence.

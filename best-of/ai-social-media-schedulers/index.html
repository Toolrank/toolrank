Best AI social media schedulers

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools plan and schedule social posts, with AI help for writing them. Typefully comes first: clean writing and scheduling for threads with a free plan and an API. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). 4 of the 9 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 20–22 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Typefully: Clean writing and scheduling for threads with a free plan and an API. Plan: Not yet verified
  2. Buffer: Free plan with AI Assistant and API access; paid plans from $5 per channel. Plan: Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)
    Unlimited posts, advanced analytics
  3. Predis.ai: Creates and publishes ads in one tool, with mobile apps and e-commerce integrations. Plan: Core: $19/month billed yearly ($32 monthly)
    1 brand, 1,300 credits; unlimited team members
  4. Ocoya: Simple monthly plans for creators, teams and agencies. Plan: Starter: $29/month
    1 user, 5 social profiles, 300 credits
  5. Metricool: A free plan for one brand, low per-brand pricing, plus MCP and API access. Plan: Starter: From $20/month billed annually; from $25/month billed monthly
    Up to 10 brands, unlimited publishing, LinkedIn
  6. Hootsuite: A 14-day trial with no card, unlimited scheduling and AI post and image generation. Plan: Standard: $99 per user/month, billed annually
    Up to 10 social accounts, AI posts and images
  7. SocialBee: Unlimited AI content on all plans and a 14-day free trial. Plan: Bootstrap: $24.20/month billed annually ($290/year); $29/month billed monthly
    5 social profiles, 1 user
  8. Later: A 14-day free trial, iOS and Android apps, and 25% off yearly. Plan: Starter: $18.75/month billed yearly
    1 social set (8 profiles), 1 user, 5 AI credits a month
  9. Publer: A free-forever plan, mobile app and MCP and API access on paid plans. Plan: Not yet verified

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Marketing & Sales, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Typefully for creators growing on X and LinkedIn (free plan: 1 social set, 1 user, 10 posts/month); think twice if you need a full social media suite. Pick Buffer for creators and small teams scheduling posts on several social networks on a budget (Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). Watch out: free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel and 1 user. Pick Predis.ai for small brands and agencies making social ads and posts without a designer (Core: $19/month billed yearly ($32 monthly)). Watch out: the 3-day trial needs a card and is charged when it ends. Pick Ocoya for teams and agencies managing several brands' social posts (Starter: $29/month). Watch out: no free plan; the 7-day trial needs a card. Pick Metricool for freelancers and agencies scheduling and reporting on several brands' social accounts (Starter: From $20/month billed annually; from $25/month billed monthly). Watch out: LinkedIn and X need a paid plan; the free plan caps posts at 20 a month. Pick Hootsuite for marketing teams who manage many social accounts and messages in one place (Standard: $99 per user/month, billed annually). Watch out: no free plan; plans are sold per user and start at $99. Pick SocialBee for freelancers and small businesses posting to 5 to 25 social profiles (Bootstrap: $24.20/month billed annually ($290/year); $29/month billed monthly). Watch out: no free plan; extra users and workspaces cost $10 a month each.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://buffer.com/pricing, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Typefully: 1 social set, 1 user, 10 posts/month; Buffer: Free plan: $0 forever, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant; Metricool: Free: 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month, AI assistant, MCP; Publer: Free forever: 1 user, 3 social accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with post scheduling plus AI writing tools on the vendor site, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 20–22 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best AI tools with browser extensions

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these tools offers a Chrome or browser extension, so it works on the pages you already use. ChatGPT comes first: the broadest general assistant: a usable free plan, apps on every major platform and a ladder of paid plans for heavier use. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). 9 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 18–23 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick ChatGPT for everyday drafting, Q&A and file analysis across web and mobile (Go: $8/month); think twice if you need guaranteed access to the strongest models without paying. Pick Kombai for frontend developers and teams who want polished UI code from designs or prompts (Pro: $20/month). Watch out: credit-based usage; SSO and audit logs need Enterprise. Pick Jenni AI for students and researchers writing papers with citations (Plus: $12/month as displayed). Watch out: free plan caps autocompletes at 10 a day; team prices need a demo. Pick LanguageTool for multilingual writers and teams who want one checker across browser and desktop (free plan: Basic: free, 2,000 characters per text field, 3 AI rephrasings a day). Watch out: basic caps text at 2,000 characters per field and 3 AI rephrasings a day; prices not shown. Pick Buffer for creators and small teams scheduling posts on several social networks on a budget (Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). Watch out: free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel and 1 user. Pick Publer for solo creators and small teams who want cheap scheduling and AI-agent access (free plan: Free forever: 1 user, 3 social accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account). Watch out: paid prices depend on account count and were not shown in the fetched text. Pick Tactiq for teams who want meeting transcripts and AI notes without a bot joining the call (Pro: $8 per user/month, billed annually). Watch out: free plan caps transcripts at 10 a month; monthly-billing prices were not shown.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://buffer.com/pricing, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

ChatGPT: Free plan: unlimited* everyday text chats, limited messages with uploads (vendor footnotes apply); Kombai: Free: $0/month, 150 credits at sign-up plus 50 a day (max 300 a month); Jenni AI: Free: $0/month, 10 AI autocompletes a day, unlimited citations; LanguageTool: Basic: free, 2,000 characters per text field, 3 AI rephrasings a day; Buffer: Free plan: $0 forever, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant; Publer: Free forever: 1 user, 3 social accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account; Tactiq: Free forever, no credit card: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month; Scam Sniffer: Free browser extension; open-source scam database ($0, free for commercial use); Zerion: Free wallet on iOS, Android, extension and web; free Developer API plan ($0 forever, 2K requests/day).

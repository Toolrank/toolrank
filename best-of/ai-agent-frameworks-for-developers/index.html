Best AI agent frameworks for developers

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These are code-first frameworks or platforms for building AI agents, each with a free way to start. CrewAI comes first: multi-agent orchestration framework plus hosted platform. The cheapest verified paid plan here is LlamaIndex Starter ($50/month). 5 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. CrewAI: Multi-agent orchestration framework plus hosted platform. Plan: Basic: Free
    50 executions/month
  2. LlamaIndex: Framework and cloud for agents over your documents. Plan: Free: $0/month
  3. Mastra: Open-source TypeScript agent framework. Plan: Starter: $0/month
    100K observability events
  4. Langflow: Open-source visual builder for agents and RAG. Plan: Free and open source, plus a free cloud account
  5. Agno: Open-source Python framework and runtime for multi-agent systems. Plan: Free: $0/month

Who should pick which

Pick CrewAI for developers building multi-agent workflows (Basic: Free). Watch out: paid self-serve tier not shown; Enterprise is custom. Pick LlamaIndex for document parsing and retrieval for LLM apps (Free: $0/month). Watch out: cloud credits limit parsing volume. Pick Langflow for building and deploying agents and MCP servers visually (free plan: Free and open source, plus a free cloud account). Watch out: cloud limits are not published on the homepage. Pick Agno for python developers running agents on their own infrastructure (Free: $0/month). Watch out: control plane needs Pro.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

LlamaIndex Starter ($50/month) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.llamaindex.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

CrewAI: Basic: free, 50 workflow executions/month; LlamaIndex: Free: $0/month; Mastra: Starter: free for everyone; self-hosted open-source framework; Langflow: Free and open source, plus a free cloud account; Agno: Free: $0/month runtime, you host it.

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a developer framework with a free tier or open-source edition, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best AI voice and audio tools with desktop apps

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI voice and audio tools runs as a desktop app (Mac, Windows or Linux) rather than only in a browser. Descript comes first: text-based editing plus the Underlord AI co-editor makes audio/video editing fast. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Krisp Core ($8/user/month annually or $16 monthly). 4 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–18 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Descript for podcasters and video creators editing talking content (Hobbyist: $16/month annual or $24 monthly); think twice if you need frame-level pro VFX editing. Pick Wispr Flow for anyone who'd rather talk than type (Pro: $12/user/month annual ($15 monthly)); think twice if you only need occasional built-in OS dictation. Pick Krisp for bot-free meeting notes and noise cancellation (Core: $8/user/month annually or $16 monthly). Watch out: no permanent free plan listed, only a 7-day trial. Pick Superwhisper for dictation on the desktop (free plan: Free tier forever; Pro features free for the first 3,000 words). Watch out: pro price could not be read reliably from the page. Pick AIVA for composing royalty-free instrumental music (€11/month (Standard, billed annually, + VAT)). Watch out: free tracks cannot be monetized; prices in euros.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Krisp Core ($8/user/month annually or $16 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://krisp.ai/pricing/, checked 18 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Descript: Free: 60 media minutes/month, 720p export, 5GB storage; Wispr Flow: Free on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android (limited dictations); Superwhisper: Free tier forever; Pro features free for the first 3,000 words; AIVA: Free forever: 3 downloads/month, must credit AIVA, no monetization.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a desktop app listed by the vendor, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–18 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

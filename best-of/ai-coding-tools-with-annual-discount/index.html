AI coding tools that are cheaper billed yearly

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI coding tools shows both a yearly and a monthly price, so you can see what committing for a year saves. Claude Code comes first: anthropic's agentic coder runs in your terminal and repo, included with Claude paid plans. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Framer Basic ($10/month (yearly)). 5 of the 7 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–18 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Claude Code: Anthropic's agentic coder runs in your terminal and repo, included with Claude paid plans. Plan: Claude Pro: $17/month annual or $20 monthly
  2. Replit: Replit Agent covers the whole loop in a browser: prompt, build, database, deploy, share. Paid plans include model credit. Plan: Core: $20/month ($18 annual)
    $20 toward powerful models, Plan mode, unlimited workspaces
  3. Base44: Prompt-to-app builder with built-in database, auth and hosting; owned by Wix. Plan: Paid tier: $40/month billed annually
  4. Figma Make: Figma's prompt-to-app feature that turns designs and prompts into working prototypes. Plan: Organization: $55/seat/month billed annually (Full seat)
  5. Bubble: Established no-code platform with an AI app generator. Plan: Starter: $29/month billed annually
    Live website, custom domain
  6. Webflow: Visual website builder with AI site and page generation. Plan: Basic: $15/month billed yearly
  7. Framer: Design-first website builder with AI page generation. Plan: Basic: $10/month (yearly)
    1,000 AI credits/month

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Coding & App Building, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Claude Code for developers who prefer terminal-driven agents working across a repo (Claude Pro: $17/month annual or $20 monthly); think twice if you want a free tool or a graphical editor experience. Pick Replit for founders and builders who want an agent to build and host an app without local setup (Core: $20/month ($18 annual)); think twice if you want to work in your own local repo and editor. Pick Base44 for building full apps from a prompt (Paid tier: $40/month billed annually). Watch out: credits cap AI messages and integration actions. Pick Figma Make for designers turning prompts and Figma designs into working prototypes and apps (Organization: $55/seat/month billed annually (Full seat)). Watch out: AI credits are capped per seat; heavier use needs paid seats or extra credits. Pick Bubble for non-coders building full web and mobile apps with a visual editor and AI (Starter: $29/month billed annually). Watch out: workload-unit limits can raise costs as apps grow. Pick Webflow for designer-grade marketing sites (Basic: $15/month billed yearly). Watch out: site and workspace plans are billed separately. Pick Framer for designing and publishing websites visually (Basic: $10/month (yearly)). Watch out: free sites use a Framer domain.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Framer Basic ($10/month (yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.framer.com/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Base44: Free to start; Figma Make: Starter: free, 150 AI credits/day up to 500/month; Bubble: Free: $0/month, 500 AI credits/month, development version only; Webflow: Starter: free, webflow.io domain; Framer: Free: 500 AI credits, Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with both annual and monthly prices on the vendor page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–18 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best AI video tools with an API

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these AI video tools offers an API, so you can build it into your own product or workflow. Kling AI comes first: high-quality text/image-to-video from Kuaishou. The cheapest verified paid plan here is D-ID Lite ($4.70/month billed annually ($56)). 6 of the 10 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–18 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Kling AI: High-quality text/image-to-video from Kuaishou. Plan: Standard: $8.80/month after a $6.99 first month ($10 list)
    660 credits/month, watermark removal, commercial use
  2. HeyGen: Fastest path to presenter-style avatar videos, with a free tier to test. Plan: Creator: $29/month
    600 credits, videos up to 30 min
  3. Synthesia: AI avatar video platform for training and corporate videos in 140+ languages. Plan: Starter: $18/month yearly or $29 monthly
    1 editor, 3 guests, no watermark
  4. Pika: Text- and image-to-video generator with effects for short social clips. Plan: Starter: $10/month ($8 annual)
    900 credits/month, no commercial license
  5. Higgsfield: Video generation with cinematic camera-motion presets, hosting several models. Plan: Starter: $19/month billed annually
    270 credits/month
  6. PixVerse: Text- and image-to-video generator with templates for social video. Plan: Standard: $8/month billed yearly ($96) or $10 monthly
    1,200 credits every 30 days + 60 daily credits
  7. Vidu: Shengshu's text- and image-to-video model with reference-to-video. Plan: Standard: $8.25/month billed yearly ($99) or $10 monthly
    800 credits/month, commercial use
  8. OpusClip: Turns long videos into short vertical clips automatically. Plan: Starter: $15/month
    No watermark, YouTube auto-import
  9. Colossyan: AI avatar video creator focused on workplace learning. Plan: Professional: $59/month yearly
    Watermark removal, 5 SCORM exports/month, up to 3 editors ($30/extra member)
  10. D-ID: Talking-avatar video and real-time streaming avatars from a photo. Plan: Lite: $4.70/month billed annually ($56)
    10 min/month, D-ID watermark

Who should pick which

Pick Kling AI for creators who want high-quality text- and image-to-video with 1080p/4K output (Standard: $8.80/month after a $6.99 first month ($10 list)). Watch out: introductory first-month prices and countdown offers make the ongoing price easy to misread. Pick HeyGen for marketers and trainers producing avatar explainer videos (Creator: $29/month); think twice if you need cinematic generative video. Synthesia (Starter: $18/month yearly or $29 monthly): AI avatar video platform for training and corporate videos in 140+ languages. Pika (Starter: $10/month ($8 annual)): Text- and image-to-video generator with effects for short social clips. Pick Higgsfield for cinematic AI video with camera-motion presets and many hosted models (Starter: $19/month billed annually). Watch out: heavy promotional pricing changes often. Pick PixVerse for social creators making short videos with templates and many third-party models (Standard: $8/month billed yearly ($96) or $10 monthly). Watch out: credits limit output; some models are only discounted, not included. Pick Vidu for reference-to-video and consistent characters across shots (Standard: $8.25/month billed yearly ($99) or $10 monthly). Watch out: listed yearly prices include a first-time purchase discount.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

D-ID Lite ($4.70/month billed annually ($56)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.d-id.com/pricing/studio/, checked 17 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

HeyGen: 3 videos/month, up to 1 min each; Synthesia: Basic: 10 minutes of video/month, 500 credits, Synthesia watermark; Pika: Free: $0, no monthly credits (credit packs only), 2 parallel video generations; Vidu: Free: $0/month, Q4 model at 540P, 10 references/month; OpusClip: Free: watermarked clips, 3-day export window, 9:16 only; Colossyan: Starter: free, 20 video minutes/month with NEO avatars, watermark.

How were these tools chosen?

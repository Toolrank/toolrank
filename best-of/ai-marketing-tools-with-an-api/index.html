The short answer

Each of these AI marketing tools offers an API, so you can build it into your own product or workflow. Typefully comes first: clean writing and scheduling for threads with a free plan and an API. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). 3 of the 4 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 20 Oct 2026.

Who should pick which

Pick Typefully for creators growing on X and LinkedIn (free plan: 1 social set, 1 user, 10 posts/month); think twice if you need a full social media suite. Pick Apollo.io for prospecting database and outbound email (free plan: Free plan with no time limit (light usage; Gmail only for sending)). Watch out: paid prices not verified. Pick Buffer for creators and small teams scheduling posts on several social networks on a budget (Essentials: $5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)). Watch out: free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel and 1 user. Pick Surfer for content teams who want to rank in Google and get cited in AI answers (Discovery: $49/month). Watch out: no free plan; API access is on the top Peace of Mind plan.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Buffer Essentials ($5/month per channel ($60 billed yearly)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://buffer.com/pricing, checked 20 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Typefully: 1 social set, 1 user, 10 posts/month; Apollo.io: Free plan with no time limit (light usage; Gmail only for sending); Buffer: Free plan: $0 forever, 10 scheduled posts per channel, AI Assistant.

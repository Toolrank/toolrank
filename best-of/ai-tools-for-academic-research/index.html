Best AI tools for academic research

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

These tools search, summarise or check scientific papers and sources. NotebookLM comes first: answers grounded in your own sources, free for personal use, with Audio and Video Overviews. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Elicit Plus ($11/user/month billed $132/yr). 4 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. NotebookLM: Answers grounded in your own sources, free for personal use, with Audio and Video Overviews. Plan: In Google AI Pro: $19.99/month (Google AI Pro)
    4x limits, 300 sources
  2. Elicit: Purpose-built for literature review, with systematic review workflows and Zotero import. Plan: Plus: $11/user/month billed $132/yr
  3. Consensus: Cited answers grounded in peer-reviewed papers with a usable free tier. Plan: Pro: $12/month ($144/yr)
    Unlimited Pro messages, 15 Deep reviews, 500 API/MCP uses
  4. Scite: Shows how papers are cited: supporting or contrasting. Plan: Basic: $12/month billed yearly
  5. Liner: AI research assistant with cited sources. Plan: Pro: $14.99/month annually or $17.99 monthly

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Research & Productivity, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick NotebookLM for students and researchers working from their own documents (In Google AI Pro: $19.99/month (Google AI Pro)); think twice if you need open web search as the main source. Pick Elicit for researchers doing literature and systematic reviews (Plus: $11/user/month billed $132/yr); think twice if you need general web search. Pick Consensus for students and clinicians checking what research says (Pro: $12/month ($144/yr)); think twice if you need non-academic sources. Pick Scite for checking how papers have been cited (Basic: $12/month billed yearly). Watch out: no permanent free plan. Pick Liner for source-cited AI search for students (Pro: $14.99/month annually or $17.99 monthly). Watch out: free plan shows ads.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Elicit Plus ($11/user/month billed $132/yr) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://elicit.com/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

NotebookLM: Standard chat/generation limits, up to 50 sources per notebook; Elicit: Limited Research Agent and Reports usage, Zotero import; Consensus: Basic paper search, 10 Pro messages/month, up to 3 Deep reviews/month; Liner: Free: $0, answers with ads.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with paper search, citation analysis or source-grounded notes on the vendor page, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

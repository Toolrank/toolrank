Best AI tools for Zoom meetings

Updated 18 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Each of these tools lists a Zoom integration. Otter.ai comes first: meeting transcription and AI notes. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Krisp Core ($8/user/month annually or $16 monthly). 4 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–18 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Otter.ai: Meeting transcription and AI notes. Plan: Pro: $8.33/user/month annually or $16.99 monthly
  2. Krisp: Noise cancellation and meeting notes. Plan: Core: $8/user/month annually or $16 monthly
    Unlimited AI note-taker, bot-free
  3. Reclaim.ai: AI time-blocking for calendars. Plan: Not yet verified
  4. Fireflies.ai: Unlimited transcription even on the free plan, across Zoom, Meet and Teams. Plan: Pro: $10/seat/month annual ($18 monthly)
  5. Fathom: Free AI meeting notetaker for Zoom, Meet and Teams. Plan: Team: $15/user/month annually or $19 monthly
    2 user minimum

Tools come from several ToolRank categories, so they are grouped by category and then by each category’s editorial rank. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Otter.ai for meeting transcription and notes for individuals and small teams (Pro: $8.33/user/month annually or $16.99 monthly). Watch out: promotional discounts were shown on the pricing page; check the current price at checkout. Pick Krisp for bot-free meeting notes and noise cancellation (Core: $8/user/month annually or $16 monthly). Watch out: no permanent free plan listed, only a 7-day trial. Pick Reclaim.ai for calendar time-blocking for Google Calendar users (free plan: Lite: free forever). Watch out: paid prices not verified. Pick Fireflies.ai for teams that want searchable meeting notes (Pro: $10/seat/month annual ($18 monthly)); think twice if you rarely have meetings. Pick Fathom for free meeting recording and summaries (Team: $15/user/month annually or $19 monthly). Watch out: team plans require at least 2 users.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Krisp Core ($8/user/month annually or $16 monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://krisp.ai/pricing/, checked 18 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Otter.ai: Basic: free forever; Reclaim.ai: Lite: free forever; Fireflies.ai: Unlimited transcription, limited AI summaries, 400 mins storage/team, 20 AI credits; Fathom: Free forever: unlimited recordings and instant summaries.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with Zoom listed as an integration, confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–18 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

Best AI writing tools for $10 a month or less

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $10 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. Sudowrite comes first: fiction-specific models (Muse, Ballad) plus 30+ models with rollover credits. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Novelcrafter Scribe ($4/month billed monthly). 4 of the 7 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–23 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Sudowrite: Fiction-specific models (Muse, Ballad) plus 30+ models with rollover credits. Plan: Hobby & Student: $10/month
    225,000 credits a month
  2. QuillBot: Cheap Premium plan and a useful free paraphraser for students. Plan: Premium: $8.33/month billed annually
    Unlimited paraphrase modes
  3. Wordtune: Low-cost rewriting with a free daily allowance. Plan: Advanced: $4.89/month annual ($6.99 list)
    30 per day
  4. ProWritingAid: Grammar and style checker aimed at fiction writers. Plan: Premium: $10/month yearly ($120) or $30 monthly
    Unlimited line editing reports
  5. NovelAI: AI-assisted fiction writing and storytelling. Plan: Subscription: From $10/month
    Text and image generation; on-demand Anlas also sold
  6. Rytr: Low-cost AI writer for short-form copy. Plan: Unlimited: $7.50/month (yearly)
    Unlimited generations, 50 plagiarism checks/month
  7. Novelcrafter: Low monthly plans, a 21-day full trial with no card, and your choice of AI model. Plan: Scribe: $4/month billed monthly
    Unlimited books; no AI features

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Writing & Content, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Sudowrite for novelists and screenwriters (Hobby & Student: $10/month); think twice if you write business copy rather than fiction. Pick QuillBot for students and non-native writers rewording text (Premium: $8.33/month billed annually); think twice if you need original long-form writing. Pick Wordtune for polishing emails and short-form text (Advanced: $4.89/month annual ($6.99 list)); think twice if you need unlimited rewrites for free. Pick ProWritingAid for fiction and long-form writers who want manuscript-level editing reports (Premium: $10/month yearly ($120) or $30 monthly). Watch out: monthly billing costs roughly three times the yearly rate. Pick NovelAI for AI-assisted fiction writing and anime-style image generation (Subscription: From $10/month). Watch out: no permanent free plan; plan prices beyond the starting price need a login to view. Pick Rytr for cheap short-form copy drafting (Unlimited: $7.50/month (yearly)). Watch out: yearly prices shown; monthly prices are higher. Pick Novelcrafter for novelists who want to plan series and use their own AI model keys (Scribe: $4/month billed monthly). Watch out: no free plan; AI features need your own API key and the Hobbyist plan or higher.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Novelcrafter Scribe ($4/month billed monthly) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.novelcrafter.com/pricing, checked 22 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

QuillBot: Basic grammar fixes and limited paraphrasing; Wordtune: 10 rewrites/day, 3 per month for some features; ProWritingAid: Free plan with core editing tools; Rytr: Free forever, 10K characters/month, no card.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $10/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–23 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

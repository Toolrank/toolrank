Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $10 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. Wordtune comes first: low-cost rewriting with a free daily allowance. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Wordtune Advanced ($4.89/month annual ($6.99 list)). 4 of the 5 have a free plan we could confirm. Who should pick which

Pick Wordtune for polishing emails and short-form text (Advanced: $4.89/month annual ($6.99 list)); think twice if you need unlimited rewrites for free. Pick Rytr for cheap short-form copy drafting (Unlimited: $7.50/month (yearly)). Watch out: yearly prices shown; monthly prices are higher. Pick QuillBot for students and non-native writers rewording text (Premium: $8.33/month billed annually); think twice if you need original long-form writing. Pick Sudowrite for novelists and screenwriters (Hobby & Student: $10/month); think twice if you write business copy rather than fiction. Pick ProWritingAid for fiction and long-form writers who want manuscript-level editing reports (Premium: $10/month yearly ($120) or $30 monthly). Watch out: monthly billing costs roughly three times the yearly rate.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Wordtune Advanced ($4.89/month annual ($6.99 list)) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.wordtune.com/plans, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Wordtune: 10 rewrites/day, 3 per month for some features; Rytr: Free forever, 10K characters/month, no card; QuillBot: Basic grammar fixes and limited paraphrasing; ProWritingAid: Free plan with core editing tools.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $10/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

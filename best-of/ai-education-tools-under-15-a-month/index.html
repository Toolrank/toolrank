Best AI education tools for $15 a month or less

Updated 21 Oct 2026 · Prices from vendor pages only · How we rank →

The short answer

Every tool here has a paid plan listed at $15 a month or less on its vendor's pricing page. Khanmigo comes first: tutoring tied to Khan Academy content, free for teachers and cheap for learners. The cheapest verified paid plan here is Khanmigo Learners ($4/month or $44/year). 5 of the 6 have a free plan we could confirm. Every price below is from the vendor’s own page, checked 16–21 Oct 2026.

Ranked shortlist

  1. Khanmigo: Tutoring tied to Khan Academy content, free for teachers and cheap for learners. Plan: Learners: $4/month or $44/year
  2. MagicSchool: Free forever for individual teachers, with school-safe privacy features. Plan: Plus: $8.33/user/month annual ($12.99 monthly)
  3. Eduaide: A real free plan and a cheap yearly Pro plan aimed at individual teachers. Plan: Pro: $49.99/year ($4.17/mo billed yearly)
    Unlimited generations, document chat, images, YouTube transcripts
  4. Diffit: A free Basic plan for teachers plus an individual Premium plan with exports. Plan: Individual teacher: $14.99/month billed monthly or $149.99/year billed annually
    Save 16% yearly; exports and standards alignment
  5. Twee: Built for language teaching with CEFR levels, with published per-seat school prices. Plan: Schools (3–10 seats): $8.33/seat/month ($99.95 per seat for 1 year)
    11–20 seats $7.50, 21–50 seats $6.25 per seat/month
  6. Knowt: Unlimited free creation, with Ultra about half price billed yearly. Plan: Ultra - Annual: $12.99/month, billed upfront at $149.99/year
    3-day free trial; ad-free, AI summarizers, AI voice tutor

Order follows ToolRank’s editorial ranking in Education & Learning, filtered to tools that fit this page. Anything we couldn’t confirm on a vendor page says “Not yet verified”.

Who should pick which

Pick Khanmigo for students wanting guided tutoring; teachers wanting free planning help (Learners: $4/month or $44/year); think twice if you need subjects beyond Khan Academy's content library. Pick MagicSchool for teachers planning lessons and differentiating materials (Plus: $8.33/user/month annual ($12.99 monthly)); think twice if you're a self-learner rather than an educator. Pick Eduaide for teachers who want unlimited AI-made classroom materials for under $5 a month (Pro: $49.99/year ($4.17/mo billed yearly)). Watch out: the Pro plan is sold yearly only; the free plan is capped at 24 credits (about 8 resources) a month. Pick Diffit for teachers who need the same content at several reading levels (ELL, SPED, intervention) (Individual teacher: $14.99/month billed monthly or $149.99/year billed annually). Watch out: exports to Google, Microsoft and Canva need Premium or a school licence. Pick Twee for language teachers, especially English teachers, making exercises from videos, texts and word lists (Schools: 3–10 seats) ($8.33/seat/month ($99.95 per seat for 1 year)). Watch out: only a 5-run trial is free; individual Basic and Pro prices were not shown on the fetched page. Pick Knowt for students who want AI study notes, flashcards and AP, SAT and ACT prep (Ultra - Annual: $12.99/month, billed upfront at $149.99/year). Watch out: AI summarizers and the voice tutor need Ultra; app-store purchases cannot be refunded by Knowt.

FAQ

Which of these is cheapest?

Khanmigo Learners ($4/month or $44/year) is the lowest paid price we verified on this list (https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing, checked 16 Oct 2026). Check the vendor page for tax, billing period and usage limits.

Which of these have a free plan?

Khanmigo: Free for teachers; MagicSchool: Forever free for individual teachers; Eduaide: Free: 24 credits a month (up to 8 resources), no credit card; Diffit: Basic: free (PDF download, print, share; last 90 days of resources); Knowt: Basic: free, unlimited file creation, live collaboration and storage.

How were these tools chosen?

They are the tools in ToolRank's rankings with a verified paid plan at or below $15/month (USD), confirmed on the vendor's own website on 16–21 Oct 2026. Tools we could not verify are left out rather than guessed, and anything unconfirmed is marked Not yet verified.

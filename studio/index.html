Frequently asked questions

What do I own?

Everything: the domain, the code, the content and the accounts it runs on. They are set up in your name and handed over at launch.

How long does it take?

Most builds take 1-2 weeks from an agreed brief. The timeline is confirmed in your quote before work starts.

Do you do hosting?

We set up hosting on your own account, so you are never locked in. Any hosting or domain fees are billed to you directly by the provider. The optional care plan covers updates and fixes.

What happens after launch?

The site or app is yours to run. You can join the care plan (A$49/month, no lock-in) for updates and small fixes, or ask us to quote new work. The Web app MVP includes 30 days of bug fixes.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit to start and 50% on handover, by bank transfer or card invoice.

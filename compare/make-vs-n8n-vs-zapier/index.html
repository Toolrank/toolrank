Make vs n8n vs Zapier

Updated 16 Oct 2026 · Facts from vendor pages · How we rank →

Who should pick which

Pick Zapier if you want the easiest setup and the biggest app catalogue; the free plan gives 100 tasks a month. Pick Make if you like building visual, branching scenarios; its free plan gives 1,000 credits a month. Pick n8n if you're technical, want unlimited steps per execution or the option to self-host; cloud plans start at €20/month billed annually.

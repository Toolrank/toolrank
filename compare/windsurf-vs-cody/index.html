Windsurf vs Cody (Sourcegraph)

Updated 16 Oct 2026 · Facts from vendor pages · How we rank →

Who should pick which

Both have changed a lot. Windsurf is now Devin Desktop, and Cody is enterprise-only after Sourcegraph discontinued Free and Pro in 2025. For an individual or small team, Devin Desktop is the only one of the two you can sign up for: Free, or Pro at $20/month. Cody makes sense only inside an existing Sourcegraph Enterprise deployment (from $16K/year), where its value is code-search context across huge monorepos.

FAQ

Can individuals still use Cody?

No. Cody Free and Pro were discontinued in 2025. Cody remains part of Sourcegraph Enterprise.

Is Windsurf still sold?

Yes, as Devin Desktop (renamed 2 Jun 2026): Free, Pro $20/month, Max $200/month and Teams plans.

Which handles very large codebases better?

Sourcegraph's strength is code search across very large codebases. Devin Desktop offers 'Fast Context' and cloud agents. We haven't benchmarked either.

Cursor vs JetBrains AI Assistant

Updated 16 Oct 2026 · Facts from vendor pages · How we rank →

Who should pick which

Pick JetBrains AI if IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm or another JetBrains IDE is home. AI Pro is $10/month on personal pricing, and JetBrains Air now runs Junie alongside Claude Agent, Codex and Copilot, with your own keys if you like. Pick Cursor if you're on VS Code or don't mind switching, and want an editor built around AI from the ground up. The deciding factor is usually your IDE: JetBrains users rarely want to give up its refactoring and inspections.

FAQ

How much does JetBrains AI cost?

JetBrains' store lists AI Pro at $10/month ($100/year) and AI Ultimate at $30/month ($300/year) on personal pricing, as of 16 Oct 2026. We didn't verify business pricing or free-tier limits.

What is Junie?

Junie is JetBrains' coding agent, now part of JetBrains Air, which can also run Claude Agent, Codex, Copilot and other ACP agents.

Can I use Cursor with JetBrains IDEs?

Cursor is its own editor, not a JetBrains plugin. If you want AI inside JetBrains, use JetBrains AI or another plugin such as GitHub Copilot.
